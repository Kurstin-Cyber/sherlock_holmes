# Cast Roles

> **PREVIEW (Stage 1). Full character profiles are now drafted in `characters/` (Stage 2).**
> This table maps Doyle's characters to their camp roles only. First names, appearances,
> ages, family details, catchphrases, strengths and flaws will be set in Stage 2.

## Naming rules

- **Holmes, Watson and Baskerville** are fixed.
- Other characters keep Doyle's surnames as a nod where they fit (**Mortimer, Stapleton,
  Barrymore, Selden, Frankland**), each with a diverse first name, chosen in Stage 2.
- Every character is a **child**.

## Doyle role to camp role

| Doyle character | Camp character | Camp role | Core trait (preview) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sherlock Holmes | **Holmes** | Self-appointed camp detective | Super observant; sometimes misses feelings |
| Dr. John Watson | **Watson** | Holmes's cabin-mate and best friend; keeps the case journal | The heart of the team |
| Sir Henry Baskerville | **Henry Baskerville** | New camper from the camp's founding family | Excited, but a little homesick |
| Dr. James Mortimer | **Mortimer** | Camp storyteller; tells the legend and brings the first case | Loves a good story |
| Jack Stapleton | **Stapleton** | Nature Club president; **pilot villain** | Scares campers off part of the park to protect a rare butterfly for the Park Prize; learns to share |
| Beryl Stapleton | **Beryl Stapleton** | Nature Club vice-president | Torn between loyalty and honesty |
| Mr. and Mrs. Barrymore | **The Barrymore twins** | Kitchen-helper twins | Know every creak in the Hall |
| Selden (the convict) | **Selden** | Shy younger camper | Secretly caring for the dog |
| Mr. Frankland | **Frankland** | The telescope kid | Reports everything he "sees" |
| The Hound | **The Hound** | Big, friendly, misunderstood dog; secret camp mascot after the pilot | Shaggy, goofy, loves snacks |

## Notes for Stage 2

- **Diversity** across the cast in ethnicity and culture, gender, ability (for example a
  wheelchair, hearing aids or glasses), body type and family structure. No stereotypes.
  It shows through names, appearances, family details, food and traditions.
- **Every character** gets a real personality, strengths, flaws and a catchphrase that stay
  the same in every episode.
- **Doyle's darker roles are softened.** Selden is no escaped convict; he's a shy kid with a
  kind secret. Stapleton is no murderer; he's a competitive kid who learns to share.
- Beryl's and Selden's relationships to others (siblings? cousins? friends?) are open; see
  `open-questions.md`.
