# Holmes & Watson: The Hound of Camp Baskerville

A children's 3D animated cartoon series (ages 5-8), adapted from Arthur Conan Doyle's
*The Hound of the Baskervilles* (1902, public domain).

Every character is a kid. The setting is Camp Baskerville, a summer camp in an old stone
manor at the edge of a big wild park. The spooky Hound turns out to be a friendly,
misunderstood dog, and young detective Holmes, with best friend Watson, solves a new
mystery every episode using observation and logic.

## Series at a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | Ages 5-8 |
| **Format** | About 11-minute episodes, standalone mysteries, watchable in any order |
| **Look** | Stylized 3D animation with a storybook feel (not photoreal) |
| **Tone** | Warm, funny, adventurous, mildly spooky, never truly scary |
| **Pilot** | A full, kid-sized retelling of Doyle's novel that reveals the friendly "Hound" |
| **Series engine** | Every spooky thing at camp gets blamed on the Hound, and Holmes clears his name |

## How we work

1. **One stage at a time.** We don't start a stage until the producer names it.
2. **The bible is the source of truth.** Names, looks, personalities, catchphrases and roles
   stay the same in every episode. If something changes, the bible changes first.
3. **Ask before big creative changes.** Changing a character or a plot point needs the
   producer's okay. Small wording and gag choices don't.
4. **Flag it.** Anything that might be too scary or too complex for ages 5-8 gets a
   `⚠️ FLAG` note with a suggested fix.
5. **Open questions live in one place.** Anything undecided goes in
   `bible/open-questions.md` until it's answered, then it moves into the right bible file.
6. **Read-aloud test.** Lines should be simple and rhythmic, fun to say out loud.
