# EPISODE 12: "THE BOY WHO SAW THE HOUND"

*11 minutes · Script draft 1 · Stage 4*

**Spotlight:** Tavita · **Lesson:** Kindness (listen kindly, even to someone who is often wrong)
**Clues:** (1) a big wet nose-print smudge and a long grey hair on Tavita's telescope lens · (2) grey fur on the boot-room back door and wobbly prints on the mat at five o'clock · (3) a faint "woo" from Tor Hill at sunset
**Red herring:** "Hound Rock" on Tor Hill, a boulder with two heather ears that looks very like a hound
**Setting:** the dining room, Tor Hill, the boot room

*Script key, look reminders and flags work as in `ep01-the-hound-comes-home.md`.*

**Optional thread:** The dining-room chalkboard reads **"CAMP DAY: 5 DAYS TO GO."**

**A payoff to plan for:** Tavita's catchphrase is "I SEE IT! ...Oh. Never mind." In this episode, the
last time he says "I SEE IT!" he is right, and there is no "Never mind."

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00 to 1:00)

### INT. THE HALL, DINING ROOM. EVENING.

*A long wooden table set for supper. Lanterns glow. Campers file in with plates and cups. The
room smells of soup. It is noisy and warm.*

*ANJALI MORTIMER (cape, scarf, curls) climbs onto a bench, spoon raised like a baton.*

**ANJALI**
(booming, quick)
Remember! The Hound is big, he's shaggy, and he wants a snack!

**CAMPERS**
(together, clinking spoons)
...WANTS A SNACK!

*Under the table, a huge shaggy tail thumps once. A hand with a cheese crust drops down from the
bench where LINH BARRYMORE sits, and the crust vanishes with a happy slurp.*

*The doors burst open. TAVITA FRANKLAND (huge sun hat, telescope, binoculars, cheeks pink from
running) skids to a halt in the doorway.*

**TAVITA**
(not a whisper)
I SAW THE HOUND!

*Every head turns. A sigh ripples round the table. Spoons go down.*

**CAMPERS**
(together, kindly, wearily)
Oh, Tavita...

*Tavita's smile wobbles.*

**TAVITA**
(quickly)
On Tor Hill! At five o'clock! He was HUGE, and he sniffed my TELESCOPE!

*HENRY BASKERVILLE (plaid, star-stickered hat) reaches over and pats the bench beside him.*

**HENRY**
(kindly)
Sit down, Tavita. Have some soup.

**LINH**
(whispering, nodding at the floor)
The Hound's right here. Under the table.

**TAVITA**
(urgently)
Not THAT one! The other... the...

*He stops. He sees thirty faces looking at him with patient, tired smiles. His shoulders sink.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
(small)
Never mind.

*He sits. He doesn't touch his soup. He turns his telescope over in his hands, looking at the
dented brass and the smudged glass. Under the table, Moony's tail thumps again.*

**CUT TO: TITLE CARD.** *Golden magnifier swirls. A tiny wobbly harmonica "woo."*

## ACT ONE: THE CASE ARRIVES (1:00 to 4:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Fairy lights. The wall chart. IMANI HOLMES (cap with flaps tied up, golden-blonde loc buns
poking out, glasses, raincoat) sits on her bed with her feet crossed, eating toast. MATEO WATSON
sits at his desk, not writing. His journal is open, but his pencil hovers above the page.*

**WATSON**
(quietly)
Holmes? I think we hurt Tavita's feelings.

**HOLMES**
(mouth full of toast)
Did we?

**WATSON**
(patiently)
At supper. Everyone said "Oh, Tavita." He didn't finish his soup.

*Holmes stops chewing. She thinks about it.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
Tavita doesn't not finish soup.

**WATSON**
That's why I'm worried.

*A beat. Holmes puts down her toast.*

**HOLMES**
(sincerely)
I said "Oh, Tavita" too. I didn't ask what he actually saw.

**WATSON**
(gently)
We could ask him now.

*Holmes grabs her cap. Watson rolls to the door. DING (once, serious).*

### INT. THE HALL, DINING ROOM. NIGHT.

*The dining room is almost empty. Lanterns burn low. Tavita sits alone at the long table, his
chin on his arms, his telescope in front of him. He's not looking at it.*

*Holmes and Watson sit down opposite him. Holmes puts her hands flat on the table.*

**HOLMES**
(carefully)
Tavita. Would you tell me exactly what you saw?

*Tavita lifts his head slowly. His eyes are suspicious and hopeful at once.*

**TAVITA**
(a little voice)
You won't laugh?

**HOLMES**
(a beat)
No.

**WATSON**
(gently)
We promise.

*Tavita sits up slowly. He picks up his telescope.*

**TAVITA**
It was five o'clock. I was on Tor Hill. And I saw a shape. A big, shaggy, grey shape. It walked
right up to me. And it sniffed my telescope. And it breathed on it. I felt it!

*He holds the telescope out. Holmes takes it. She turns it over. She angles the end of it toward
the lantern.*

*On the lens, right in the middle, is an enormous, round, wet smear, the shape of a very large
dog's nose. Stuck to the brass rim, curled and shiny, is a single long grey hair.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Oh.

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the nose-print and the
long grey hair.*

**WATSON**
(writing)
A nose. And a hair.

*He sketches the smudge and the hair. A star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

**TAVITA**
(wiping his eyes with his sleeve)
I told everyone, but nobody looked at my telescope.

*Holmes puts the telescope down very carefully, the way she'd hold something fragile.*

**HOLMES**
(quietly)
It's a good clue. Thank you for showing me.

*Tavita sniffs hard. He gives a wobbly smile.*

**TAVITA**
(in a small voice)
Aua le popole.

**WATSON**
(gently)
What does that mean?

**TAVITA**
(rubbing his nose)
It means "Don't worry." My nana says it when I'm sad.

**HOLMES**
(slowly, trying)
Aua... le popole?

**TAVITA**
(a surprised, wet, enormous grin)
That's nearly right!

*Watson's bell goes DING-DING.*

**HOLMES**
(tapping her magnifier twice)
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles Tavita and the telescope.*

## ACT TWO: THE INVESTIGATION (4:00 to 8:00)

### EXT. TOR HILL. MORNING.

*Bright morning. Dew on the heather. The Boardwalk winds up to a flat grassy lookout with a view
of the whole park. The team has gathered: Holmes, Watson, Henry, Anjali, Tavita (hat tipped back,
telescope strapped on). A cheerful breeze.*

*Tavita points proudly to a spot on the grass.*

**TAVITA**
(grandly)
He stood right THERE.

*Holmes gets down on her hands and knees. She sweeps her magnifier across the grass.*

**HENRY**
(looking up at the skyline)
Holmes? What's that over there?

*A little way off, at the edge of the hill, sits a huge grey boulder. It has two pointed
tufts of heather on top like ears. A long, jagged crack makes a smiling mouth. A lump of moss
makes a nose. It looks exactly like an enormous, sleeping hound.*

**ANJALI**
(delighted, hand to her heart)
Hound Rock!

**TAVITA**
(slowly, looking at it)
...That's not what I saw.

*Everyone laughs, not meanly, but it's a laugh. Holmes grins, rising from the grass.*

**HOLMES**
(cheerfully)
Well! That explains it. You saw a rock!

*Tavita's face closes. He looks at his boots. He turns away and walks to the edge of the hill
with his back to them.*

*Watson rolls over quietly. He looks at the rock. He looks at Tavita's back. He looks at Holmes.*

**WATSON**
(softly)
Holmes.

**HOLMES**
(still cheerful)
What?

**WATSON**
(gently)
The rock doesn't have a wet nose.

*Holmes's grin fades. She looks at the rock. She looks at Tavita. She looks at the lens of the
telescope in her pocket.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
No. No, it doesn't.

*She strides over to the boulder, kneels, and peers at it with her magnifier. She taps it. It
goes tonk. She sniffs it. She sits back.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(loudly)
It's a rock.

**HENRY**
(whispering)
That's what Tavita said.

**HOLMES**
(a pause)
It is. He said, "That's not what I saw." And he was right.

*Tavita turns slowly. Holmes stands, walks over to him, and stops a few feet away.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(sincerely)
I'm sorry I laughed. You told me what you saw. It was a real dog. The rock is a different
thing.

*Tavita looks at her. He looks at the rock. He starts to smile.*

**TAVITA**
(a little bashfully)
It does look like a hound, though.

**HOLMES**
It does look like a hound.

**TAVITA**
(giggling)
It looks like a very sleepy one.

*Everyone laughs, and this time they laugh together. Tavita laughs loudest.*

**ANIMATOR:** The laughter before was at Tavita; this laughter is with him. Make the difference
clear in the performances.

### EXT. THE HALL, BOOT ROOM, BACK DOOR. LATE AFTERNOON.

*A little wooden back door with a big round flap cut into the bottom, painted with a crayon
sign: "MOONY'S DOOR." The flap is swinging gently. Caught on its latch is a tuft of long grey hair.
Below it, on the mat, are the wobbly grey prints of an enormous dog.*

*Holmes kneels by the mat. Tavita stands behind her, telescope clutched to his chest. Watson
and Zaid (yellow raincoat, Tut) wait by the wall.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Fur on the latch. Prints on the mat. Wobbly, grey, and fresh.

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the fur on the latch and
the wobbly prints on the mat.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
It's five past five. Zaid, where was Moony at five o'clock yesterday?

**ZAID**
(quietly)
In the boot room. In his basket.

*Holmes looks at the basket in the corner. It is empty. She looks at the mat.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
Was he?

*Zaid looks at the empty basket. He looks at the flap. He looks at the mat. His eyes go very
wide.*

**ZAID**
(whispering)
He's not here.

*Watson's pencil flies across the page: the door, the flap, the fur, the prints. A star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

**TAVITA**
(breathless)
Does that mean...

**HOLMES**
(smiling at him)
It means someone is sneaking out of the boot room at five o'clock every day.

*From far away, over the hills, drifts a long, wobbly sound.*

**SFX:** *"Wooooo-oo-oooo."*

*Everyone goes still. Tavita grabs his telescope.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces golden sound ripples
drifting down from the direction of Tor Hill.*

**TAVITA**
(a whisper)
That's him.

**HOLMES**
(softly)
That's him.

**WATSON**
(writing)
Writing that down!

*He draws the hills, the ripples and a tiny sun setting behind them. A star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

*Holmes turns to Tavita and puts a hand on his shoulder. She doesn't speak. She doesn't need to.*

## ACT THREE: THE REVEAL (8:00 to 9:30)

### EXT. TOR HILL. SUNSET.

*Golden hour. The sky is rose, apricot and violet. The whole team lies in the long grass
at the edge of the lookout, very still, watching: Holmes, Watson, Henry, Anjali, Zaid and
Tavita, with the telescope trained on the path. Fireflies. The glittery mist curls at their
ankles.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Three clues. One: the nose-print and the long grey hair on the telescope. Two: the fur on the
latch and the prints on the mat at five o'clock. Three: the "woo" at sunset from Tor Hill.

**ZAID**
(whispering)
He comes up every day?

**HOLMES**
(gently)
I think so.

*They wait. A long, quiet, golden minute.*

*A soft crunch of footsteps. A huge, shaggy shape appears at the bottom of the lookout path, big
ears, one up, one down, a silver collar glinting. MOONY pads up the hill, slowly, stately,
tail swinging.*

*He walks to the very edge of the lookout and stops. He sits down. He sighs. He looks at the
sunset, as if he'd been looking at it every day of his life.*

*Nobody breathes.*

*Tavita rises very slowly to his knees. He lifts his telescope, aims it at Moony, and in the
smallest, sweetest, proudest whisper any of them has ever heard, says:*

**TAVITA**
I see it.

*A beat. Then a murmur runs through the group. No one laughs. Henry reaches across and
squeezes Tavita's arm.*

**HENRY**
(whispering)
You were right.

**ANJALI**
(whispering, wiping her eyes)
You saw him.

**TAVITA**
(a little voice, beaming)
I saw him.

*Zaid creeps forward, still on his knees. Moony turns his head and sees him. His tail thumps.*

**ZAID**
(whispering, to Moony)
You come up here every day?

*Moony tilts his head. He looks at the sunset again. He opens his mouth and lets out a long,
soft, golden-edged "woo," addressed to the whole valley. Golden notes drift out and curl into
the sky.*

**ZAID (CONT'D)**
(softly)
You like the sunset.

**MOONY**
(a small, contented)
...woo.

**ANIMATOR:** This is the quiet emotional high point. Slow the camera push. No music for three
seconds, then a single harmonica note.

## ACT FOUR: THE LESSON (9:30)

### EXT. TOR HILL. SUNSET. CONTINUOUS.

*The group sits together in a loose circle in the grass, Moony at the edge, a golden sunset
behind them. Watson takes a gold star sticker from his satchel and holds it up.*

**WATSON**
(gently)
Tavita.

*He holds out the sticker. Tavita looks at it.*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
(simply)
Official Lookout.

*Tavita takes it with both hands. He presses it onto the side of his telescope, next to the
dent. It glints.*

**TAVITA**
(a whisper)
Official Lookout.

**HENRY**
(grinning)
Back home, we'd have a badge.

**ANJALI**
(sweeping her cape open, with a flourish)
Then we shall make one.

**HOLMES**
(clearing her throat, a bit shy)
Tavita. Next time you say you saw something...

*She stops. She chooses her words.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(quietly)
I'll look at it properly first.

**TAVITA**
(beaming)
And I'll say "I think I saw" first.

**HOLMES**
(smiling)
Then we'll both be doing better.

*Everyone laughs. Watson's bell goes DING-DING-DING.*

**WATSON**
(softly)
Aua le popole, Tavita.

*Tavita's whole face lights up.*

**TAVITA**
(delighted)
You said it right!

---

### EXT. TOR HILL. DUSK. (BUTTON)

*The sun is just disappearing. The group heads down the Boardwalk, lanterns bobbing. Tavita
walks at the back, telescope up, scanning the sky.*

*Far down the path, near the Hall, a very large, very round, very woolly shape ambles out of the
heather. DUCHESS.*

*Tavita stops dead. He raises the telescope. His eyes go wide.*

**TAVITA**
(loudly, delighted)
I SEE IT!

*Everyone stops. They turn round. They look where he is pointing.*

*A beat.*

**HENRY**
(politely, kindly)
Tavita... that's Duchess.

*Tavita lowers the telescope. He looks at Duchess. He looks at the telescope. He looks at his
friends.*

**TAVITA**
(a pause, then, grinning)
I know! I SEE HER!

*A beat. Everyone bursts out laughing. Duchess looks at them calmly and says:*

**DUCHESS**
Baa.

**TAVITA**
(delighted)
And I did NOT say "Never mind."

**THE END.**

## "HOW HOLMES FIGURED IT OUT" (9:30 to 11:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Fairy lights. The wall chart. Holmes and Watson on the floor with the journal. On Holmes's lap,
Tavita's telescope, with a gold star on its side. Tavita himself sits on the beanbag, wrapped in a
blanket, a mug of cocoa in his hands.*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Case journal, day eighty-one. Case closed. The Hound on Tor Hill was real, and Tavita was right.

**HOLMES**
Let's look at the clues.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame fills the screen.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**CLUE ONE: THE NOSE-PRINT AND THE HAIR**

*The smudge on the telescope lens replays, with the long grey hair on the rim.*

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A big, wet nose-print on the lens and a long grey hair on the brass. A rock doesn't have a nose.
Tavita told us the Hound sniffed his telescope, and the telescope said so too.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE TWO: THE FUR AND THE PRINTS**

*The latch, the fur and the wobbly prints replay.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
Grey fur on the latch and wobbly prints on the mat at five o'clock. The basket was empty.
Moony had slipped out of the back door.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE THREE: THE SUNSET "WOO"**

*The golden sound ripples drifting from Tor Hill replay.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A happy "woo" from Tor Hill at sunset. Moony goes up every evening to watch the sky go pink. He
doesn't hurt anybody. He just likes the view.

*A gold star, a heart, and a doodle of a big dog watching the sunset.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(turning to Tavita)
You were right the whole time.

**TAVITA**
(grinning)
I know.

**HOLMES**
(a beat, smiling)
It's much nicer to say that than to say I'm sorry.

**TAVITA**
(grinning wider)
I accept both.

*Everyone laughs. Watson writes, with a flourish: "LISTEN KINDLY. LOOK PROPERLY." He draws a heart.
DING-DING-DING: three dings for "solved."*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Case closed. Hound cleared.

*Holmes adds a line to the wall chart: "THINGS THAT GO BUMP: A DOG WHO LIKES SUNSETS."*

**HOLMES**
Curious... very curious!

## Complexity and logic check

- **Clue 1** is a physical object, a nose-print and a hair, and everyone knows what a wet dog nose looks like.
- **Clue 2** is the "where was Moony?" puzzle: an empty basket at five.
- **Clue 3** is a sound seen as ripples. The reveal (Moony watching the sunset) is quiet and visual.
- The emotional logic (listen to people before you laugh) is more important than the puzzle.

## Flag summary

| Flag | Where | Fix built in |
|---|---|---|
| Tavita being dismissed | Cold open, Act One | Brief, warm, and quickly noticed by Watson; the team repairs it within the episode |
| Laughter at Tavita | Act Two | The first laugh is shown as hurtful; Watson points it out; the second laugh is shared |
| Hound Rock | Act Two | A funny, sleepy boulder in bright daylight; it is cleared in the same scene |
| Moony alone | Act Three | Safe, in the park, on a path he knows; he sits and watches the sunset |
| Dusk on Tor Hill | Act Three | Golden hour; lanterns and fireflies; the whole group together |

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **"Aua le popole"** is a Samoan phrase meaning "don't worry." I'm not certain of the exact
   spelling or usage. It needs a Samoan consultant before animation. Keep it as a recurring
   Tavita phrase?
2. **Holmes apologises once** (at Hound Rock). In the dining-room scene she thanks Tavita for
   the clue instead of apologising, so the apology lands once.
3. **The payoff**: Tavita finally says "I SEE IT!" and is right, with no "Never mind." The very
   last button then plays with that (Duchess). Does the button undercut the payoff?
4. **Moony sneaks out daily** to watch the sunset on Tor Hill. This is a new fact about him; it
   adds charm and a reason for the legend ("the hound would climb to the top of Tor Hill and
   howl across the park"). Add to his profile?
5. **"MOONY'S DOOR"**: a big dog flap in the boot-room back door. A new world detail. OK to
   add to the bible?
