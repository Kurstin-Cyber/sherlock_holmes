# EPISODE 2: "THE CASE OF THE MISSING SANDWICHES"

*11 minutes · Script draft 1 · Stage 4*

**Spotlight:** Linh and Minh · **Lesson:** Honesty (say sorry out loud, not in secret)
**Clues:** (1) crumbs of two different breads · (2) half a "sorr—" note in yellow crayon · (3) the sandwiches vanish from both trays at the same moment
**Red herring:** Giant "paw prints" in the flour (Tavita's sun hat)
**Setting:** the Hall's kitchen, dining room and Kitchen Loft

*Script key, look reminders and flags work as in `ep01-the-hound-comes-home.md`.*

**Optional thread:** The dining-room chalkboard reads **"CAMP DAY: 55 DAYS TO GO."** (Banner
number = (13 minus the episode number) × 5. Background only.)

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00 to 1:00)

### INT. THE HALL, DINING ROOM. LUNCHTIME.

*Sunbeams through tall windows. A long wooden table with two big trays, one at each end.
On each tray: rows of neat sandwiches. Campers file in with plates. Bright, noisy, happy.*

*At one table, ANJALI MORTIMER (cape, scarf, big hair) is telling a story to three small
campers, using a sandwich as a puppet.*

**ANJALI**
(booming, quick)
Remember! The Hound is big, he's shaggy, and he wants a snack!

*The small campers giggle. The sandwich-puppet bows.*

**ANIMATOR:** This is the series' one-line reminder for new viewers. Keep it quick, funny,
and over by 0:15.

*LINH BARRYMORE (10, yellow headband, yellow apron) and MINH BARRYMORE (10, orange hearing
aids, green apron) are serving. They stand at opposite ends of the room. They do not look at
each other.*

*Linh lifts the lid off her tray. She counts. Her face drops.*

**LINH**
(a gasp)
There were twelve!

*At the other end, Minh lifts his lid. He counts. His face drops too.*

**MINH**
(quiet)
There were twelve.

*Both trays have eleven sandwiches. A single crumb sits where the twelfth used to be.*

*Cut to TAVITA FRANKLAND (huge sun hat, telescope) pressed to the window, telescope pointed
at the garden.*

**TAVITA**
(not a whisper)
It's the Hound!

*A beat. The whole room turns. Tavita lowers the telescope and checks the end of it.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
...That's a crumb. On the lens.

*He wipes the lens. The room groans, laughing.*

**CUT TO: TITLE CARD.** *Golden magnifier swirls. "HOLMES & WATSON: THE HOUND OF CAMP
BASKERVILLE." A tiny wobbly harmonica "woo."*

## ACT ONE: THE CASE ARRIVES (1:00 to 4:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. DAY.

*Fairy lights, maps, a shelf of jars. IMANI HOLMES (cap with flaps tied up, golden-blonde loc buns, gold glasses,
mustard raincoat) is on all fours with a magnifier. MATEO WATSON (teal hoodie, orange
wheelchair with stickers) is at the desk, drawing.*

*Knock, knock. Then a pause. Then another knock, from a different direction.*

**HOLMES**
(not looking up)
Come in! Both of you.

*The door opens. Linh comes in on one side. Minh comes in on the other. They are in the
same doorway but have somehow arranged to stand as far apart as possible.*

**LINH**
We need a detective.

**MINH**
(at the same moment)
We need a detective.

*A tiny, furious pause.*

**LINH**
(to the ceiling)
Someone is stealing sandwiches.

**MINH**
(to the floor)
Someone is stealing sandwiches.

**WATSON**
(gently)
Um. Is something wrong? You two aren't... talking.

**LINH & MINH**
(together, flat)
We're fine.

**HOLMES**
(not hearing it)
Excellent! A case. How many sandwiches?

**LINH**
Eleven, not twelve.

**MINH**
Eleven, not twelve.

**HOLMES**
Both trays?

**LINH & MINH**
Both.

**HOLMES**
Curious... very curious!

*She taps her magnifier twice. Watson looks from one twin to the other, writing nothing. He
looks worried.*

**LINH**
(low, fast)
Please don't tell Grandpa. He'd think we weren't looking after the kitchen.

**MINH**
(to Watson, in sign, subtitled)
Please help.

**SUBTITLE:** *Please help.*

**WATSON**
(signing back, slow, with a smile)
Yes.

**SUBTITLE:** *Yes.*

**ANIMATOR:** Watson knows only a few signs and signs them carefully. Minh's face lights up.
The sign for "yes" is shown clearly, in close-up, so children can learn it. No joke at
anyone's expense.

### INT. THE HALL, KITCHEN. DAY.

*A big, warm kitchen with copper pots and garlic braids. From a door offscreen, GRANDPA
BARRYMORE and BÀ NGOẠI clatter and hum a duet (voices only). A long counter with two trays
on it: Linh's and Minh's.*

*Holmes climbs onto a stool. Watson rolls alongside. The twins stand at the far ends.*

**HOLMES**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame circles the counter.*

*Holmes moves her magnifier slowly along the empty spot on Linh's tray. Then on Minh's. Then
back again.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Crumbs.

*She points at the crumbs on Linh's tray: light, flaky, crispy, from a baguette.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Bánh mì crumbs. Crispy outside, soft inside.

*She points at the crumbs on Minh's tray: dense, crumbly, with tiny seeds.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
And these are soda bread. Different crumbs. Different bread.

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. A glowing outline traces each pile of crumbs.*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Two breads...

*He doodles a baguette and a round loaf with two big stars.*

**[JOURNAL]** *Close on the doodle: "TWO BREADS?"*

**HOLMES**
(delighted)
A thief who likes two kinds of bread!

**MINH**
(low)
Or two thieves.

**LINH**
(low)
Don't be silly.

*Their eyes meet for one second. Both look away.*

**HOLMES**
(already moving)
Nobody saw anything?

**LINH**
We were doing chores.

**MINH**
(at the same time)
We were doing chores.

*They glare at each other. Holmes doesn't notice. Watson does.*

## ACT TWO: THE INVESTIGATION (4:00 to 8:00)

### INT. THE HALL, KITCHEN. LATER.

*Holmes is on her knees, studying the floor. Watson sits at the counter, watching the twins,
who have gone back to their tasks at opposite ends of the room.*

*A faint SCRAPE from the pantry. Everyone freezes. Behind the pantry door, a pair of
wiggling boots.*

*Tavita bursts out, covered in flour, hat tipped back, holding his telescope like a sword.*

**TAVITA**
(triumphant)
I FOUND PAW PRINTS!

*On the floury counter: three huge, round, floury marks, evenly spaced in a row.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
They're enormous! They're round! It's the Hound!

**ANJALI**
(appearing in the doorway, enthralled)
He walks on the counter!

**HOLMES**
(kneeling, examining the marks)
Hmm.

*She puts her magnifier close. The "paw prints" are perfectly round rings with a tiny
woven pattern. She looks at Tavita's hat. She looks at the rings. She holds the brim of his
hat against the flour.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
...Woven straw.

*She presses the hat to the counter. It leaves an identical ring.*

**TAVITA**
(slowly)
Oh. Never mind.

*Everyone laughs. Even Linh and Minh, at the same time. They stop. They look away again.*

**WATSON**
(writing, softly)
The Hound's innocent. Again.

**ANIMATOR:** Moony is nowhere in this scene. The twins' kitchen is a Moony-free zone for
now. He is asleep in the boot room (a quick insert shot later).

### INT. THE HALL, DINING ROOM. DAY.

*Holmes picks up a scrap of paper from beneath Linh's tray. It's a torn corner, with yellow
crayon on it, in big loopy letters:*

**NOTE (close-up):** *"I'm sorr—"*

*Under it, a small, wobbly star.*

**HOLMES**
(holding it up)
Half a note.

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. A glowing outline traces the letters and the star.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
"I'm sorr" ...sorrel? Sorrow? Sorrowful?

**WATSON**
(gently)
Sorry. I think it says "sorry."

**HOLMES**
Who writes "sorry" on a stolen sandwich?

*Watson opens his mouth. Holmes is already moving.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
It's yellow crayon. Who uses yellow? ...Tavita?

**TAVITA**
(from across the room)
I use blue!

*Watson, who had been about to speak, closes his mouth. DING (once, quiet).*

**HOLMES**
(over her shoulder)
Watson, you were saying?

**WATSON**
(small)
...Never mind.

*Holmes stops. She replays it. Her face falls.*

**HOLMES**
Oh. I interrupted you again, didn't I.

**WATSON**
(smiling)
A bit.

**HOLMES**
(sincerely)
I'm sorry. Say it again. Out loud. I'll listen.

**WATSON**
(careful)
I was going to say: the writing is on Linh's side. But the crayon... Linh's apron is yellow.
And Minh's is green.

*Holmes looks at the note. She looks at Linh's yellow apron across the room.*

**HOLMES**
Yellow crayon. Yellow apron. Hmm.

*Linh, across the room, goes pink.*

**[JOURNAL]** *Watson's doodle of the note, with a yellow crayon and a question mark. A star.*

**ANIMATOR:** The "sorry" interrupts-and-apology beat is the episode's mirror of the twins'
story: Holmes learns to say sorry out loud while the twins do it in secret. Keep it small,
warm and quick, no moral spelled out.

### INT. THE HALL, KITCHEN. 3:10 P.M.

*A big round clock on the wall. 3:10. Both trays are full again: twelve sandwiches on each.
Holmes, Watson, Anjali and Tavita hide behind the big copper pots. The twins are sent away
to "do chores" at different ends of the Hall. Both look at the clock as they go.*

*Tick. Tick. Tick.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Watch the trays.

**ANJALI**
(whispering)
If the Hound comes, I'll—

**TAVITA**
(whispering, loud)
I'LL SEE HIM FIRST!

**EVERYONE**
(whispering)
Shh!

*The clock reads 3:14. Then 3:15.*

*DING! A bright bell from outside: the chore bell.*

*Flick, flick! Two hands reach, one from the window on the left, one from the hatch on the
right. At exactly the same moment, a sandwich vanishes from each tray.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline pulses around both empty
spaces and the clock.*

*Holmes leaps up.*

**HOLMES**
Both trays. At the same time. At 3:15!

**WATSON**
(writing)
One thief can't be in two places...

**HOLMES**
(quickly)
...so there are TWO thieves!

**[JOURNAL]** *Watson's doodle: a clock at 3:15 and two arrows, one left, one right. A star.*

**TAVITA**
(whispering)
Two Hounds?

**ANJALI**
(whispering)
Two Hounds?!

**HOLMES**
Two people. Two breads. Two hands. And a note that says sorry.

*Holmes looks at Watson. Watson looks back, gentle and sure.*

**WATSON**
(quietly)
They're not fighting, Holmes. They're sad.

*Holmes thinks. She looks at the twins' quiet, unhappy faces through the window, at opposite
ends of the lawn.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
Oh. Oh!

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles Holmes. Then Watson. Then the two empty
trays.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

*She grins.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Follow the crumbs.

## ACT THREE: THE REVEAL (8:00 to 9:30)

### INT. THE HALL, KITCHEN LOFT. LATE AFTERNOON.

*A tiny, cosy room above the kitchen: two narrow beds, a round window with a ledge, string
lights, a stack of comic books. A little bunting reads "BARRYMORES." Golden light slants in.*

*A trail of crumbs leads from the stairs: a crispy flake, then a seedy crumb. Another flake.
Another crumb. Holmes, Watson and the others follow in a quiet line.*

*The crumbs end at a round window with a ledge. On the ledge, two small picnic blankets.*

*LINH tiptoes in through the left door with a bundle of sandwiches wrapped in a yellow
cloth.*

*MINH tiptoes in through the right door with a bundle wrapped in a green cloth.*

*They see each other. They freeze.*

**LINH**
(gasping)
You—

**MINH**
(gasping)
You—

*They look at each other's bundles.*

*Linh slowly unwraps hers. Inside: soda bread sandwiches with cheese and pickle. Minh's
favourite.*

*Minh slowly unwraps his. Inside: bánh mì with pork and pickled carrot. Linh's favourite.*

**LINH**
(a little voice)
That's my favourite.

**MINH**
(a little voice)
That's my favourite.

*Beat. Linh laughs. Then Minh laughs. Then they're both laughing and hiccuping.*

**LINH**
I told everyone your joke before you did.

**MINH**
You ruined my punchline.

**LINH**
I know! I couldn't help it! I'm so bad at secrets!

**MINH**
(signing, spoken aloud for the audience; subtitle: "I was stubborn. I'm sorry I sulked.")
I was stubborn. I'm sorry I sulked.

**SUBTITLE:** *I was stubborn. I'm sorry I sulked.*

**LINH**
(signing back, wiping her eyes)
I'm sorry, too.

**SUBTITLE:** *I'm sorry, too.*

**ANIMATOR:** Both twins sign AND speak. The camera holds on their hands in close-up for the
two "sorry" signs. Warm light. No music sting. Just two kids.

*Holmes steps forward gently. She holds up the torn note.*

**HOLMES**
You wrote this one.

**LINH**
(yellow crayon in hand)
I wanted to put it on his sandwich. And then I got scared and ripped it up.

**MINH**
(from his pocket, a scrap of green pencil note)
I wrote one too. I didn't send it.

*They hold up two half-notes. They fit together:*

**NOTE (close-up):** *"I'm sorry I sulked. I'm sorry I told. Let's be friends again."*

*Everybody sighs happily.*

**WATSON**
(softly)
You could have just said it.

**LINH & MINH**
(together)
It's hard to say it out loud.

**HOLMES**
It's harder not to.

*She looks at Watson. He smiles. DING-DING.*

**TAVITA**
(sniffling)
That's the best case ever.

*He wipes his eye with the telescope. It pokes him in the eye.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
Ow. Never mind.

*Everyone laughs.*

## ACT FOUR: THE LESSON (9:30)

### INT. THE HALL, KITCHEN LOFT. CONTINUOUS.

*Linh lifts a sandwich. Minh lifts a sandwich. They swap. They both take a bite at the same
time and make the same happy face.*

**LINH**
Third stair?

**MINH**
(grinning)
...always squeaks.

*Linh and Minh look at the audience-side window. In the garden below, Moony's enormous
shaggy shape is seen from behind, sniffing in the dirt, tail wagging. A tiny crumb floats
down from the window. Moony catches it in mid-air.*

**ANIMATOR:** A one-second "wink" from Moony, the series' optional thread. He is seen
**after** the crime is solved, so he cannot be a suspect.

**LINH**
(whispering, to Minh)
Don't tell anyone he's out there.

**MINH**
(quietly)
I won't.

**LINH**
(a beat)
...I mean it this time.

**MINH**
(deadpan)
She can't keep a secret.

*Linh throws a pillow. Minh ducks. Laughter.*

## "HOW HOLMES FIGURED IT OUT" (9:30 to 11:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Fairy lights. Holmes, Watson and the journal on the floor. A plate with two half sandwiches
on the rug. Warm.*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Case journal, day thirty-one. Case closed. Two sandwiches. Two thieves. One very sorry
Barrymore each.

**HOLMES**
Let's look at the clues.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame fills the screen.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**CLUE ONE: TWO BREADS**

*The crumbs replay: crispy flakes on Linh's tray, seedy crumbs on Minh's, each glowing.*

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
Bánh mì crumbs on one tray. Soda bread crumbs on the other. Two kinds of bread. A thief
who likes both? Or...

**WATSON**
(V.O.)
Two thieves.

*A gold star on the doodle.*

**CLUE TWO: THE HALF NOTE**

*The torn note replays, with its yellow crayon, its star and its "I'm sorr—."*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A half a note that says "sorry." In yellow crayon. Yellow like Linh's apron. Someone was
sorry.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE THREE: BOTH TRAYS AT 3:15**

*The clock replays. The two hands. The two empty trays.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
Both trays emptied at exactly 3:15. One thief can't be in two places. So there were two
thieves, and a very good reason.

*A gold star. A heart.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(turning to Watson)
And one more thing I got wrong.

**WATSON**
What?

**HOLMES**
You knew they were sad before I did. And I interrupted you.

**WATSON**
(smiling)
You said sorry out loud.

**HOLMES**
(grinning)
It's easier than hiding it in a sandwich.

*Watson laughs. DING-DING-DING: three dings, for "solved."*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Case closed. Hound cleared.

*Holmes picks up a half sandwich and takes a bite.*

**HOLMES**
Curious... very curious.

**THE END.**

## Lesson placement

The lesson (honesty: say it out loud) is **shown** twice: the twins' secret notes, and Holmes
apologising to Watson. It is **said** once, by Holmes: "It's harder not to."

## Flag summary

| Flag | Where | Fix built in |
|---|---|---|
| "Thief" language | Throughout | Played for comedy; the thieves are two kids being kind. Nobody is in trouble. |
| Holmes interrupts Watson | Act Two | A gentle flaw gag with a sincere apology; Watson is never made small |
| Sign language | Acts One and Three | Subtitled, held in close-up, treated as ordinary conversation |
| Siblings in conflict | Acts One and Three | Light; resolved with humour and affection |
| Moony | Cold open (absent), final gag | Appears only after the case is solved |

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Sign language consultant** still needed before animation. The signs I've specified are
   "please help," "yes," "sorry" and "I'm sorry I sulked."
2. **"Day thirty-one"** in Watson's journal: I've used day numbers that suit each episode's
   place in the suggested order, but they don't matter to the story.
3. **Grandpa Barrymore and Bà Ngoại** appear only as voices. They're never named in this
   script beyond the Kitchen Loft bunting.
4. **Tavita's "Never mind"** appears twice in this episode (the hat rings and the
   telescope poke). Is twice too many? The cold open's crumb gag has no "Never mind", so
   I allowed a second one. Cut the second if you want strictly once.
