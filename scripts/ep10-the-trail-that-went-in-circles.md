# EPISODE 10: "THE TRAIL THAT WENT IN CIRCLES"

*11 minutes · Script draft 1 · Stage 4*

**Spotlight:** Jasper and Beryl · **Lesson:** Honesty (say "I made a mistake," and listen when a friend tells you the truth)
**Clues:** (1) the signpost arrow turns by itself (a yellow ribbon proves it) · (2) little heart-shaped hoofprints around the post · (3) a tuft of white wool snagged on the sign
**Red herring:** Moony's big paw prints on a side path (he is just on a morning walk with Zaid)
**Setting:** the Boardwalk Trail, the main fork and the daisy post

*Script key, look reminders and flags work as in `ep01-the-hound-comes-home.md`.*

**Optional thread:** The dining-room chalkboard reads **"CAMP DAY: 15 DAYS TO GO."**

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00 to 1:00)

### EXT. BOARDWALK TRAIL. MORNING.

*Bright, fresh morning. Dew on the heather. Birds. A line of campers marches along the Boardwalk
behind JASPER STAPLETON (sweater vest, Nature Club badge, clipboard, a laminated map held up
like a flag). Beside him walks BERYL STAPLETON (lilac cardigan, long plait, sketchbook).*

*At the back, ANJALI MORTIMER (cape, lantern in daylight) walks backwards, waving a banner that
says "NATURE WALK!" in Jasper's neat letters.*

**ANJALI**
(booming, quick)
Remember! The Hound is big, he's shaggy, and he wants a snack!

**CAMPERS**
(together, a little out of breath)
...WANTS A SNACK!

**JASPER**
(not looking round)
Please keep to the Boardwalk. Rules are rules.

*HENRY BASKERVILLE (plaid, star-stickered hat) trots beside the post at the next bend. A wooden
post. A painted daisy on it, with one petal missing: a funny gap-toothed daisy.*

**HENRY**
(pleasantly)
Hello, daisy.

*The group marches on. The Boardwalk curves round a hill. They reach a fork with a tall
signpost: three arrows, painted in Jasper's neat letters: "TOR HILL ←", "STONE CIRCLE →", "HALL ↓".*

*Jasper checks the map. He points confidently along the "HALL ↓" arrow.*

**JASPER**
This way.

*They march on. The Boardwalk winds round a hill, round a bush, round a big rock...*

*...and arrives at a wooden post with a painted daisy that has one petal missing.*

**HENRY**
(slowly)
Hello, daisy.

*The campers stop. They look at the daisy. They look at each other. Someone's cocoa goes
"glug."*

**TAVITA FRANKLAND**
(not a whisper, telescope raised, hat flapping)
It's the Hound! He's leading us in CIRCLES!

*The campers gasp. Jasper's shoulders go stiff.*

**JASPER**
(too fast)
It is NOT the Hound. The signs are correct.

*Henry glances at the gap-toothed daisy. He gives it a polite nod.*

**CUT TO: TITLE CARD.** *Golden magnifier swirls. A tiny wobbly harmonica "woo."*

---

## ACT ONE: THE CASE ARRIVES (1:00 to 4:00)

### EXT. THE MAIN FORK. MORNING.

*The tall signpost stands at the fork, bright in the morning sun. KURSTIN HOLMES (cap, glasses,
raincoat) stands before it with her magnifier. MATEO WATSON is beside her, journal on his lap.
Jasper stands very straight a few feet away, clipboard clutched to his chest. Beryl hovers
behind him, twirling her plait.*

**HOLMES**
(clearly)
Your signs are wrong.

*A pause. Jasper stiffens. A stunned silence.*

**JASPER**
(coldly)
I painted these signs myself. The arrows are correct.

**HOLMES**
But the trail goes in circles. If the signs were right, it wouldn't.

**WATSON**
(gently, to Holmes)
Maybe ask him kindly.

**HOLMES**
(surprised)
I'm being kind. I said they're wrong, not that he's wrong.

**JASPER**
(frostily)
Rules. Are. Rules.

*He taps his clipboard twice. Beryl winces. Henry steps forward with a gentle smile.*

**HENRY**
Back home, we'd test it. Then we'd know.

*Holmes grins.*

**HOLMES**
Henry. Brilliant. A test.

*She pulls a ribbon from her raincoat pocket, a bright yellow one, and ties it to the signpost,
just under the "TOR HILL ←" arrow, in a neat bow.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
At eight o'clock, this sign points to Tor Hill. When we come back, we'll see whether it still
does.

*She turns the arrow, with one finger, to check: it is firm. She steps back, satisfied.*

**ANIMATOR:** Show the sign pointing clearly left, with the yellow ribbon fluttering beside the
arrow. This is the "before" image the kids will remember.

**JASPER**
(stiffly, not looking at anyone)
It will still be correct.

*Beryl opens her mouth. She looks at her brother. She looks at the post. She closes it again.*

**BERYL**
(very quietly)
Jasper...

**JASPER**
(not turning)
Not now, Beryl.

*Beryl drops her eyes. Watson catches her eye and tilts his head: Are you okay? She gives the
tiniest nod. Holmes, studying the arrow, doesn't notice.*

### EXT. THE MAIN FORK. TWO HOURS LATER.

*The sun is higher and the heather is dry. The team has gathered again. Watson holds up the
journal; Henry holds up a lantern; Tavita holds up a snack. Jasper stands apart, arms crossed.*

*The signpost stands exactly as before. Holmes's yellow ribbon still hangs from the post, just
under the arrow. But the arrow that was pointing to Tor Hill...*

*...points to the Stone Circle.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
Look at the ribbon.

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the ribbon on the post and
the arrow, which now points the wrong way.*

**HENRY**
(a gasp)
It turned!

**WATSON**
(writing)
Same ribbon. Different arrow.

*He sketches two matching signposts, with a ribbon on each and the arrows facing opposite
ways. A star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

**JASPER**
(very quietly)
...That isn't possible.

**HOLMES**
(carefully, looking at him)
It's happened, though.

*Jasper stares at the sign. For the first time, he looks unsure. Beryl's hand goes halfway
towards him, then stops.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(muttering)
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles the ribbon and the arrow.*

## ACT TWO: THE INVESTIGATION (4:00 to 8:00)

### EXT. THE MAIN FORK. CONTINUOUS.

*Holmes crouches. She sweeps her magnifier slowly around the base of the signpost. The soil
is soft and dark. In it, everywhere, are prints.*

*Not paws. Each print is neat and small and shaped like a little split heart, two oval halves
pressed together. Dozens of them, in a circle round the post.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Look at these.

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the little heart-shaped
prints, spiralling round the post.*

**HENRY**
(squinting)
They look like hearts.

**HOLMES**
Split hearts. Two toes. That's a hoof.

**WATSON**
(writing)
Hoof prints.

*He sketches one: two ovals pressed together like a heart. A star.*

**JASPER**
(from a distance, stiffly)
That's not possible. Hoofed animals can't turn a sign.

**HOLMES**
(not unkindly)
Can't they?

*Jasper doesn't answer.*

*A shout from the path.*

**TAVITA (O.S.)**
(delighted)
I FOUND PAW PRINTS!

*The group turns. Tavita stands on a side path, pointing at the ground with his telescope. The
path is muddy, soft and dark. Across it march enormous, round, four-toed prints with claw
marks.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
(thrilled)
Huge ones! They go right past the sign! It's the Hound! He turned the sign with his PAW!

*Holmes strides over and crouches by the big prints. She compares them with the little
heart-shaped ones near the post.*

**HOLMES**
Four toes. A big pad. Claws.

*She turns.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(pointing at the post)
Two toes. A split heart. No claws.

**WATSON**
(rolling over)
Different animals.

**HOLMES**
Different animals!

*A soft rustle in the heather. ZAID SELDEN (yellow raincoat, stuffed rabbit Tut) appears at the
edge of the path. Behind him, a huge shaggy shape crashes through the heather and arrives
beside him with a big stick in its mouth. MOONY.*

**ZAID**
(very quietly)
He likes walks. He doesn't like signs.

*Moony drops the stick at Tavita's feet. He wags his entire back half. He looks hopeful.*

**TAVITA**
(slowly, looking at the stick)
...Oh. Never mind.

**ANIMATOR:** A quick, gentle joke: Moony just wants the stick thrown. He's ignored the
signpost completely. He is the red herring and is cleared on the spot.

*Tavita throws the stick. Moony bounds off. Zaid trots after him. Henry waves.*

**HENRY**
Hello, Zaid.

*Holmes straightens up, satisfied.*

**HOLMES**
Moony's innocent. And so are his paws.

### EXT. THE MAIN FORK. A MOMENT LATER.

*Holmes returns to the post. She sweeps her magnifier slowly up the wooden signpost. At the edge
of the arrow marked "STONE CIRCLE →", on a splintery corner of the board, a little tuft of
something hangs.*

*It is soft, white and fluffy, and curly, with a tiny sprig of heather caught in it.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Wool.

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the tuft of wool and the
sprig of heather.*

**HENRY**
(wondering)
What has hooves and wool?

*Holmes does not answer. She looks at Watson. He looks at her. They both look at the tuft. A
thoughtful silence.*

**BERYL**
(very small, from the back)
Actually...

*Everyone turns. Beryl takes a step forward, sketchbook clutched to her chest. Her cheeks are
pink.*

**BERYL (CONT'D)**
(quietly)
I drew a sheep on Monday. Scratching her back on this post.

*She opens her sketchbook. On the page, in soft watercolour, a round white sheep with a bell,
leaning against the signpost. The post is tilting.*

**[JOURNAL]** *Watson's doodle of the wool and the sheep. A star.*

*Jasper stares at the drawing. His arms uncross.*

**JASPER**
(slowly)
You told me. On Monday.

**BERYL**
(small)
I said the post was wobbly.

**JASPER**
(quietly)
I said, "Rules are rules."

*A very long pause. A bird calls somewhere. Jasper's face is doing something complicated. He
sits down on the Boardwalk.*

**HOLMES**
(softly, for her)
Look closer.

**WATSON**
Notice more.

**HENRY**
Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles Jasper, then the post, then the tuft.*

## ACT THREE: THE REVEAL (8:00 to 9:30)

### EXT. THE MAIN FORK. SUNRISE, NEXT MORNING.

*Mist in the heather, pink and gold. Birdsong. The team is lying in the long grass beside the
Boardwalk, spying through the heather stems: Holmes with her magnifier, Watson with
binoculars, Henry with a lantern and a blanket, Beryl with her sketchbook, Tavita with a
snack. And, a little apart, wearing his neat sweater vest and no clipboard, Jasper.*

*They wait. Nothing happens. A cricket chirps.*

**HENRY**
(whispering)
Back home, we'd have a thermos for this.

*Footsteps. Soft ones. A dozen little hooves on wood.*

*Everyone goes still.*

*A big, round, fluffy sheep wanders into view along the Boardwalk, chewing. A bell on her
collar goes tink-tink-tink. DUCHESS.*

**TAVITA**
(a tiny whisper)
It's Duchess.

*Duchess stops at the signpost. She looks left. She looks right. She sidles up to the post,
turns her back to it, and, with a happy sigh, rubs her woolly back from side to side.*

*Scritch. Scritch. Scritch.*

*The signpost wobbles. The arrows begin to turn. TOR HILL ← swings right. STONE CIRCLE →
swings left. HALL ↓ spins right round, like a wheel.*

*Duchess closes her eyes, utterly content.*

**SFX:** *Baaaa.*

*She shifts her weight. The signpost spins once more, with a tiny, wobbly, delighted "wheeeee"
noise. It settles. TOR HILL now points towards the Hall.*

*Duchess opens one eye, surveys the post and wanders away, tink-tink-tink.*

**ANIMATOR:** This is the comic centre of the episode. Slow the shot a little. Duchess is
dignified and blissful; the sign is merrily out of control.

*The team rises, one by one, from the grass. Nobody speaks. Then Henry starts to giggle,
and then Tavita, and then, after a moment, a very small sound escapes Jasper.*

*He is laughing. It starts as a hiccup and turns into a full, helpless, delighted laugh. He
wipes his eyes.*

**JASPER**
(gasping)
A... sheep. A SHEEP.

**HOLMES**
(grinning)
Three clues. One: the arrow turned by itself. Two: heart-shaped hoofprints round the post.
Three: a tuft of white wool.

**WATSON**
(writing, DING-DING-DING)
Writing that down!

*Jasper walks slowly to the signpost. He puts a hand on the wood and gives it a little push.
It wobbles.*

*He looks at the bottom of the post. A bolt, loose, hangs out of the wood. He looks at another.
And another. Four of them dangle.*

**JASPER**
(very quietly)
I forgot to tighten them.

*The team goes quiet.*

**JASPER (CONT'D)**
(small)
I put the signs up in a rush. Before the walk. I didn't check the bolts. And when people got
lost I said "Rules are rules," because... because I didn't want to say I'd made a mistake.

*He turns to Beryl.*

**JASPER (CONT'D)**
(gently)
You told me, and I didn't listen.

**BERYL**
(softly)
It's hard to hear.

**JASPER**
(trying a smile)
I'm sorry, Beryl.

**BERYL**
(a tiny, wet smile)
Actually... I'm not cross. I just wanted the signs to work.

*She slips her hand into his.*

**HOLMES**
(stepping forward, a bit stiffly)
I'm also sorry. I told everyone the signs were wrong before I knew why.

**WATSON**
(gently)
That was very loud.

**HOLMES**
(sincerely)
It was a bit loud.

*Jasper nods, with a quick, grateful smile.*

## ACT FOUR: THE LESSON (9:30)

### EXT. THE MAIN FORK. MORNING.

*Together: Jasper with a spanner (small and shiny), Henry holding the post steady, Watson passing
bolts from his lap, Holmes handing out ribbons, Beryl painting a new, sturdier arrow. Tavita
holds the clipboard upside down.*

*Jasper tightens the last bolt with a satisfying click. He steps back. He gives the post a firm
shake. It doesn't move.*

**JASPER**
(proudly, quietly)
It's fixed.

*He tests each arrow in turn: TOR HILL ←, STONE CIRCLE →, HALL ↓. They stay perfectly still.*

*Duchess wanders into view, tink-tink-tink, and eyes the signpost hopefully. She rubs her
back on it. Scritch. Nothing moves. She looks puzzled.*

**BERYL**
(gently)
Oh, poor Duchess.

*Jasper looks at Duchess for a moment. Then at his clipboard. Then he walks briskly to a fallen
log by the path, plants it upright with Henry's help, and with a pot of brown paint writes
across it, in his neat capitals:*

**LOG (close-up):** *"DUCHESS'S SCRATCHING POST. Please scratch here."*

*Duchess trots over, turns her back and scratches. She closes her eyes in bliss.*

**ANJALI**
(arriving, whispering, wiping her eyes)
It's the best story I've ever seen.

**JASPER**
(a small, bright smile)
It's a good sign.

*Everyone groans. Beryl giggles. Jasper looks very pleased with himself.*

---

### EXT. THE BOARDWALK TRAIL. LATER. (BUTTON)

*The nature walk resumes, this time in a clean straight line: past TOR HILL ←, past STONE
CIRCLE →, past the gap-toothed daisy.*

**HENRY**
(passing the daisy, politely)
Goodbye, daisy.

*The daisy looks at him. The walk continues. Far in the heather, a huge shaggy shape follows at
a distance, dragging a stick the size of a tree.*

**THE END.**

## "HOW HOLMES FIGURED IT OUT" (9:30 to 11:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Fairy lights. The wall chart. Holmes and Watson on the floor with the journal. A yellow ribbon
is pinned beside the chart. On the desk, Beryl's watercolour sheep has been propped up like a
treasure.*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Case journal, day seventy-one. Case closed. The trail went in circles because a sheep
scratched her back.

**HOLMES**
Let's look at the clues.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame fills the screen.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**CLUE ONE: THE TURNING ARROW**

*The two matching signposts replay, with the yellow ribbon and the arrows facing opposite ways.*

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
My yellow ribbon stayed on the sign, but the arrow had turned. The sign wasn't wrong. It was
moving by itself.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE TWO: THE HEART-SHAPED PRINTS**

*The little split-heart prints replay, ringed round the post.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
Two toes, in the shape of a heart. That's a hoof, not a paw. Moony's prints have four toes
and claws. These were someone else.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE THREE: THE WOOL**

*The tuft of wool and the sprig of heather replay.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A tuft of white wool on the sign. Hooves and wool. There's only one animal at camp that fits.

*A gold star, a heart, and a doodle of Duchess scratching her back.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(turning to Watson)
And Beryl told us, on day one. She said the post was wobbly.

**WATSON**
(gently)
Nobody was listening.

**HOLMES**
(a beat)
...I wasn't either.

*She picks up her pencil.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(writing carefully on the chart)
Listen to the quiet one.

**WATSON**
(smiling)
That's the best rule.

*He writes, with a flourish: "SAY SORRY. LISTEN. FIX IT TOGETHER." He draws a heart. DING-DING-DING:
three dings for "solved."*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
Case closed. Hound cleared.

*Holmes adds a line to her wall chart: "THINGS THAT GO BUMP: A SHEEP AND A LOOSE BOLT."*

**HOLMES**
Curious... very curious!

## Complexity and logic check

- **Clue 1** is a physical test the viewer can do at home: tie a ribbon, check later.
- **Clue 2** is a contrast of two print shapes (dog paw versus hoof).
- **Clue 3** is easy, and Beryl's sketch confirms the answer before the stakeout.
- The reveal (a sheep scratching) is visual and funny, so first-time viewers get the "aha."

## Flag summary

| Flag | Where | Fix built in |
|---|---|---|
| "Getting lost" | Cold open | A short, bright morning walk; a funny daisy; no one is ever far from the Hall |
| Jasper's stiffness | Acts One, Two | Played as awkward pride; he's quickly sympathetic; Holmes's loudness is also called out |
| Public "wrong" | Act One | Holmes says the signs are wrong in front of everyone (her flaw); Watson and Jasper both address it; she apologises later |
| Beryl torn | Acts One to Three | Quiet and gentle; her honesty is respected; Jasper apologises to her |
| Moony as red herring | Act Two | He is just on a walk with Zaid; cleared in seconds |

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Jasper's "Rules are rules"** is used as his shield. It shows his flaw and makes him
   sympathetic. In the end he laughs at himself, which I like for his character. Right
   direction?
2. **Holmes's flaw** (being loud and blunt) gets a gentle callout from Watson and a short
   apology from her. I kept her more oblivious than in earlier episodes: she doesn't notice
   Beryl's worry until Beryl speaks. Her closing line, "Listen to the quiet one," is a
   growth beat; cut it if you want her to stay at zero.
3. **Duchess's Scratching Post** and the sign pun ("It's a good sign") are new gags. Keep the
   pun? It is the only wordplay in the season so far.
4. **Beryl's "Actually... I'm not cross"** uses her catchphrase in a warm way, not a
   correction. Okay as a variation?
5. **No leash.** Moony walks beside Zaid carrying a stick. A dog this big and gentle doesn't need
   one in the park, and it avoids confusing the entrance.
