# EPISODE 8: "THE FLYING LANTERN"

*11 minutes · Script draft 1 · Stage 4*

**Spotlight:** Watson · **Lesson:** Teamwork (ask for help; a tangle is easier with many hands)
**Clues:** (1) the light drifts the way the wind blows and sinks when the wind stops · (2) a long, thin string glinting on a Boardwalk post · (3) a soft paper "flap-flap" sound
**Red herring:** Tavita swears it is a flying hound (a round owl sitting beside the light)
**Setting:** the Boardwalk Trail, the heather hills, Tor Hill's lower slope

*Script key, look reminders and flags work as in `ep01-the-hound-comes-home.md`.*

**Optional thread:** The dining-room chalkboard reads **"CAMP DAY: 25 DAYS TO GO."**

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00 to 1:00)

### EXT. BOARDWALK TRAIL. EVENING.

*Golden hour. The heather glows rose and violet. A group of campers strolls along the
Boardwalk with mugs of cocoa, the flowers on the posts glowing in the low sun. Fireflies begin
to wink.*

*ANJALI MORTIMER (plum cape, lantern, curls) walks backwards at the front, conducting with a
marshmallow stick.*

**ANJALI**
(booming, quick)
Remember! The Hound is big, he's shaggy, and he wants a snack!

**CAMPERS**
(together, sleepy)
...WANTS A SNACK.

*HENRY BASKERVILLE (plaid, star-stickered hat) stops mid-sip. He is looking up at the sky above
the nearest hill.*

**HENRY**
(slowly)
Anjali? What's that?

*Above the hill, small and bright and perfectly round, a warm yellow glow floats in the pink
sky. It bobs. It drifts sideways. It tilts, then rights itself, like a tiny moon with a hiccup.*

*Every camper goes silent. Cocoa steams.*

**ANJALI**
(a delighted, hushed gasp)
The Hound's lantern.

**HENRY**
(hat sliding down)
Does the Hound have a lantern?

**ANJALI**
(blissful)
He does now.

*TAVITA FRANKLAND pops up from behind a post, telescope raised, hat flapping.*

**TAVITA**
(not a whisper)
It's the Hound!

*He swings the telescope. The glow bobs behind him. He swings back. The glow has moved. He
swings again.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
It keeps moving!

**HENRY**
(patiently)
That's because it's a light.

**TAVITA**
(thinking about that)
...Ohhh.

**CUT TO: TITLE CARD.** *Golden magnifier swirls. A tiny wobbly harmonica "woo."*

## ACT ONE: THE CASE ARRIVES (1:00 to 4:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. EVENING.

*Fairy lights, maps, the wall chart "THINGS THAT GO BUMP." IMANI HOLMES (cap, glasses, raincoat)
stands on her bed with her nose pressed to the window, magnifier up. MATEO WATSON sits at his
desk with the journal open and a pencil behind his ear. He is drawing, very small and neatly,
and looks up at the window every few seconds.*

**HOLMES**
It's been there three evenings. Same time. Same place. Same bob.

*Outside the window, the glow floats over the far hill.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(to herself)
Round. Warm. Floating.

**WATSON**
(quietly)
It does look... familiar.

**HOLMES**
(not hearing, taking notes)
Familiar? No, it's definitely a mystery. Write that down.

*Watson writes "FAMILIAR?" in the margin and then, very quickly, closes the journal on his
hand, as if he'd said too much. DING (once, nervous).*

**ANIMATOR:** Watson is quietly worried. Holmes, absorbed, misses it entirely, and the viewer
should notice it. Keep the beat subtle and warm.

*Holmes jumps off the bed and lands in front of Watson with a thump.*

**HOLMES**
Case journal, day sixty-one! A light on the hill!

**WATSON**
(smiling)
It's already day sixty-one.

**HOLMES**
Then it's overdue.

*She taps her magnifier twice.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles the window and the glow beyond it.*

### EXT. HEATHER HILLS, BOARDWALK, TOR HILL FOOT. EVENING.

*The glowing light hovers above a distant ridge. The team has hurried out along the Boardwalk:
Holmes in front, Watson rolling alongside, Henry carrying a lantern, Tavita carrying nothing but
a very large hat. The Boardwalk curves between purple hills. A little flag on a post flutters.*

*They stop. Holmes holds up a finger. Everyone looks at the sky.*

*The glow drifts to the left. The flag on the post flutters to the left.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Watch.

*The wind drops. The flag hangs limp. The glow slowly, gently, sinks, as if it were a
leaf. It dips toward the ridge.*

*The wind picks up again. The flag lifts. The glow lifts with it.*

**WATSON**
(softly)
It moves with the wind.

**HOLMES**
(delighted)
The flag goes left, the light goes left. The wind stops, the light sinks!

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the flag and the glow
side by side, a golden arrow linking them.*

**HENRY**
(a bright thought)
Do hounds follow the wind?

**HOLMES**
(a beat)
Excellent question. I have no idea.

**WATSON**
(writing, quietly)
Writing that down!

*He sketches the flag and the glow, with two matching arrows. Beside it: a star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

*Watson looks at his own doodle for a long moment. His pencil hovers. His bell does not ring.*

## ACT TWO: THE INVESTIGATION (4:00 to 8:00)

### EXT. BOARDWALK TRAIL, A WINDING STRETCH. DUSK.

*The light is deeper now, rose and gold. The glow bobs closer. The team follows the Boardwalk
along the side of a hill, where it passes a signpost with a painted arrow: "STONE CIRCLE →".*

*Holmes pauses. A thin line glints in the low light, curled around the signpost. She crouches.*

**HOLMES**
A thread.

*It is very thin, as fine as a hair, shimmering in the sun. It is looped twice around the
post, then trails away across the heather.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the string as it curves
away toward the hill.*

*Holmes follows the line with her magnifier, hand over hand, as if holding a rope, and looks up.
At the far end of the string, tiny against the sky, bobs the glow.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(whispering)
It's tied to something.

**HENRY**
(excited)
It's on a string!

**TAVITA**
(not whispering)
THE HOUND'S ON A LEASH!

**HOLMES**
(smiling)
I don't think the Hound is on a leash, Tavita.

**WATSON**
(small)
A string... and a light. Holmes?

**HOLMES**
(not hearing, thrilled)
Yes?

*Watson opens his mouth. His eyes slide to Henry, to Tavita, to the long string. He closes it.*

**WATSON**
...Never mind.

**ANIMATOR:** This echoes Tavita's "Never mind," but in a worried, hesitant way. Do not make it
a joke. Watson has an answer and is nervous to say it.

*Holmes turns, finally noticing. She looks at him. She frowns, a tiny frown, thinking.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
Are you alright?

**WATSON**
(too brightly)
Fine!

**HOLMES**
(relieved)
Excellent.

*She turns back to the string. Watson's shoulders sink, just a little. DING (once).*

### EXT. TOR HILL, LOWER SLOPE. DUSK.

*A big, rounded boulder at the foot of Tor Hill. Perched on top of it, perfectly round, perfectly
fluffy and perfectly still, sits an enormous owl with huge amber eyes. Beside it, a few feet
away, the warm glow bobs on the end of its string.*

*Tavita sees it first. He gasps so loudly that the owl rotates its head all the way round.*

**TAVITA**
(in a quavering whisper)
A flying hound.

*The owl stares at him. Slowly, it spreads its wings, a wide, soft, silent fan of feathers.
The sun gleams through them.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
(awed)
It's got WINGS.

**HOLMES**
(drily)
Owls have wings.

**TAVITA**
(slowly)
This isn't a hound.

**HOLMES**
It's an owl.

*The owl blinks at him once, and says in a soft, round voice:*

**OWL**
Hoo.

**TAVITA**
(deeply moved)
...Oh. Never mind.

*The owl flaps away, unbothered. Everyone laughs, softly. Henry waves at it.*

**ANIMATOR:** The owl is plump, fluffy and charming, never menacing. Treat the wing-spread as
comic, not scary, with a little "fwump" sound and a drifting feather.

### EXT. TOR HILL, LOWER SLOPE. A MOMENT LATER.

*In the sudden quiet, there is a new sound. It is soft and dry and rhythmic, like someone
shaking out a paper bag.*

**SFX:** *Flap-flap-flap. Flap-flap. Flap.*

*Everyone listens. Holmes tilts her head. The sound is coming from the glow.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Paper.

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline shows soft paper-coloured
ripples drifting from the glow to the group.*

**HENRY**
(whispering)
Why is it going flap?

**HOLMES**
Because it's made of something that flaps in the wind.

*Watson has gone completely still. He is staring at the glow, and the glow, tugging on its
string, is dipping and rising and tilting, as if saying hello.*

**[JOURNAL]** *Watson's hand, almost without thinking, draws a tiny diamond shape with a long
tail. The doodle glows. He stares at it. His face falls.*

*He looks at the page he drew. It is a kite. It is exactly the kite on the first page of his
journal, which says: "CAMP DAY KITE. LANTERN KITE. FAIRY LIGHTS INSIDE."*

---

## ACT THREE: THE REVEAL (8:00 to 9:30)

### EXT. TOR HILL, LOWER SLOPE. CONTINUOUS.

*Holmes has paced a circle round the group, magnifier raised.*

**HOLMES**
Three clues. Clue one: the light moves with the wind. Clue two: it's tied to a string. Clue
three: it flaps, like paper.

*She stops. Her eyes widen.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
It's a kite! A kite with a light inside!

**HENRY**
(delighted)
A lantern kite!

**TAVITA**
(also delighted)
I SAW A FLYING LANTERN!

*Silence. The kite bobs gently above them.*

**HOLMES**
(cheerfully)
Whose kite is it?

*Nobody answers. Holmes looks round. Everyone is looking at Watson.*

*Watson takes a breath. He looks at the sky. He looks at the string. His bell is silent.*

**WATSON**
(small)
...Mine.

*Holmes's mouth falls open.*

**HOLMES**
Yours?

**WATSON**
(rushing)
It was for Camp Day. I built it all week. I wanted to show everyone. And I took it up Tor Hill
to test it, and the wind came, and it pulled out of my hand, and I couldn't catch it. And then
you said it was a mystery, and everyone was so excited, and I didn't... know how to say it
was mine.

*He stops. His ears are pink.*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
(very quietly)
I thought you'd be disappointed it wasn't a hound.

*A beat. Holmes looks at him. For once, she doesn't leap to the next clue. She sits down on
the Boardwalk next to his chair. She looks at the kite for a long moment.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
Watson. I told you it looked familiar and I didn't listen.

**WATSON**
(small smile)
You were very busy.

**HOLMES**
(sincerely)
That's not a good reason. I'm sorry.

*Watson's face softens. DING, once, gentle.*

**HENRY**
(kindly)
We'd have helped you catch it.

**TAVITA**
(delighted)
I'd have caught it with my HAT!

**WATSON**
(wiping his nose, smiling)
...I should have asked.

**HOLMES**
It's hard to ask.

**ANJALI**
(arriving breathless with a lantern)
It's the best kite I've ever seen.

*Watson blinks up at her. Her eyes are shining.*

**ANJALI (CONT'D)**
(softly)
It's a lantern. It floats. It's a story.

*Watson's whole face lights up.*

---

## ACT FOUR: THE TEAM (9:30)

### EXT. TOR HILL, LOWER SLOPE. A MOMENT LATER.

*Everyone looks up at the lantern kite. The wind has dropped. It sinks gently, but the long
string has twisted round the signpost, round a tuft of gorse and round a fence. It is a
beautiful tangle.*

**HOLMES**
(sighing)
It's in a knot.

**WATSON**
(looking at the tangle, then at his friends)
...I can't do this one alone.

*The words hang there. Watson, bravely and clearly:*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
Please can you help?

*Everyone steps forward at once.*

**ALL**
(together)
Yes!

*A quick, warm, funny montage. Henry untwists the string around the signpost with his long
arms. Tavita holds the reel. Anjali holds the lantern up and reads the tangle out loud like a
story ("Over the gorse, under the fence..."). Holmes guides the string, hand over hand. Watson,
wheeling to the middle, calls out the next move with his bell, like an orchestra conductor.*

*DING-DING. Go left. DING. Stop. DING-DING. Go.*

*The last loop slips free. The kite lifts, glowing, into the sky, free and golden.*

*Watson takes the reel from Tavita. He holds the string. The kite dances above the pink hills,
an enormous, soft, glowing flower of paper and light.*

**WATSON**
(softly)
Hello.

*Everyone gazes up. The kite's warm light is reflected in every pair of eyes. A single
firefly drifts past. Somewhere nearby a harmonica note drifts across the hills: Jasper's,
faint.*

**ANIMATOR:** This is the quiet high point. Give it room. No dialogue for five seconds.

---

### EXT. TOR HILL, LOWER SLOPE. LATER. (BUTTON)

*The group heads home along the Boardwalk, with Watson holding the kite reel and the lantern
kite bobbing overhead like a pet. Behind them, the heather rustles.*

*MOONY bursts out, delighted, and chases the dangling string. He leaps. He misses. He
catches the loose end and trots in circles, trailing the string, completely tangled up in
three seconds. A wrapped, wobbling, grinning dog.*

*Zaid trots after him, in yellow, with Tut, whispering.*

**ZAID**
(whispering)
Moony. No.

*Moony looks at the kite. He looks at Watson. He lets out a tiny, muffled "woo," through a
mouthful of string.*

*Watson laughs so hard he has to stop and hold the reel up high. Everyone helps untangle. Again.*

**THE END.**

## "HOW HOLMES FIGURED IT OUT" (9:30 to 11:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Fairy lights. The wall chart. Holmes and Watson on the floor with the journal. Through the
window, the lantern kite floats above the Hall, tied to the roof with a long string.*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Case journal, day sixty-one. Case closed. The glow on the hill was a kite, and the kite was mine.

**HOLMES**
Let's look at the clues.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame fills the screen.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**CLUE ONE: THE WIND AND THE LIGHT**

*The flag and the glow replay side by side, with the golden arrow.*

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
The light went where the wind went. When the wind stopped, the light sank. Something that
floats on the wind. Not a hound.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE TWO: THE STRING**

*The thin, shimmering string looped round the signpost replays.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A long, thin string wrapped round a post, running to the light. If it has a string, someone
was holding it. Someone let go.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE THREE: THE FLAP-FLAP**

*The paper-coloured ripples replay.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A dry flap-flap, like paper in the wind. Wind. String. Paper. Light. That's a kite.

*A gold star, a heart, and a doodle of a lantern kite with a face.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(turning to Watson)
And one thing I should have noticed sooner.

**WATSON**
(smiling)
That it was mine?

**HOLMES**
That you were worried. You said "familiar." Twice.

**WATSON**
(gently)
You noticed in the end.

**HOLMES**
(grinning)
Next time I'll listen the first time.

*Watson writes, with a flourish: "ASK FOR HELP. MANY HANDS, FEWER KNOTS." He draws a heart.
DING-DING-DING: three dings for "solved."*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Case closed. Hound cleared.

*Holmes adds a line to her wall chart: "THINGS THAT GO BUMP: A KITE WITH A HICCUP."*

**HOLMES**
Curious... very curious!

## Complexity and logic check

- Each clue is visible or audible (an arrow, a string, a ripple of sound).
- The solution needs three small steps combined: wind + string + paper = kite. Holmes lists it
  aloud so viewers can follow.
- Watson's hesitation is the emotional thread; the mystery clues are a puzzle for kids to
  solve before he confesses.

## Flag summary

| Flag | Where | Fix built in |
|---|---|---|
| A floating light at dusk | Cold open, Act One | Golden hour, warm yellow glow, bobbing like a hiccup; Henry's calm explanation within a minute |
| Owl | Act Two | Round, fluffy, silent, "Hoo"; a feather drifts; played for comedy |
| Watson hiding the truth | Acts One to Three | Gentle, sympathetic, not deceitful; Holmes apologises for not listening |
| Dusk on the hills | Acts Two, Three | Golden-hour light, glittery mist; friends together; lantern and kite glow |
| Watson and the Boardwalk | Throughout | The Boardwalk is his route; he leads the untangling by bell, with no pity beats |

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Holmes misses Watson's worry** until the reveal, then apologises. After your note in the last
   batch I let her stay oblivious for most of the episode. Her apology is short and sincere.
   Right level?
2. **Jasper's harmonica** is a quick background sound at the quiet high point. It's a wink for
   viewers of the pilot. It might read oddly to a first-time viewer; I can cut it.
3. **Watson says "Never mind" in Act Two.** It's a nervous echo of Tavita's catchphrase. It
   uses up the "never mind" allowance for Watson, not Tavita; Tavita says his own too, so
   the episode has two. Okay, or cut Watson's?
4. **The Camp Day kite** sets up the finale. It doesn't require the finale to mention it.
   Should the kite reappear on Camp Day as a bonus?
