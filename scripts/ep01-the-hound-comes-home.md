# EPISODE 1: "THE HOUND COMES HOME"

*Pilot special · 22 minutes in two 11-minute halves · Script draft 2 · Stage 4*

**Lesson:** Kindness. Sharing a discovery makes it bigger, not smaller.
**Clues:** (1) stamped glow-paint paw prints · (2) the harmonica wobble and the green netting · (3) cheese crumbs and wobbly grey paw prints
**Red herring:** Tavita's "Hound" on Tor Hill (Anjali in the boot-room rug)

---

## SCRIPT KEY

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **INT. / EXT.** | Scene heading: inside or outside, place, time of day |
| *Italic lines* | Action: what we see and hear |
| **NAME** | Dialogue. `(parenthetical)` is how it's said |
| **[CLUE ALERT #n, 1st/2nd]** | Soft chime and warm sparkle. Every clue gets it twice |
| **[CHANT]** | Golden magnifier frame circles the scene |
| **[JOURNAL]** | Cut to Watson's 2D crayon doodles on the 3D page |
| **ANIMATOR:** | A note for the animation team |
| **⚠️ FLAG** | A moment that might be too scary or too complex, with the fix built in |

**Look reminders** (from the bible): dusk is golden and sparkly, never black. The howl is a
silly "woo-oo-oo" with a wobble, more harmonica than horror. Moony is shown in silhouette
only until the reveal, always with one floppy ear and a wagging tail.

# PART 1: "THE LEGEND"

## COLD OPEN (0:00 to 1:00)

### EXT. CAMP BASKERVILLE, MAIN GATE. GOLDEN DUSK.

*A tall iron gate with a swirly "B" on it. Behind it, the Hall: an old stone manor with a
wobbly tower, lit windows and a wide wooden ramp painted with flowers. Beyond it, purple
heather hills roll away under a pink-gold sky. Glittery mist curls over the grass.*

*HENRY BASKERVILLE (9, very tall, gangly, auburn curls, red plaid shirt, bobble hat)
stands at the gate with an enormous backpack. He holds a small photo, looks at it, and
sniffs. He steps through the gate.*

**ANIMATOR:** Start with a wide, sunny, cosy shot. Henry is tall, so frame him from a low
angle that makes him look lanky and friendly, not looming.

*He walks up the path. His backpack bumps the gatepost. CLANG. He bounces off. He
apologises to the gatepost.*

**HENRY**
Oh! Sorry!

*Far away, over the hills, comes a long, wobbly sound.*

**SFX:** *"Woo-oo-oo-oooo!"* A silly, round sound, like a very big harmonica being blown by
a very big puppy.

*Henry freezes. His hat slides down over his eyes. He peeks out from under the brim.*

**ANIMATOR:** The howl is gold: soft musical notes drift over the heather like fireflies.

### EXT. THE HALL, ROOF. CONTINUOUS.

*TAVITA FRANKLAND (9, tall, strong, huge sun hat, brass telescope) is sitting on the roof
ridge with his legs dangling. He points the telescope toward the hills.*

**TAVITA**
(a loud whisper, which is not a whisper)
It's the Hound!

*A dozen CAMPERS pop out of windows and doors. Gasps.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
It's big! It's hairy! It's... a bush.

*He turns the telescope the right way round.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
Oh. Never mind.

*The campers sigh and go back inside. Henry stands alone in the garden, hat over his
eyes.*

**HENRY**
(small)
Hello?

*A SQUEAK of wheels. A bell goes DING-DING.*

*MATEO WATSON (10, wheelchair user, teal hoodie, orange chair covered in stickers) rolls
down the ramp with a wide, warm smile. A green notebook peeks out of the satchel on the
back of his chair.*

**WATSON**
Hi! You must be Henry. I'm Watson. Welcome to Camp Baskerville.

**HENRY**
(pushing his hat up)
Is it always this... howly?

**WATSON**
(thinking)
Um. Sometimes.

*DING-DING goes his bell: twice, happy.*

**CUT TO: TITLE CARD.** *Golden magnifier swirls around the words "HOLMES & WATSON: THE HOUND
OF CAMP BASKERVILLE." A tiny howl "woo" plays at the end, with a wobble.*

**ANIMATOR:** The title sting is a very short harmonica phrase, the same wobble that becomes
Clue #2. Keep it cheerful and brief, 8 seconds at most.

**⚠️ FLAG (cold open howl):** The howl is the only "spooky" sound in the first minute. It
plays under a sunny pink-gold sky and is followed within ten seconds by Tavita's "Never
mind." Keep the notes golden and bouncy, never low or long.

## ACT ONE: THE CASE ARRIVES (1:00 to 4:00)

### INT. THE HALL, DINING ROOM. EARLY EVENING.

*A long wooden table. Lanterns. Portraits of old Baskervilles on the walls, in very silly
hats. LINH and MINH BARRYMORE (10, twins, matching aprons, Linh's yellow headband, Minh's
bright orange hearing aids) are setting out bowls.*

*Henry and Watson enter. The twins swing around at the same time.*

**LINH**
New kid! Fourth bunk on the—

**MINH**
—left. In Bracken Cabin.

**LINH**
Careful on the—

**LINH & MINH**
(together)
Third stair!

**MINH**
...always squeaks.

*Henry steps on the stair. SQUEAK. Everyone smiles.*

**HENRY**
(delighted)
It really does!

**LINH**
Grandpa made soup. Phở!

**MINH**
With extra noodles.

**HENRY**
Back home, we'd have pancakes for dinner.

**LINH**
(shocked)
Pancakes for DINNER?

**MINH**
(to Linh, in sign, with subtitle: "Why don't we do this?")

**SUBTITLE:** *Why don't we do that?*

**ANIMATOR:** Minh signs; the subtitle floats in a soft white bubble, never covering faces.
Show his hearing aids catch the lantern light. Treat the signing like any other
conversation.

### EXT. CAMPFIRE CIRCLE. DUSK.

*A ring of logs around a small, crackling, friendly fire. Lanterns hang from poles. The
sky is gold and violet. Glittery mist curls around everyone's ankles. Fireflies.*

*The campers settle on the logs. Henry sits next to Watson. TAVITA sits at the back,
telescope on his lap. A tall chair has been set at the head of the circle.*

*ANJALI MORTIMER (11, round, strong, enormous curly hair under a silk scarf, a plum-purple
velvet cape) sweeps in, lantern held high.*

**ANJALI**
(booming)
Gather round, little campers... for the tale of the Hound!

*The campers cheer.*

**ANIMATOR:** SWITCH TO PAPER-CUT PUPPET STYLE for the legend. Flat painted paper shapes on
sticks, moving against a glowing backdrop, with Anjali's voice-over and shadow-puppet
hands in the corner of frame.

**ANJALI (V.O.)**
Long, long ago, when the Hall was new...
the Baskervilles had a hound.

*Paper-cut hound: enormous, shaggy, with big round eyes made of two yellow circles.*

**ANJALI (V.O.) (CONT'D)**
A great big hound. A shaggy hound.
A hound as tall as a pony,
with eyes that glowed like two little moons.

*The paper hound climbs a paper hill. A paper moon rises behind him.*

**ANJALI (V.O.) (CONT'D)**
And whenever a Baskerville came home to the Hall...
the hound would climb to the top of Tor Hill
and howl across the park:

*Anjali, in the real world, cups her hands.*

**ANJALI**
"Woo-oo-oooooo!"

*Campers shiver, delighted. Tavita grabs the nearest person.*

**ANJALI (CONT'D)**
Some say he's still out there.
Some say he's waiting.
And some say...

*A long pause. Everyone leans in.*

**ANJALI (CONT'D)**
(a wink)
...he just wants a snack.

*The campers burst into laughter. Henry laughs too, then stops.*

**HENRY**
Wait. A Baskerville came home. That's... me.

*Every head turns to Henry. Silence. Then Tavita lifts his telescope and looks at Henry,
very closely.*

**TAVITA**
It's a Baskerville.

**HENRY**
(pulling his hat down)
Is that the... *good* kind?

**ANJALI**
(delighted)
The snack-loving kind!

*More laughter. Henry peeks out from under his hat, and smiles for the first time.*

**⚠️ FLAG (the legend):** This is the show's spookiest moment. It ends on a joke, with a
wink, and the paper-puppet style tells children it is a story, not a real threat. There is no
curse, no harm, no danger in the telling.

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*A cosy cabin lit by a string of fairy lights. Two bunks. Maps, drawings, a very tidy
shelf of jars (a button, a bottle cap, a leaf). On the wall: a hand-drawn sign reading
"HOLMES AND WATSON, DETECTIVES. KNOCK FIRST."*

*IMANI HOLMES (10, small, wiry, round gold-rimmed glasses, green tweed deerstalker-style cap
with its flaps tied up, golden-blonde locs in two buns poking out at the sides, mustard-yellow raincoat) is crouched on the floor with a magnifying
glass, examining a button.*

*Watson rolls in, followed by Henry, who ducks under the doorframe.*

**WATSON**
Holmes, this is Henry. He just arrived.

*Holmes doesn't look up. She holds up one finger. She hums three notes.*

**HOLMES**
Hmm. Hm-hm.

*She jumps up, looks at Henry through the magnifier (his eye is enormous), then hops back.*

**ANIMATOR:** Henry's magnified eye should be goofy and warm. He blinks. She blinks. They
both laugh.

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
You came a long way. By boat, then bus, then a very long car.

**HENRY**
(amazed)
How do you...?

**HOLMES**
Sand in your left shoe. A ferry ticket in your pocket. And your hat has a bus seat on it.

*She points at a tiny pattern on the hat.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
You miss someone. A lot. You have a photo.

*Henry's hand goes to his pocket.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
It's your dad and a baby.

**HENRY**
(quietly)
My sister. Hana.

**HOLMES**
(delighted)
Curious... very curious!

*She taps her magnifier twice. Henry's eyes are shining. Holmes doesn't see. She is
already kneeling to look at his bootlace.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Double knot. You're careful. Or you're worried. Or both. Interesting!

*Watson rolls up beside Henry and speaks softly, so only Henry hears.*

**WATSON**
(gently)
Missing people is hard. I miss my dads, too.

**HENRY**
(a little voice)
A bit.

*Watson takes a small gold star sticker from his chair and sticks it on the front of
Henry's hat. Henry touches it. Almost smiles.*

**HOLMES**
(not looking up, scribbling)
Why did everyone stop talking?

*Watson and Henry share a look. DING-DING.*

**ANIMATOR:** Holmes is busy with the bootlace and doesn't notice. The warmth in this scene
belongs to Watson and Henry. Holmes's oblivious moment is a gentle gag, not a flaw to
punish.

**WATSON**
(writing)
Writing that down!

*He opens the green journal. A crayon doodle of Henry, tall and shy, appears on the page.*

**[JOURNAL]** *Watson's voice-over as the pen scribbles.*

**WATSON (V.O.)**
Case journal, day one. A new camper came to the Hall. His name is Henry Baskerville. He is
tall. He is kind. He is a little bit homesick. And somewhere, far away... a hound howled.

**ANIMATOR:** The journal pages are 2D crayon-and-pencil, popping out in tiny sparkles. The
page-turn sound is soft and papery.

## ACT TWO: THE CASE (4:00 to 8:00)

### EXT. BRACKEN CABIN, FRONT STEPS. MORNING.

*Bright sun. Birds. Henry stands on the steps in socks and one boot. He is holding up his
other boot, which isn't there.*

**HENRY**
(calling)
Holmes? Watson? My boot's gone!

*Holmes and Watson run (roll) up. Several campers gather around.*

**TAVITA**
(gasping)
The Hound took it!

**ANJALI**
(sweeping in, thrilled)
The legend is TRUE!

**HOLMES**
(calm)
The legend is a legend.

*She crouches next to the step and examines the spot where the boot was. Nothing. A
smudge of green on the wood.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(muttering)
Look closer. Notice more.

**WATSON**
(joining in)
Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *A golden magnifier frame circles the empty spot on the step. A tiny sparkle
catches the smudge.*

**HOLMES**
Green. Bright green. And sticky!

*She touches it, then holds up a finger. It glows faintly.*

**[SPARKLE ONLY]** *A warm sparkle on Holmes's fingertip. No chime: the chime is reserved for numbered clues.*

*A trail of glowing GREEN PAW PRINTS runs from the step down the Boardwalk Trail, toward the
purple hills.*

**ANIMATOR:** Make the prints bright, cartoony and neon green, with a tiny sparkle on each.
They should look **painted**, not scary. They glow in full morning sunshine.

### EXT. BOARDWALK TRAIL, HEATHER HILLS. MORNING.

*A wooden walkway winds through waves of purple heather. A flower is painted on every post.
Holmes walks in front, Watson rolls next to her, Henry follows, ducking under flowers. A
crowd of campers trails behind.*

*The paw prints keep going: one, two, three, four... Holmes crouches. Her eyes dart.*

**HOLMES**
(counting)
Big. Big. Big. Big. All the same.

**HENRY**
(in a small voice)
Is that bad?

**HOLMES**
It's... interesting.

*She crouches. She places her own hand next to a print. She looks at the next one. Then
the next.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Real paws wobble. They're a little bit different each time. These are exactly the same!
Same size. Same spacing. Same fuzz of paint at the edge.

**WATSON**
(writing)
So, they're... stamped?

**HOLMES**
Maybe.

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline flashes around one print.*

**[JOURNAL]** *Watson doodles a paw print. Beside it he writes: "ALL THE SAME?" and draws a
tiny star.*

*A figure steps onto the Boardwalk ahead of them, blocking the path with a clipboard.*

*JASPER STAPLETON (11, slim, neat, tan sweater vest, Nature Club badge, butterfly net
over his shoulder) holds up one hand like a traffic officer.*

**JASPER**
Stop. This area is closed.

**HOLMES**
Why?

**JASPER**
For your safety. The Hound has been seen. Rules are rules!

*He taps his clipboard twice. Nervously.*

**ANIMATOR:** Jasper stands very straight. His net has a small, neat patch of green netting
missing at the tip. (We'll see the scrap later. Keep it in frame, but not highlighted.)

*Behind him, BERYL STAPLETON (9, small, long red plait, lilac cardigan with butterfly
buttons, sketchbook) peeks out. She twirls her plait. She says nothing.*

**HENRY**
But my boot...

**JASPER**
(softening a tiny bit)
I'm sure it will turn up. Please stay on the Boardwalk. And don't go past the Stone Circle.
It's... closed. For nature.

**BERYL**
(small)
Jasper...

**JASPER**
(not looking)
Rules are rules, Beryl.

*Beryl looks at her feet. Holmes notices Beryl's muddy shoes. She doesn't notice her worried
face.*

**HOLMES**
Curious.

*Jasper marches away. Holmes watches him go. Then, casually, she follows the prints with her
eyes, down the path, until they turn toward a tall hill with a lookout rock on top.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Tor Hill.

### EXT. TOR HILL, BOARDWALK BASE. MIDDAY.

*A wide, sunny lookout. A sign reads "TOR HILL" with a painted dog (a friendly doodle) on
it. At the top, a huge, shaggy shape moves among the rocks.*

*Tavita is already there, pointing, enormous hat flapping.*

**TAVITA**
(gasping)
I SEE IT! The Hound! It's HUGE! It's got EYES!

*Everyone stops. Henry grabs Watson's chair. Watson's bell goes DING (once, nervous).*

*On the rock: a huge, shaggy brown shape with two shiny round eyes, its back to us.*

**⚠️ FLAG (red herring):** This is the "monster" gag. It's in bright daylight, with Tavita
yelling, so it reads as comedy. The reveal comes within thirty seconds.

**HOLMES**
(narrowing her eyes)
Hmm. That fur is... carpet.

*The shaggy shape turns around. It's ANJALI, wearing the boot-room rug over her shoulders
and holding two round bike reflectors up to her eyes.*

**ANJALI**
(deep, theatrical)
Wooooo... I am the Hound of the Baskervilles!

*Silence. Then she coughs.*

**ANJALI (CONT'D)**
(normal voice)
...I was rehearsing for tonight's campfire.

*Tavita lowers his telescope.*

**TAVITA**
Oh. Never mind.

*Everyone laughs. Henry laughs the loudest. Watson's bell goes DING-DING.*

**ANIMATOR:** Keep the rug and reflectors bright and silly. Anjali's hair sticks out from
under the rug like a mop.

**HOLMES**
(grinning)
That rug has a lot of fluff. Fluff leaves a trail.

*She points. A trail of fluff leads back down the path toward the Hall. Case: the
rug-Hound is cleared.*

*Behind them, as the laughter dies, the glittering mist begins to roll in over the hills.
Golden. Pink. Soft.*

## ACT THREE: THE SHAPE IN THE MIST (8:00 to 11:00)

### EXT. TOR HILL. LATE AFTERNOON, TURNING TO DUSK.

*Warm gold light. The sun sinks toward the hills. Lanterns glow on the lookout rock.*

*Holmes, Watson and Henry sit together. The campers have drifted back to the Hall. Watson
is writing. Henry is turning the bottle cap over in his hand.*

**HENRY**
(quietly)
Back home, we'd watch the ferries come in at this time.

**WATSON**
Do you miss it?

**HENRY**
Yeah. But... it's pretty here, too.

*Holmes, busy holding up a wet finger to test the wind, doesn't look round.*

**HOLMES**
East wind. Good for howls.

*Watson smiles at Henry. DING-DING.*

*Then, from the Stone Circle across the valley, comes the long, wobbly sound.*

**SFX:** *"Woo-oo-oo-oooo!"* A little closer this time.

*The three look up. The mist is rising around the standing stones. Between two of them
stands a HUGE, SHAGGY SILHOUETTE. One ear flops down. One ear stands up. A long tail
wags. It lifts its great head... and sneezes. "ACHOO!" A cloud of golden sparkles puffs
out of the heather.*

**ANIMATOR:** The silhouette stays black-gold against the sunset and is never menacing: the
wagging tail and the floppy ear are always visible. The mist glitters. The sneeze comes
**within three seconds** of the first glimpse.

**⚠️ FLAG (first sight of Moony):** The only "scare" in Part 1. It's silhouetted, but it
wags, sneezes and flops. Cut to Holmes's grin immediately.

*Henry pulls his hat over his eyes. Peeks out.*

**HENRY**
(a whisper)
Is it... the Hound?

*Watson's bell goes DING (once).*

*Holmes slowly smiles. Her cap tilts.*

**HOLMES**
(delighted)
Curious... very curious!

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame around the silhouette.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

*The silhouette sneezes again, then turns and trots away between the stones, tail wagging.*

**FADE OUT.**

**TITLE CARD:** *PART ONE: THE END.*

*A very small "woo." A crayon doodle of a floppy-eared dog's tail pops onto Watson's journal
page, and the page turns.*

# PART 2: "THE HOUND"

## RECAP (11:00 to 12:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Warm fairy lights. Watson and Holmes sit on the floor with the journal open between them.
Henry watches from the doorway.*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Case journal, day two. So far, we have: one missing boot, one very big howl, and one very
silly rug.

*Doodles pop onto the page: the boot, a paw print, a rug.*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
And Clue Number One.

**[JOURNAL]** *The paw print doodle glows with a golden outline.*

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES**
Stamped prints. All the same. Paws don't do that.

**HENRY**
(a little brave)
So, someone made them?

**HOLMES**
(quickly)
Someone made them.

*She looks at Henry's hat. It is trembling.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Fascinating. Your hat is shaking.

**WATSON**
(gently)
Holmes. He's scared.

**HOLMES**
Oh! ...Are you?

(honest)

## ACT FOUR: THE HOWL (12:00 to 14:30)

### EXT. TOR HILL. SUNSET.

*Golden lanterns line the top of the Boardwalk. The three friends climb the ramp to the
lookout rock. Watson rolls easily; Henry ducks under painted flowers. Fireflies.*

*At the top, they stop. All is quiet.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Listen.

*They listen. Wind in the heather. A faraway bird. And then:*

**SFX:** *"Woo-oo-oo-oooo!"* ...with a **wobble** at the end. A tiny shaky note.

*Holmes's head snaps around.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Again.

**SFX:** *"Woo-oo-oo-oooo!"* ...wobble.

**WATSON**
(whispering)
Did you hear the end? It goes... *waaa-aa-aa.*

*He does the wobble with his voice.*

**HENRY**
(excited, then softer)
That sounds like... a harmonica!

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
A harmonica.

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. A glowing outline traces the sound waves.*

*She hunts around the top of the rock. In a bush, snagged on a thorn, is a small scrap of
bright green netting.*

*She holds it up.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
And this.

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st (the netting)]** *Chime. Sparkle. A glowing outline traces the scrap.*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Writing that down!

*He sketches the netting. Beside it: "A NET?" and a star.*

**HENRY**
Dogs don't carry nets.

**HOLMES**
(grinning)
No, they don't. Who carries a net?

*Cut to a fast flash of memory: Jasper's butterfly net over his shoulder, a small patch
missing at the tip. The flash lasts one second.*

**ANIMATOR:** This is a quick mental flash, not a reveal. Do not linger. The viewers get the
"aha," but the characters don't say Jasper's name yet.

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(hushed)
Look closer...

**WATSON**
Notice more...

**HENRY**
(trying)
Think it through?

*Holmes smiles. He's got it.*

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame, with three voices this time.*

## ACT FIVE: THE KITCHEN (14:30 to 16:30)

### INT. THE HALL, KITCHEN. EVENING.

*A big, warm kitchen: copper pots, garlic braids, a steaming pot. Grandpa and Bà Ngoại's
voices drift from offscreen, out of sight, as they clatter and sing. The twins are making
cheese toasties.*

**VOICE (O.S., BÀ NGOẠI)**
(calling)
Linh! Minh! More cheese!

**LINH & MINH**
(together, rolling eyes)
Coming!

*Holmes enters with a plate of sweet potato biscuits.*

**HOLMES**
A trade. One plate of sweet potato biscuits for information.

*The twins' eyes light up.*

**LINH**
What kind of information?

**HOLMES**
Anything unusual in the Hall.

**LINH**
(already talking)
Someone's been taking cheese every night!

**MINH**
Only a little.

**LINH**
A crust, a slice, a corner of toastie. Always at the same time.

**HOLMES**
(thinking)
Cheese. Crusts. Every night. And how do they get out?

*The twins look at each other.*

**LINH & MINH**
(together)
Third stair!

**MINH**
...squeaks.

**HOLMES**
Tell me about the squeak.

*Minh looks at the floor. He kneels and puts his palm flat on the boards.*

**MINH**
(thoughtful)
It's a little squeak. Not a big foot. A small foot.

**ANIMATOR:** Show the creak as a **ripple of gold light** along the floorboards, so every
viewer can "see" the sound. Minh's face as he feels it: calm, concentrated, expert.

**MINH (CONT'D)**
Soft shoes. Quick. They wear something floppy.

**HOLMES**
(delighted)
A small person. In a floppy coat. Who takes cheese.

*Holmes looks at Minh, properly.*

**HOLMES**
Thank you, Minh. You noticed something I couldn't.

*Minh blinks. Then grins.*

**MINH**
(quiet)
Thanks.

*DING-DING from offscreen: Watson's bell.*

## ACT SIX: THE DEN (16:30 to 18:30)

### EXT. BOARDWALK TRAIL, NEAR THE STONE CIRCLE. DUSK.

*The sun is low, a rich, pink-gold. Lantern flames bob. Fireflies. The mist glitters. Holmes,
Watson and Henry roll and walk quietly down the Boardwalk toward the standing stones. Beryl,
with her sketchbook, follows at a distance, unseen.*

*Holmes stops and points. At the side of the path, partly under a bush:*

*A trail of crumbs. A crust. A corner of cheese. A bit of toastie. Beside them: a single,
WOBBLY paw print in thick grey mud. It's big. It's smudged. It's a little off-centre.*

**HOLMES**
Look.

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. An outline traces the crumbs and the print.*

**HENRY**
(whispering)
It's not the same as the green ones.

**HOLMES**
(proud)
Exactly. These wobble. And look at the mud.

**WATSON**
It's grey.

**HOLMES**
Grey mud comes from only one place.

**HENRY & WATSON**
(together)
The Squelch!

**HOLMES**
The real paws have been in the Grimpen Squelch. These are real. The green ones are stamped.

**WATSON**
(writing)
Two kinds of prints.

**ANIMATOR:** Show the two print types side by side in a split-screen moment: neat green on
the left, wobbly grey on the right, each with a golden outline. This is the show's "teaching

**HENRY**
(a whisper)
So there's a real dog out there. And a fake dog. And one of them is somebody's idea.

**HOLMES**
(softly)
And the real one... is eating cheese.

*They follow the crumbs. The path bends between two big standing stones.*

### EXT. THE STONE CIRCLE. DUSK. CONTINUOUS.

*The Stone Circle: ancient, mossy standing stones in a ring, ringed with heather. A soft
golden glow. Fireflies. A cosy, lantern-lit den made of a draped blanket and a tarp under
the biggest stone. A faint HUM, four soft notes, over and over.*

*Holmes peeks around the stone.*

*Inside: a tiny boy in an enormous yellow raincoat, ZAID SELDEN (6), sits cross-legged
with a floppy stuffed rabbit, TUT, in his lap. He is humming. Beside him, curled up and
the size of a small pony, is a gigantic, shaggy, grey dog.*

*The dog has one ear up. One ear down. A tail as long as a broom, thumping on the ground. A
bright reflective collar that shines like a tiny moon. A half-eaten cheese toastie in his
paws. He is chewing noisily.*

**ANIMATOR:** The dog should be impossibly huge, impossibly fluffy and impossibly happy.
His eyebrows are enormous. He has a beard like a mop. He is NOT scary. He looks like a
marshmallow that has learned to wag.

*Zaid looks up. He sees Holmes. His eyes go wide. He pulls his hood down.*

**ZAID**
(whispering)
Please don't be scared.

*Holmes stands very still.*

**HOLMES**
(softly, for once)
I'm not scared.

*The dog lifts his huge head. He looks at Holmes. He tilts his head. He sneezes.*

**SFX:** *ACHOO!* A cloud of sparkles puffs out.

*Henry and Watson step carefully into view. Henry takes off his hat.*

**ZAID**
(very quietly, to Henry)
He's not scary.

*The dog's eyes fix on Henry. His tail begins to thump. THUMP. THUMP-THUMP. He stands. He
walks, slowly, to Henry, picks something off the ground...*

*...Henry's missing boot. Tied neatly with a bow made of heather.*

*The dog drops it at Henry's feet.*

**ZAID (CONT'D)**
(a tiny voice)
He's shy.

*Henry looks at the boot. At the dog. At the dog's face.*

**HENRY**
(wondering)
Why did you take my boot?

*The dog sniffs Henry. He sniffs the boot. He sniffs Henry again, and licks him on the

**HENRY (CONT'D)**
(sniffing the boot, laughing)
It smells like... Dad's pancakes! He took it because it smells like home!

*Zaid nods, relieved. Henry laughs a wet, happy laugh. He holds out his arms. The dog
leans into them, nearly knocking Henry over, tail wagging.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle. The outline traces a muddy grey paw print on the
dog's actual paw, then the crumbs on Zaid's pocket.*

**HOLMES**
(softly, to herself)
Clue three: the real dog. The real paws. The real friend.

*She notices Zaid pull his raincoat tight and look at the ground.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(slowly, finding the words)
You've been feeding him.

**ZAID**
(very small)
He was hungry. And lost. I... didn't know who to tell.

*Holmes kneels down so she's at his level. She stops being the detective for a moment.*

**HOLMES**
That was kind. You were kind.

*Zaid looks up, surprised. Almost smiles.*

**ZAID**
(whispering)
His name is Moony. Because of his eyes.

*The dog thumps his tail. MOONY lifts his chin, and out comes a small, wobbly, happy
"Woo-oo-oo!"*

**ANIMATOR:** This is the first time we hear the howl up close and with the wobble. It's a

**WATSON**
(wiping his eyes, smiling)
Writing that down!

## ACT SEVEN: THE REVEAL (18:30 to 20:00)

### EXT. GORSE CABIN, BACK STEPS. TWILIGHT.

*A quiet corner behind a small cabin. Lantern light. Jasper sits alone on the steps, his
clipboard beside him, polishing a small silver harmonica with his sleeve. Beryl comes
round the corner and sits next to him. She doesn't say anything for a moment.*

**BERYL**
Holmes found the prints. And the netting.

**JASPER**
(stiffly)
She can't prove anything. The meadow is closed. Rules are rules.

*He taps his clipboard twice.*

**BERYL**
(gently)
Actually... that's not quite true.

*Jasper looks at her.*

**BERYL (CONT'D)**
There's no rule that says you can't share it. You made that one up. I know. I've read the
Nature Club rules twice.

*Silence. Jasper looks at the harmonica.*

**BERYL (CONT'D)**
It's the loveliest thing you ever found. I want everybody to see it. And I don't want to
tell on you. So... would you tell them? I'll sit with you.

**JASPER**
(small)
What if they're angry?

**BERYL**
Then I'll still be sitting there.

*She takes his hand. A long breath. Jasper leaves his clipboard on the step, puts the
harmonica in his pocket, and stands.*

**ANIMATOR:** Keep this scene small and quiet: two kids, one lantern, soft gold light. The
clipboard left on the step is the visual beat: he is putting the rules down.

### EXT. CAMPFIRE CIRCLE. NIGHT.

*The fire crackles. The whole camp is gathered on the logs. Lanterns glow. Stars are coming
out, soft and sparkly. Moony is NOT there; Zaid and Henry are guarding him nearby, out of
sight.*

*Holmes stands in the centre of the circle, magnifier in hand. Watson sits beside her with
the journal open. Jasper and Beryl slip in at the back and stand together, hand in hand.*

**HOLMES**
(calm)
I have solved the mystery of the Hound.

*Gasps. Tavita almost drops his telescope.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Henry's boot was not taken by a monster. It was taken by a friend.

*Murmurs.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
But somebody else wanted you to be scared. And they used two tricks.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame circles Holmes.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Trick one: the green paw prints. Stamped. All the same.

*A glowing outline of a stamped print appears in the air.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Trick two: the howl. It wobbles like a harmonica. And a scrap of net was left on Tor Hill.

*A glowing outline of the netting. Heads begin to turn.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Whoever did this carries a net. And a clipboard. And a lot of rules. And that person is...

*Before she can finish, Jasper steps forward into the firelight, still holding Beryl's
hand. He takes the silver harmonica from his pocket and holds it up.*

**JASPER**
(clearly, though his voice shakes)
It was me.

*Holmes's mouth stays open. She lowers her finger.*

**HOLMES**
(small)
...I was about to say that.

**WATSON**
(softly)
Holmes.

**HOLMES**
Right. (to Jasper) Thank you for telling us.

*Jasper nods. He swallows.*

**JASPER**
(very quietly)
I found a Silverwing Skipper.

*Tavita whispers to Anjali.*

**TAVITA**
(not quietly)
What's a Silverwing Skipper?

**BERYL**
(softly)
It's a very rare butterfly. There are only a few left.

**JASPER**
(the words tumbling out)
It's in the meadow behind the Stone Circle. I found it first. I wanted to protect it. And I
wanted to win the Park Prize for the best discovery. I thought if people came, they'd step
on the flowers, or scare it away, or... take the credit. So I painted the paw prints. And I
played the harmonica from Tor Hill.

*He swallows.*

**JASPER (CONT'D)**
I didn't think it would scare anyone *that* much.

*The camp is quiet. Then Henry's voice, from the shadows, small but steady:*

**HENRY**
(stepping in, with Zaid)
Can I see it?

*Jasper looks up.*

**JASPER**
(surprised)
...What?

**HENRY**
The butterfly. I've never seen a rare one. Back home, we'd... go and look together.

*Jasper blinks. Then, very slowly, he nods.*

**ANIMATOR:** Nobody laughs. Nobody points. The camp's faces soften one by one. Anjali puts a
hand over her heart. Tavita lowers his telescope.

### EXT. THE MEADOW BEHIND THE STONE CIRCLE. TWILIGHT.

*The camp walks, slowly and in a line, along a lantern-lit path to a hidden meadow full of
small blue flowers. Fireflies. In the centre, on a flower, rests a small silver-winged
butterfly. It opens its wings. They shimmer, silver and gold.*

*Everyone gasps. A long, soft, shining moment.*

**BERYL**
(whispering)
It's called a Silverwing.

*Beryl opens her sketchbook and begins to draw. Zaid sits down beside her. Watson rolls
alongside, bell silent.*

**JASPER**
(very quietly)
It's... better with other people looking.

**ANJALI**
(softly, a hand on her heart)
Now that's a story.

*She turns to Jasper.*

**ANJALI (CONT'D)**
You could tell it at the campfire. The true one.

*Jasper looks at her. Looks at the butterfly.*

**JASPER**
(a small, shy smile)
I'd like that.

## "HOW HOLMES FIGURED IT OUT" (20:30 to 22:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Warm lights. Holmes, Watson and Henry sit on the floor around the journal. Moony's head
rests in Henry's lap. A very large, very sleepy, very contented dog.*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Case journal, day three. Case closed.

**HOLMES**
Let's look at the clues. Together.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame fills the screen.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

*Watson turns the page. The doodle of the paw print glows.*

**CLUE ONE: THE STAMPED PRINTS**

*The green paw prints replay: neat, even, sparkling, with a golden outline.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
Real paws wobble. These didn't. Same size. Same spacing. They were stamped. Somebody wanted us
to think the Hound was near.

*The doodle gets a gold star.*

**CLUE TWO: THE WOBBLE AND THE NET**

*The howl replays as a wiggly golden line. The netting scrap appears beside it.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
The howl wobbled at the end, like a harmonica. And there was a scrap of net on Tor Hill. Dogs
don't carry nets. Nature Club presidents do.

*A doodle of Jasper's net gets a star.*

**CLUE THREE: THE CRUMBS AND THE GREY MUD**

*The wobbly grey print and the trail of crumbs replay.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
Wobbly prints. Grey mud from the Squelch. And crumbs from a sandwich. That's not a monster.
That's a hungry dog. And a kind friend who was feeding him.

*A doodle of Zaid and Moony gets a star, and then a heart.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(turning to Henry)
And one more thing.

**HENRY**
What?

**HOLMES**
(counting on her fingers)
Watson noticed you were sad on day one. I noticed on day... three. I'm sorry I was slow.

**HENRY**
(smiling)
That's okay. You got there.

**HOLMES**
(grinning)
Curious... very curious!

*Moony lifts his head and thumps his tail.*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Case closed. Hound cleared.

*He draws a heart in the margin of the page. DING-DING-DING goes his bell: three times, for
"solved it."*

### EXT. THE HALL, ROOF. NIGHT. A FINAL GAG.

*Tavita on the roof ridge, telescope in hand, sees Moony's big silhouette crossing the lawn.*

**TAVITA**
(whispering)
It's the Hound!

*He lowers the telescope.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
And that's Moony. And that's fine.

*A tiny, wobbly "woo" drifts across the garden. The telescope, forgotten, tips backwards.
Tavita falls off the roof ridge into a hay bale. WHUMP. He pops up, laughing.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
Never mind!

**SMASH CUT TO: END CARD.** *"HOLMES & WATSON: THE HOUND OF CAMP BASKERVILLE." A small
harmonica phrase. A single tiny wag of a tail.*

**THE END.**
