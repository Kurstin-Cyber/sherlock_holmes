# EPISODE 13: "CAMP DAY"

*Season finale · 11 minutes · Script draft 1 · Stage 4*

**Spotlight:** Zaid, Anjali and the whole camp · **Lesson:** Teamwork (everyone has a job, and the day works because of all of them)
**Clues:** (1) a crayon note that smells of za'atar and has a yellow thread on it · (2) a trail of cheese crumbs that stops at the stage · (3) a faint, wobbly "woo" and a thumping wag from under the stage
**Red herring:** Tavita's telescope "finds" Moony in the bunting (a very shaggy mop in a bucket)
**Setting:** the Hall lawn, the stage, the dining room, the Boardwalk

*Script key, look reminders and flags work as in `ep01-the-hound-comes-home.md`.*

**Optional thread:** The dining-room chalkboard reads **"CAMP DAY: TODAY!"** (A banner, bunting and a
big paper rosette on the Hall door say the same.)

**Callbacks for fans (never needed):** Watson's lantern kite flies above the lawn; Beryl's paintings
of Anjali's stories hang on a washing line; the sun-catcher ferry glints in a window; Jasper's
moth sheet is on show; Duchess grazes by the gate. A first-time viewer sees only a lovely party.

**Tone rule for this episode:** It is a celebration. The only worry is gentle: a big dog is scared of the
music and the visitors must not notice. The camp solves it with cleverness, not panic.

## COLD OPEN (0:00 to 1:00)

### EXT. THE HALL, LAWN. MORNING.

*A sunny, spectacular summer morning. The Hall is covered in bunting and paper lanterns. A big
hand-painted banner flies over the door: "CAMP DAY!" On the lawn: a stage made of crates
and planks, a long table of food, stalls with hand-lettered signs, a clothes line hung with
watercolour paintings, and a lantern kite bobbing high above.*

*Campers dash about in their best hats. Music from a gramophone trumpet blares, bright and jolly.
Dozens of visitors in matching orange caps (the kids from BRAMBLEDOWN CAMP) are crossing the
lawn behind a boy with a flag.*

*ANJALI MORTIMER (cape, lantern, curls, a new gold sash) stands on the stage with a megaphone.*

**ANJALI**
(booming, quick)
Welcome to Camp Day! Remember! The Hound is big, he's shaggy, and he wants a snack!

*Some of the Brambledown kids giggle. Some of the Camp Baskerville campers laugh. Under the
long table, a few feet from the sandwich platter, a shaggy tail pokes out. It thumps.*

*It stops thumping.*

*The music pumps up, brassy and loud: a tuba, a triangle and a drum. The tail goes very still.
Then, quick as a flash, it whisks out of sight.*

**ANIMATOR:** Keep this sly: a tail under a table, then gone. The music is the cause, shown in a
moment.

*A small, wobbly sound drifts out from somewhere. It's very faint, and no one but the audience
hears it.*

**SFX:** *"Wh... woo?"*

*TAVITA FRANKLAND (sun hat, telescope) swings round.*

**TAVITA**
(a delighted whisper)
It's the Hound!

*Several Brambledown kids look at him with interest. One gasps.*

**BRAMBLEDOWN KID**
(wide-eyed)
Is there a Hound?

*Tavita freezes, and then slowly lowers the telescope.*

**TAVITA**
(a brave grin)
...No. It's a bush. Never mind.

**CUT TO: TITLE CARD.** *Golden magnifier swirls. A tiny wobbly harmonica "woo."*

## ACT ONE: THE CASE ARRIVES (1:00 to 4:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. MORNING.

*Fairy lights, the wall chart, a pile of Camp Day ribbons on the desk. KURSTIN HOLMES (cap with flaps
tied up, golden-blonde loc buns poking out, glasses, raincoat, a gold Camp Day rosette pinned on
top) is dressing her cap with extra care. MATEO WATSON (teal hoodie with his own rosette,
orange chair decorated with ribbons) is at his desk, writing a very long list.*

*A faint SCRAPE. A piece of paper slides slowly under the door.*

*Holmes and Watson look at each other. Holmes tiptoes to the door and flings it open. The
corridor is empty. Only a fading patter of small footsteps.*

**HOLMES**
(delighted)
A mystery!

*She snatches up the paper. It's a torn page of coloured paper, folded in four. On the front,
in wobbly yellow crayon: "FOR HOLMES."*

*She unfolds it. Her magnifier goes up. The two friends lean over it.*

**NOTE (close-up):** *"MOONY IS GONE. PLEASE HELP. DON'T TELL."*

*Beneath the words, a tiny drawing of a very large dog with one ear up and one ear down, and a
tear on his cheek.*

**WATSON**
(softly)
Oh no.

**HOLMES**
(sharper, grave)
Moony is missing. On Camp Day. With visitors.

*She turns the note over. She sniffs it. A faint golden-green ribbon of "smell" curls up, with
tiny flatbread-and-herb icons dancing in it.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Sesame. Thyme. Something... lemony.

**WATSON**
(writing)
Za'atar.

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the crayon words, the
golden-green smell-ribbon and a short loop of yellow thread caught in the paper's fold.*

*Holmes plucks the thread out between two fingers and holds it up to the light.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
A yellow thread. From a coat, or a rug, or a sock.

**WATSON**
(writing, softly)
Yellow thread. Za'atar.

*He draws the thread, the note and the herbs. A star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

**HOLMES**
(tapping her magnifier twice)
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles the note.*

*Holmes looks up. Her face is serious and kind.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Whoever wrote this didn't want to be seen. They're scared. And they've asked for help in the
only way they could.

**WATSON**
(gently)
Then let's help.

### EXT. THE HALL, LAWN. MORNING.

*The lawn is full of music, laughter and colour. Holmes and Watson push through the crowd. At
a corner of the stage, HENRY BASKERVILLE (plaid, star-stickered hat, a rosette) hurries up.*

**HENRY**
(whispering urgently)
Holmes. Moony's gone from the boot room. The basket's empty.

**HOLMES**
(quickly)
Who else knows?

**HENRY**
Just us. And Zaid.

*All three look round. There is no sign of a small yellow raincoat.*

**WATSON**
(thinking aloud)
We have to find Moony before anyone sees him.

**HOLMES**
(nodding)
And before the visitors notice a dog the size of a pony.

*A cheerful voice cuts in.*

**BRAMBLEDOWN KID**
(brightly, a tall boy with a flag)
Excuse me! Is it true there's a Hound?

*Holmes, Watson and Henry freeze. The boy is smiling, openly curious.*

**HOLMES**
(brightly)
It's a legend.

**BRAMBLEDOWN KID**
(thrilled)
Can we see him?

**WATSON**
(as casually as he can)
...Not today.

*The boy looks round, blinks, and wanders off. The three friends let out a breath.*

**HOLMES**
(low)
Right. Everyone. We need a plan.

## ACT TWO: THE INVESTIGATION (4:00 to 8:00)

### EXT. THE HALL, LAWN. MORNING.

*Holmes, Watson and Henry have gathered the whole team behind the long food table, quietly, in a
huddle: Anjali, Tavita, Linh, Minh, Jasper, Beryl. A tablecloth hangs between them and the crowd.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Moony's missing. A note says so. We have to find him and keep him hidden.

**ANJALI**
(whispering, scandalised)
The Hound! On Camp Day!

**LINH**
(whispering)
We heard a woo this morning.

**MINH**
(signing, then whispering)
From the stage.

**SUBTITLE:** *From the stage.*

**JASPER**
(clipboard clutched, whispering)
Then the stage is the first place to look. Rules are rules: we search in pairs.

**HOLMES**
(gratefully)
Thank you, Jasper.

*Jasper stands a little straighter.*

**TAVITA**
(whispering, much too loud)
I'LL SEARCH WITH MY TELESCOPE!

**EVERYONE**
(whispering)
Shhh!

*Tavita squeezes his telescope to his chest and whispers, tiny.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
...I'll search with my telescope.

### EXT. THE HALL, LAWN. A MOMENT LATER.

*Tavita strides across the lawn, telescope raised, scanning. He stops. His eyes go wide.*

*Tied to the washing line, above the paintings, among the bunting and paper lanterns, hangs a
huge, shaggy, grey shape. Two floppy "ears" hang down from the top. A long mop-head of grey
strands swings gently in the breeze.*

**TAVITA**
(a gasp)
I SEE HIM!

*Every head on the lawn turns. Tavita has just shouted. Several Brambledown kids look up at the
washing line.*

*Holmes sprints in, grabs Tavita's arm, and drags him into the shadow of a stall.*

**HOLMES**
(hissing)
Tavita!

**TAVITA**
(wide-eyed, whispering)
He's been tied up in the bunting!

**HOLMES**
(looking up)
He's been what?

*They both look at the shaggy shape. It sways in the breeze. A drip falls from one end of it.
It is a very large, very grey, very wet mop, hung up to dry.*

*A tiny pause. Holmes turns to Tavita.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(gently)
It's the Hall's mop. Linh washed it this morning.

**TAVITA**
(slowly)
...Oh. Never mind.

*Holmes squeezes his shoulder. She doesn't laugh. She smiles, and then so does he.*

**HOLMES**
(quietly)
Good eyes. Wrong dog.

*Tavita nods, bravely cheerful, and tiptoes away to search elsewhere.*

**ANIMATOR:** This is the red herring. It is funny, quickly cleared, and Holmes is kind about it.
No one laughs at Tavita; the beat shows what the last episode taught.

### EXT. THE HALL, LAWN. NEAR THE STAGE. LATER.

*Holmes and Watson crouch behind the stage. The ground is bare earth, with a few scattered
crumbs.*

*Holmes drops to her knees. She sweeps her magnifier slowly along the ground.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Crumbs.

*A trail of cheese crumbs, a bit of crust and a flake of toastie leads across the grass,
neat as a path. It runs straight to the bottom edge of the stage, a wooden platform on short legs,
draped with a bright cloth. The trail stops at the cloth.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the crumb trail, from the
back door of the Hall across the lawn to the stage.*

**WATSON**
(rolling up, writing)
Cheese. All the way to the stage.

*He draws the trail of crumbs and the stage, with a tiny star at the end.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

*From above them, on the stage, the brass band music rises. A drum thumps. A tuba blares. The
audience claps along.*

*Holmes and Watson both freeze. Underneath the music, from somewhere beneath the platform, comes
a small, wobbly, miserable sound.*

**SFX:** *"...woo?"*

*A pause. Then, very softly, from the same darkness: a slow, nervous THUMP. THUMP. THUMP.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces golden sound ripples
drifting out from beneath the stage, and a rhythmic thumping.*

**WATSON**
(whispering)
That's a tail.

**HOLMES**
(whispering back)
That's Moony.

*They look at each other with enormous relief, and then dismay.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(whispering)
He's under the stage. And the stage has a brass band on it.

**WATSON**
(whispering)
He must be terrified.

**HOLMES**
(a determined whisper)
We have to get him out. Without anyone seeing.

*She raises her magnifier. Her expression is clear.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles the stage, and the dark gap beneath it.*

## ACT THREE: THE REVEAL (8:00 to 9:30)

### EXT. THE HALL, LAWN. BEHIND THE STAGE. CONTINUOUS.

*Holmes is on her stomach at the edge of the cloth. She lifts it an inch. A pair of very large,
very frightened brown eyes look back out of the darkness. One floppy ear is flat. Moony.*

*Beside him, curled up like a small yellow snail, with Tut clutched in both arms, is ZAID
SELDEN. His eyes are red. His thumb is in his mouth.*

**HOLMES**
(softly)
Zaid.

*Zaid takes his thumb out.*

**ZAID**
(a tiny voice)
It was loud. And there were so many people. He got scared and ran.

*Moony pokes his nose out and gives Holmes a gentle, shaky lick. His tail thumps weakly.*

**WATSON**
(rolling up, softly)
You wrote the note.

**ZAID**
(wiping his nose)
I put it under your door. I didn't know who else.

**HOLMES**
(gently)
That was a good idea.

**ZAID**
(whispering)
I didn't want the Brambledown kids to see him. They might tell. Or take him. Or... be scared.

*Holmes lies down next to Zaid, on her stomach, eye to eye.*

**HOLMES**
(quietly)
You did the right thing, Zaid. You asked.

**ZAID**
(a wobbly almost-smile)
It's still hard.

**HOLMES**
(a small smile)
I know.

*She turns to Watson.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(quickly, in plan mode)
Three clues. Clue one: the note, with the za'atar smell and the yellow thread from Zaid's
raincoat. Clue two: the crumb trail from the back door to the stage. Clue three: the "woo" and
the thump under the stage.

**WATSON**
(writing, DING-DING-DING)
Writing that down!

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(turning to Zaid)
Moony is scared of the music. And we need to get him out while a hundred people are watching.
It's too big a job for one detective.

*She turns to the huddle gathered at the end of the stage. Her face is bright.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(in a firm, quiet voice)
I need everyone.

## ACT FOUR: THE PLAN (9:30)

### EXT. THE HALL, LAWN. MORNING (MONTAGE).

*A rhythmic, funny, warm team montage, to a bouncy tune. Each job is clear and short:*

*1. THE TWINS: Linh and Minh slip into the "sound corner" by the gramophone. They turn the
volume knob. The brass band quiets from BLARE to a gentle oom-pah. Minh gives a thumbs-up.*

*2. ANJALI: She climbs onto the stage with her megaphone. "Welcome, one and all, to a tale so
long it takes an hour to begin!" The Brambledown kids settle on the grass to listen, rapt. She
launches into a story. The squirrel gets bigger every sentence. Beryl, beside her, quietly
holds up a painting of a MEDIUM squirrel, gently correcting. Anjali sighs and shrinks the
squirrel.*

*3. WATSON: He wheels to the front of the Brambledown group, bell ringing. "Boardwalk tour! This
way to the Stone Circle and Tor Hill! Follow the bell!" The visitors follow, charmed, away from
the stage.*

*4. JASPER: He stands at the edge of the lawn with his harmonica. "If anyone hears a woo," he
tells the others, "it's me." He plays a slow, wobbly phrase, the same wobble as the pilot's
fake howl, and the visitors cheer, thinking it's part of the show.*

*5. TAVITA: He stands at the door of the Hall with his telescope, scanning the horizon for
Brambledown stragglers. He is completely, utterly in his element. "NOBODY GETS PAST ME!"
Someone gets past him. He doesn't notice.*

*6. HENRY AND ZAID: Behind the stage, they lift the cloth. Henry kneels, holding a plate of
cheese in one hand and his bobble hat in the other. Zaid whispers "Come on, Moony..." into
the darkness.*

*7. HOLMES: She stands in the middle of the lawn, calmly watching all of it, conducting with
her magnifier. Every few seconds she looks at her watch and gives a small nod. A cap flap
wiggles.*

**ANIMATOR:** The montage should feel like a heist film for five-year-olds: cross-cuts, thumbs-up,
a secret signal. Every kid's job uses their trait: twins (hearing the music), Anjali
(stories), Watson (tours and the bell), Jasper (the harmonica), Tavita (lookout), Henry and
Zaid (gentleness), Holmes (planning). Keep it under 90 seconds.

*Behind the stage, a huge shaggy head ducks out from under the cloth. MOONY crawls out, belly low,
ears flat. He looks at Zaid. He looks at Henry. He looks at the plate of cheese.*

*Zaid takes his paw. Henry offers a cheese cube. Moony sniffs it, takes it politely, and licks
Henry's hand. His tail begins to wag. He stands.*

*The music is soft. The voices are far away. The coast is clear.*

*Zaid and Henry walk Moony, very slowly, along the back wall of the Hall toward the boot-room
door, a giant dog and two kids, tiptoeing together in a line.*

*As they reach the door, Moony pauses. He lifts his head. He lets out a long, wobbly, happy
"wooooo!"*

*On the lawn, Jasper's harmonica plays the same note at exactly the same moment. The crowd
cheers. Nobody looks round.*

*Behind the door, the three of them let out a breath. Zaid looks at Henry. Henry looks at
Zaid. They both burst into silent giggles.*

---

### EXT. THE HALL, LAWN. AFTERNOON.

*The stage is empty. A golden afternoon. Camp Day is in full, joyful swing: stalls, games, paper
lanterns, laughter. The lantern kite bobs overhead. Duchess grazes by the gate.*

*Anjali, Beryl, Jasper and Watson stand together on a cheerful platform. In front of them a
crowd of campers and visitors has gathered. Jasper holds a small golden acorn on a ribbon.*

**JASPER**
(clearing his throat, pink)
The Park Prize for Best Discovery goes this year to... everyone.

*A cheer goes up.*

**JASPER (CONT'D)**
For the Silverwing Skipper. For the kite. For the sun-catcher, the moth sheet, the squirrel
paintings and... (he glances at a boot room window) ...a few other things we found.

*In the boot-room window, a large, shaggy face appears behind a curtain, wearing a Camp Day
bandana. Zaid's hand pats his head. Tut waves.*

*Only the audience, and Zaid, Henry and Holmes, notice.*

**HOLMES**
(in the crowd, whispering to Watson)
He's going to be fine.

**WATSON**
(smiling)
We all are.

## "HOW HOLMES FIGURED IT OUT" (9:30 to 11:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. EVENING.

*Fairy lights. The wall chart. A pile of Camp Day ribbons. Holmes and Watson on the floor with the
journal. Through the window, paper lanterns bob in the dusk. Zaid sits on the
edge of the bed, Tut in his lap. He's been invited. He is smiling.*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Case journal, day eighty-six. Case closed. Moony was scared of the music, and the whole camp
helped.

**HOLMES**
Let's look at the clues.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame fills the screen.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**CLUE ONE: THE NOTE**

*The crayon note replays, with its za'atar smell-ribbon and the loop of yellow thread.*

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A note in crayon, with a smell of za'atar and a yellow thread. Za'atar is Zaid's favourite. The
yellow thread was from his raincoat. Someone who loves Moony wrote it.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE TWO: THE CRUMBS**

*The crumb trail replays, from the Hall's back door to the stage.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A trail of cheese crumbs from the Hall's back door to the edge of the stage. The crumbs went
one way: toward somewhere safe. Moony was following the cheese.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE THREE: THE WOO AND THE THUMP**

*The golden ripples and the thump replay, from under the stage.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A tiny "woo" and a slow thump, thump, thump under the stage. A scared Hound, with a wagging
tail.

*A gold star, a heart, and a doodle of a whole camp holding hands round a stage.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(turning to Zaid)
You asked.

**ZAID**
(quietly, bravely)
I asked.

**HOLMES**
(gently)
And everyone came.

*Zaid's whole face lights up. Tut nods.*

**WATSON**
(writing, with a flourish)
"A CAMP IS A TEAM." (He draws a small heart, and then, slowly, a large one.) "A HOUND IS A
FRIEND."

*DING-DING-DING: three dings for "solved."*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
Case closed. Hound cleared.

*Holmes steps up to the wall chart. She draws a very large star at the top. Beneath it she writes,
in her neatest hand: "THINGS THAT GO BUMP: US."*

**HOLMES**
Curious... very curious!

### EXT. THE HALL, EAST TERRACE. NIGHT. (FINAL BUTTON)

*Dusk. Lanterns glow across the lawn far below. The campers sing a sleepy song. The big paper
rosette on the Hall door flutters. Far above, the lantern kite floats among the first stars.*

*On the stone terrace, behind the thick railing, TAVITA sits on the floor with his back against the
wall, his telescope and its gold star across his knees. Beside him sits Moony, in his Camp Day
bandana, head resting on Tavita's shoulder. A very large, very shaggy, very happy Hound.*

**TAVITA**
(whispering)
I see it.

*Moony looks where Tavita is looking. The first star.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
(whispering)
...Do you see it too?

**MOONY**
(very softly)
Woo.

*Tavita nods, deeply satisfied, and leans against him.*

**ANIMATOR:** Hold the shot for five seconds. Sparkling stars, a boy and a dog. Gentle harmonica.
This is the series' last image.

*In the garden below, the camp hums its song. A single paper lantern drifts up into the sky.*

**THE END.**

## Complexity and logic check

- This is a mystery about **where**, not **who**. The solution (Moony is under the stage, scared by
  the music) is easy; the real puzzle is how the camp gets him out in secret.
- The teamwork montage shows each kid's trait doing the job. Every character gets a clear action.
- The reveal is gentle and emotional. For ages 5 to 8 the "heist" is the exciting part.

## Teamwork beat map (lesson shown, not told)

| Character | Job | Trait used |
|---|---|---|
| Holmes | Plans and signals | Organising, observing |
| Watson | Leads the Boardwalk tour by bell | Warm, trusted by strangers |
| Henry | Gently coaxes Moony with cheese | Kind, steady, tall |
| Zaid | Holds Moony's paw | The one Moony trusts most |
| Anjali | Tells a very long story | Storytelling |
| Beryl | Holds up the "medium squirrel" painting | Honest, artistic |
| Jasper | Covers the "woo" with his harmonica | Redemption of the pilot's fake howl |
| Linh and Minh | Turn the music down | Know the Hall and its sounds |
| Tavita | Lookout at the door | Telescope, loyalty |

## Flag summary

| Flag | Where | Fix built in |
|---|---|---|

| Zaid distressed | Act Three | Short; he is comforted straight away; thumb-sucking is gentle, not a joke |

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **This is the weakest mystery of the season,** by design: the puzzle is simple, and the episode is
   a celebration. If you want a sharper puzzle, the best lever is to make the note's author
   unknown until the reveal (right now the yellow thread and the za'atar point plainly at Zaid).
2. **Brambledown Camp** is a new, unnamed group of visitors. They are friendly and curious, and
   one tall boy asks about the Hound twice. Do you want any of them to become a recurring
   character or a later episode?
3. **Jasper's harmonica cover** pays off the pilot's fake howl. It's a lovely callback, but it
   needs a first-time viewer to understand "he plays the wobble to hide the woo." I've kept it
   visual: Moony woos, the harmonica plays, the crowd cheers.
4. **The Park Prize** is mentioned in passing, as promised in the pilot. Its golden acorn is a
   new prop.
5. **The final image** is Tavita and Moony on the East Terrace (an established safe space with a
   thick stone railing), not on the roof. I moved it there after weighing the risk of a boy and a big
   dog on a roof ridge.
6. **"A Hound is a friend"** in Watson's last journal line is the series' closing thought. OK?
7. **Season-end wrap.** I haven't added a "see you next summer" card. Do you want one?
8. **Loud music** is a new fear for Moony (squirrels, wind, hats, ducks, his own shadow, and now
   a brass band). Add to his profile?
