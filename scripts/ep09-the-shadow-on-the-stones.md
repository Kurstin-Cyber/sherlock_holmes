# EPISODE 9: "THE SHADOW ON THE STONES"

*11 minutes · Script draft 1 · Stage 4*

**Spotlight:** Holmes, Anjali and Tavita · **Lesson:** Curiosity (look at things from another angle)
**Clues:** (1) the shadow-hound appears only at sunset, when the sun is low · (2) the shadow moves when the kids move · (3) its pieces match the kids: pointy ears, a long snout, a shaggy mane
**Red herring:** Duchess the sheep, wandering among the stones
**Setting:** the Stone Circle at golden hour

*Script key, look reminders and flags work as in `ep01-the-hound-comes-home.md`.*

**Optional thread:** The dining-room chalkboard reads **"CAMP DAY: 20 DAYS TO GO."**

**Light rule for this episode:** the sun is always warm, low and golden. The "shadow-hound" is
soft-edged and gold-brown, not black. It wags, flaps and wobbles. It is silly from its first second.

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00 to 1:00)

### EXT. THE STONE CIRCLE. SUNSET.

*A ring of tall, mossy standing stones on a hill of purple heather. The sun is low and huge,
rose-gold, just above the horizon. Every stone throws a long, soft, golden-brown shadow across
the grass.*

*A cluster of campers sits cross-legged in the heather, sketching, eating cheese toasties and
watching the sunset. ANJALI MORTIMER (cape, lantern, curls) stands on a flat stone like a
conductor.*

**ANJALI**
(booming, quick)
Remember! The Hound is big, he's shaggy, and he wants a snack!

**CAMPERS**
(together, mid-chew)
...WANTS A SNACK!

*Behind her, on the biggest stone in the circle, something is happening to the shadows.*

*They stretch. They gather. They join together, long and soft and gold, into a single
enormous shape: a hound. Pointed ears. A long, snuffling snout. A great shaggy mane that waves
in the breeze.*

*The giant shadow-hound lowers its head and sniffs the ground.*

**ANIMATOR:** The shadow is huge, golden-brown and soft-edged, with comically floppy
movements. It should look like a shadow puppet, not a monster. Keep the sunset warm.

*The campers gasp, silent. A toastie drops. HENRY BASKERVILLE (plaid, star-stickered hat)
pulls his hat over his eyes and peeks.*

**TAVITA FRANKLAND**
(not a whisper, hat flapping, telescope raised)
It's the Hound!

*He swings the telescope towards the shadow. The shadow swings its nose to match. Tavita
freezes. The shadow freezes. Tavita blinks. The shadow blinks. Slowly, Tavita lowers the
telescope. The shadow's snout lowers too.*

*A long, strange beat.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
(a tiny voice)
He copied me.

**CUT TO: TITLE CARD.** *Golden magnifier swirls. A tiny wobbly harmonica "woo."*

## ACT ONE: THE CASE ARRIVES (1:00 to 4:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. EVENING.

*Fairy lights. The wall chart. IMANI HOLMES (cap, glasses, raincoat) is pacing, magnifier raised.
MATEO WATSON is at his desk, drawing the stone circle with a long, golden shadow stretched over it.
HENRY sits on the floor. ANJALI is sitting upside down on the beanbag, her cape draped over her
face, narrating with her hands.*

**ANJALI**
(muffled by cape)
It was ENORMOUS. It had EARS like a dragon. And a NOSE like a trumpet. And it looked right AT me!

**HOLMES**
(not looking up)
Describe the ears.

**ANJALI**
(muffled)
Pointy.

**HOLMES**
Left and right?

**ANJALI**
(sitting up, cape sliding off)
One on each side.

**HOLMES**
(delighted)
Observation!

*She makes a note.*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Case journal, day sixty-six. A shadow that looks like a hound.

*Holmes strides to the wall chart and adds a bold line: "SHADOWS." She underlines it twice.*

**HOLMES**
Three nights now. Every night at sunset. Same stones. Same shape.

**HENRY**
(a small voice)
Back home, we'd go and look at it in the day.

**HOLMES**
(snapping her fingers)
Henry! Brilliant!

*She whirls round to Watson.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Did you do the noon visit?

**WATSON**
(turning a page)
I did.

*He holds up his journal. On the page, a drawing of the stone circle at midday: every stone has
a short, stubby shadow. No hound.*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
At noon, the stones have tiny shadows. No hound.

**HOLMES**
(thrilled)
So the shadow-hound only appears when the sun is low!

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the short noon shadows and
the long sunset shadows side by side.*

**HENRY**
(thinking)
Do hounds come out at sunset?

**ANJALI**
(sitting bolt upright)
HOUNDS DO, IN THE BEST STORIES.

**HOLMES**
(drily)
But shadows do in all of them.

*She taps her magnifier twice.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles the page.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle: a noon sun and a sunset sun, each over the stones, with their
shadows. A star.*

## ACT TWO: THE INVESTIGATION (4:00 to 8:00)

### EXT. THE STONE CIRCLE. LATE AFTERNOON.

*The sun is lower than at noon, higher than at sunset. The shadows are medium-length. Holmes,
Watson, Henry, Anjali and Tavita arrive along the Boardwalk, the golden light warm on their
faces. Watson rolls up the final ramp onto the flat grass, where the Boardwalk ends in a circle.*

*Holmes strides among the stones with her magnifier.*

**HOLMES**
We watch. We note. We wait.

*She assigns places.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Watson, journal. Henry, lantern. Anjali, voice. Tavita, telescope.

**TAVITA**
(proudly)
I'M THE TELESCOPE.

*They settle. A soft sound makes everyone look up.*

**SFX:** *Baa.*

*A big, fat, fluffy white sheep ambles into the Stone Circle, chewing a mouthful of heather.
She is as round as a barrel, with a wobbly bottom and a very serious face. She plods straight to
the centre of the stones and sits down with a thump.*

**TAVITA**
(a loud whisper)
THE HOUND IN DISGUISE.

**ANJALI**
(whispering back)
He's wearing wool!

*The sheep looks at them. She chews. She looks completely unbothered.*

**HOLMES**
(carefully)
That is a sheep.

**TAVITA**
(sadly)
Are you sure?

**HOLMES**
She is wearing a collar with a bell and a name tag. (reading) "DUCHESS."

*Duchess lifts her head, regards them for a moment with great dignity, and says:*

**DUCHESS**
Baa.

**LINH (O.S.)**
(from the path, laughing)
She lives behind the kitchen! She comes for the carrot peelings!

*LINH and MINH BARRYMORE (matching aprons) wander up the Boardwalk, each carrying a bucket.*

**MINH**
(signing, then aloud)
She's always wandering.

**SUBTITLE:** *She's always wandering.*

**TAVITA**
(bowing to Duchess, deadly serious)
...Oh. Never mind.

*Everyone laughs. Duchess takes a bite of heather, utterly pleased.*

**ANIMATOR:** Duchess is a recurring, lovable background animal. She is a very round, calm
sheep who is always slightly in the way. She will return in episode 10.

### EXT. THE STONE CIRCLE. SUNSET.

*The sun sinks, large and rose-gold. The light has turned warm orange. The stones stretch their
long gold shadows across the grass.*

*The team has taken up positions. Watson is parked beside the tallest stone with his journal.
Holmes is in the middle with her magnifier. Henry holds a lantern, Anjali stands with her cape
spread, and Tavita crouches in the heather with his telescope.*

*They wait. Nothing happens. Then, slowly, the shadows begin to join.*

*A pointy ear rises first. A second ear. A long, wobbling snout. A great shaggy mane. The
shadow-hound stands on the ground and stretches.*

**HENRY**
(a squeak)
There he is!

**TAVITA**
(whispering)
He's looking at me again.

*Tavita carefully moves his telescope up. The shadow's snout rises.*

*He moves it down. The snout falls.*

*He swings it left. The snout swings left.*

*Tavita stares.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
(a tiny voice)
He's doing what I do.

**HOLMES**
(sharply)
Do it again!

*Tavita raises the telescope. The snout rises. He lowers it. The snout lowers.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the telescope and the snout
rising and falling together.*

**WATSON**
(writing, wide-eyed)
When Tavita moves, the snout moves.

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
It's not looking at him. It's *copying* him.

*Watson draws the doodle: a boy, a telescope, a long gold shadow-snout. A star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

*Holmes stands very still. She is thinking. She looks at the shadow-hound's pointed ears. She
looks at the shaggy mane. She looks at the long snout. She looks around the Stone Circle, at
her friends. Her eyes flick from one to the next.*

*Holmes takes one step to the left. The shadow-hound's left ear turns.*

*She steps right. The ear turns back.*

*Her face goes very, very still.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
Look closer.

**WATSON**
Notice more.

**HENRY**
(trying)
Think it through?

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles each of the friends in turn, and settles on the
shadow.*

## ACT THREE: THE REVEAL (8:00 to 9:30)

### EXT. THE STONE CIRCLE. CONTINUOUS.

*Holmes steps out of the way, lifts both hands, and wiggles her fingers beside her cap. The
shadow-hound's two pointed ears wiggle.*

*She stops. The ears stop. She looks at her own shadow. She looks at the shadow-hound's
head.*

**HOLMES**
(a gasp)
They're my puffs.

*She yanks her cap off. Her two round hair puffs are sticking out on each side of her head.
The ears on the shadow are two round puffs too.*

*A tiny pause. The shadow-hound's ears have gone round.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(wondering)
I'm the ears.

**TAVITA**
(delighted)
I'M THE NOSE!

*He raises his telescope. The snout stretches.*

**ANJALI**
(gasping, clutching her cape)
Then I'm...

*She shakes her head. Her enormous curls toss. The shadow-hound's mane waves wildly.*

**ANJALI (CONT'D)**
(awed)
I'M THE MANE.

**HENRY**
(slowly, looking at his own long arm)
Then... I'm the tail.

*He swings his arm behind him, up and down, like a rope. The shadow-hound's tail wags.*

*A silence, full of astonishment. Then Watson laughs, a big warm laugh, DING-DING-DING.*

**WATSON**
(wiping his eyes)
It's us. It's all of us. We made a hound.

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces each person's shadow-part
in turn: puffs, telescope, curls, swinging arm.*

**ANIMATOR:** This is the moment of delight. Cut quickly between each kid and their matching
part of the shadow-hound, then wide to show all five kids, one big golden hound. Warm
orchestral swell.

**HOLMES**
(shaking her head, grinning)
We were sitting inside the mystery.

**WATSON**
(writing)
Writing that down!

*He sketches the five kids, with the shadow-hound's parts in matching colours. A star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(excited, to everyone)
Three clues! One: the shadow comes at sunset, when the sun is low and shadows are long.
Two: it moves when we move. Three: its parts match us.

**HENRY**
(delighted)
It's a Hound made of friends!

**TAVITA**
(bowing)
I'M THE BEST PART.

**ANJALI**
(gasping)
I want to tell this story forever.

## ACT FOUR: THE LESSON (9:30)

### EXT. THE STONE CIRCLE. DUSK.

*The group is in a line, facing the sunset, as the sky turns from rose-gold to violet. They are
making shadow animals on the biggest stone: a rabbit, a bird, a crab, a giraffe.*

*Watson holds up a hand, and a shadow butterfly opens its wings. Henry makes a moose with his
long fingers. Anjali does an elaborate dragon. Holmes does a perfect, neat, precise snail.*

**HENRY**
(softly)
Back home, we'd call this the best part of the day.

**HOLMES**
(looking at the stones, quietly)
It was always right in front of us. We just had to look from the other side.

*The gold light fades slowly to violet. Fireflies wink. Duchess ambles through the circle. Her
shadow stretches across the stones, long and perfectly round, like a woolly cloud on legs.*

*Tavita points his telescope at the shadow, then at Duchess, then at the stone.*

**TAVITA**
(wonderingly)
...She's a hound made of wool.

**ANJALI**
(laughing)
Tavita, that's the best discovery of the day.

---

### EXT. THE STONE CIRCLE. DUSK. (BUTTON)

*The campers drift away down the Boardwalk, lanterns bobbing. On the edge of the circle, in
the last of the light, MOONY pads in and sits down. He looks at the stones, tilts his head
and looks at his own shadow, a gigantic, shaggy, floppy-eared dog on the biggest stone.*

*Moony lifts a paw. His shadow lifts a paw. Moony wags. His shadow wags. Moony stops, stares
at his shadow, and slowly, carefully, backs away. His shadow backs away too.*

**MOONY**
(small, worried)
...woo?

*He bumps into a stone. The shadow bumps into it too. He yelps and bolts out of the circle.*

**ANIMATOR:** Moony is scared of his own shadow, a gentle gag, and the reason he also isn't
the culprit. He is shown after the case is solved.

**THE END.**

## "HOW HOLMES FIGURED IT OUT" (9:30 to 11:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Fairy lights. The wall chart. Holmes and Watson on the floor with the journal. Holmes is
wearing her cap with the puffs showing. Watson holds up a shadow-puppet hound on the wall.*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Case journal, day sixty-six. Case closed. The shadow-hound was made of us.

**HOLMES**
Let's look at the clues.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame fills the screen.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**CLUE ONE: THE LOW SUN**

*The noon stones and the sunset stones replay side by side.*

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
At noon the shadows were short and there was no hound. At sunset the shadows were long and
there was. The hound only came when the sun was low.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE TWO: IT COPIES US**

*Tavita's telescope and the snout replay, rising and falling together.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
Every time Tavita moved, the shadow-snout moved. A real hound wouldn't copy a boy with a
telescope. A shadow would.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE THREE: THE PARTS MATCH**

*The four matching parts replay: puffs, telescope, curls, swinging arm.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
Two round ears. A long snout. A shaggy mane. A wagging tail. Every piece of the hound was one
of us.

*A gold star, a heart, and a doodle of five kids standing in a hound shape.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(turning to Watson)
I looked at everything in that circle except the person standing in the middle of it.

**WATSON**
(smiling)
You were looking from the wrong place.

**HOLMES**
(grinning)
...Curious.

*Watson writes, with a flourish: "LOOK FROM ANOTHER ANGLE." He draws a heart. DING-DING-DING:
three dings for "solved."*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Case closed. Hound cleared.

*Holmes adds a line to her wall chart: "THINGS THAT GO BUMP: US."*

**HOLMES**
Curious... very curious!

## Complexity and logic check

- **Shadows get longer when the sun is low** is a concept every five-year-old can see; I show
  it by comparing noon and sunset pictures.
- **The reveal** is physical and joyful: each kid finds their part of the shadow.
- **Clue 2** is the interactive heart: kids at home can try it themselves.

## Flag summary

| Flag | Where | Fix built in |
