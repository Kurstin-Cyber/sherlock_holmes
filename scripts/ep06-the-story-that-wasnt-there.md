# EPISODE 6: "THE STORY THAT WASN'T THERE"

*11 minutes · Script draft 1 · Stage 4*

**Spotlight:** Anjali and Beryl · **Lesson:** Honesty (ask first, and tell the truth even when it's hard)
**Clues:** (1) a lilac-coloured smell of paint on Anjali's story shelf · (2) a lilac smudge on the campfire bench · (3) a torn page with a tiny, neat "Actually..." correction beside Anjali's big, loopy writing
**Red herring:** Tavita says he saw the Hound carrying "a book" (Moony carrying a sock)
**Setting:** the campfire circle, Bracken Cabin, the Craft Barn

*Script key, look reminders and flags work as in `ep01-the-hound-comes-home.md`.*

**Optional thread:** The dining-room chalkboard reads **"CAMP DAY: 35 DAYS TO GO."**

## COLD OPEN (0:00 to 1:00)

### EXT. CAMPFIRE CIRCLE. DUSK.

*Golden dusk. The fire crackles low and friendly. Lanterns hang from poles. The campers sit on
the logs with blankets and cocoa. Fireflies. Glittery mist at their ankles.*

*ANJALI MORTIMER (11, plum cape, silk scarf, huge curls, lantern held high) strides to the tall
storyteller's chair. She sweeps her cape with a flourish.*

**ANJALI**
(booming, quick)
Remember! The Hound is big, he's shaggy, and he wants a snack!

**CAMPERS**
(together)
...WANTS A SNACK!

**ANJALI**
Tonight's tale: *The Squirrel the Size of a Pony.*

*In the second row, BERYL STAPLETON (lilac cardigan, long plait, a small paint smudge on her
thumb) leans toward her neighbour and whispers.*

**BERYL**
(small, to herself)
Actually... it was a medium squirrel.

*Murmurs of delight. Anjali reaches into the large satchel at her side, where she keeps her big
story notebook, fat, purple, and tied with a ribbon.*

*Her hand goes in. It searches. It keeps searching. Her face changes.*

*She turns the satchel upside-down. A pencil, a ribbon and a lump of lint fall out.*

**ANJALI (CONT'D)**
(a whisper)
It's gone.

*The campers gasp. Tavita's hat tips back. HENRY BASKERVILLE clutches his cocoa.*

**ANJALI (CONT'D)**
(rising, dramatic)
My story book! Every tale I have ever told is in that book! It's been... (she gulps)
...TAKEN!

*A beat. Then she points to the dark hills, and her voice drops.*

**ANJALI (CONT'D)**
By the Hound.

**TAVITA**
(not a whisper)
It's the Hound!

*Everyone looks at the hills. The hills look back. Nothing happens.*

**CUT TO: TITLE CARD.** *Golden magnifier swirls. A tiny wobbly harmonica "woo."*

## ACT ONE: THE CASE ARRIVES (1:00 to 4:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Fairy lights. KURSTIN HOLMES (cap, glasses) is writing a line on her wall chart. MATEO WATSON is at
his desk. A knock at the door. Holmes holds up one finger and finishes her word, then opens it.*

*Anjali stands there, cape drooping, scarf askew. Her eyes are red-rimmed. She looks smaller
than usual.*

**ANJALI**
(in a normal voice, very small)
Holmes. Can you help me find my book?

*Holmes blinks. The "very small" voice is something she has never heard from Anjali.*

**HOLMES**
(carefully, copying what she's seen Watson do)
...Yes. Come in. Would you like to sit down?

*Watson rolls over and offers her a cushion from his lap. DING, once, gentle.*

**ANJALI**
(sitting)
Every story I tell, I write in that book. The funny ones. The scary ones. The one about the
squirrel. (her voice wobbles) If it's gone, I won't have anything to tell.

**WATSON**
(gently)
That sounds really sad.

**ANJALI**
(a sniff)
It is.

*Holmes opens her mouth to say something clever. She looks at Watson. He tilts his head very
slightly. She shuts her mouth.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
I'm sorry it's gone. We'll find it.

*Anjali looks up at her. The first tiny smile.*

**ANIMATOR:** A small beat of growth for Holmes, not a big one. She remembers to pause, then
forgets by the next scene. Keep it light.

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(snapping back to herself)
When did you last see it?

**ANJALI**
(sniffing)
This morning. I put it on my shelf in Bracken Cabin. Then I went to breakfast. Then to rehearse.
Then to the campfire.

**HOLMES**
Did you tell anyone it was missing?

**ANJALI**
(her voice rising again)
I told everyone it was the Hound!

**WATSON**
(gently)
Um. Was it the Hound?

*Anjali stops. Her mouth opens, then closes. She looks at her cape.*

**ANJALI**
(very quietly)
...I don't know. I just said it because... when something goes wrong, I always say the Hound.

*Holmes taps her magnifier twice.*

**HOLMES**
Curious. Very curious.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles Anjali.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

### INT. BRACKEN CABIN. NIGHT.

*Six bunks, fairy lights, cosy. A wooden shelf above Anjali's bunk with candles, a toy
lantern, a feather and a gap, just the size of a fat notebook, between two books.*

*Holmes steps up on a stool. Watson holds the lantern. Anjali watches, arms wrapped around
herself.*

*Holmes leans toward the gap. She sniffs. Her nose twitches. She sniffs again.*

**SFX:** *Sniff. Sniff-sniff.*

*A ribbon of pale lilac colour curls up from the empty shelf: a visible, drifting trail of
"smell." It smells of fresh paint and pencil shavings.*

**ANIMATOR:** Show the smell as a pale lilac ribbon with tiny paint-pot and pencil icons
drifting in it. Nothing eerie, just a clear, pretty "smell-line." The smell fades as it leaves
the shelf.

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Paint. And pencil shavings.

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the lilac ribbon.*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Paint and pencils.

*He sketches the shelf with a lilac trail. Beside it: "A PAINTER?" and a star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

**ANJALI**
(sniffing, bewildered)
My book smells like paper. And squirrel.

**HOLMES**
Then something else was on that shelf.

**ANJALI**
(clutching her cape)
The Hound smells like paint?

**HOLMES**
(a pause)
I don't think hounds paint.

## ACT TWO: THE INVESTIGATION (4:00 to 8:00)

### EXT. CAMPFIRE CIRCLE. NIGHT.

*The fire is low and embers glow. The campers have gone to bed. Only lanterns remain, and the
moon, enormous and silver, through the mist. It is quiet and golden and pretty.*

*Holmes crouches by the storyteller's chair, a flat log with a cushion on top. She runs her
magnifier along the bench.*

**HOLMES**
Anjali sat here. Her satchel was here.

*She leans in close, until her glasses nearly touch the wood. A smear of colour glints in the
lantern light.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
A smudge.

*It's a pale, delicate lilac, the exact colour of a watercolour paint, a soft thumbprint on
the dark wood.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(thrilled)
Lilac. Wet-ish.

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the thumbprint.*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Lilac. Same as the smell.

*He draws the smudge. A tiny lilac crayon in the margin. A star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

**HENRY**
(peering down, holding a lantern)
It looks like paint.

**HOLMES**
It looks like someone's thumb.

**HENRY**
(wide-eyed)
The thief has a thumb?

**HOLMES**
(delighted)
Everybody has a thumb, Henry. But this one has paint on it.

*A swish of cape. A sudden, enthusiastic, extremely loud whisper.*

**TAVITA (O.S.)**
(whispering, which is to say, shouting)
I SAW IT!

*Everyone jumps. Tavita bounds out of the heather, hat flapping, telescope wedged under one
arm, clutching a soggy sock.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
(panting)
I saw the Hound! At dusk! He was carrying a BOOK!

**ANJALI**
(gasping)
HE HAS IT!

**HOLMES**
(drily)
Show me where you saw him.

### EXT. THE HALL, LAWN. NIGHT.

*A moonlit lawn, pale and silver. The Hall glows warmly behind. Tavita points with the telescope.*

**TAVITA**
(whispering)
There. By the boot room. Sneaking.

*From the boot room door, a huge shaggy shape slips out. It is MOONY. His head is down, tail
low, the way a dog looks when he has done something he is proud of. In his mouth, held tenderly
like a puppy, is a rolled-up grey sock.*

*Moony spots the group. He freezes. He wags. The sock wags with him.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
(a hoarse whisper)
Book...

*Silence. Holmes looks at Tavita. She looks at the sock. She looks at Anjali.*

**HOLMES**
It's a sock.

**TAVITA**
(slowly)
...It might be a very small book.

**ANJALI**
(hand on heart)
Tavita.

**TAVITA**
(a long pause, then, quietly)
Oh. Never mind.

*ZAID SELDEN (yellow raincoat, Tut) steps out of the shadows by the boot room and takes the
sock from Moony's mouth, gently.*

**ZAID**
(very quietly)
He doesn't like books. He likes socks.

*Moony licks his hand and thumps his tail. A hush. Then everyone laughs, softly, Anjali
loudest, a big watery laugh.*

**ANJALI**
(wiping her eyes)
He's the only one who's never stolen my stories.

**HOLMES**
(grinning)
The Hound is innocent. Again.

**WATSON**
(writing)
Writing that down.

### EXT. CAMPFIRE CIRCLE. LATER.

*Holmes kneels on the grass near the storyteller's log. She is looking at something under the
bench: a crumpled, folded scrap of paper, half-hidden by a tuft of heather.*

*She smooths it flat on the log with both hands. It's a torn page, plum-purple paper with
gold flecks, from Anjali's notebook. It's covered in huge, swirling, loopy handwriting.*

**HOLMES**
(reading)
"The squirrel was the size of a pony. His tail was the size of a flag. He had a voice like a
trumpet and a beard like a king."

**ANJALI**
(from a distance, a little defensive)
It was a very big squirrel.

*Holmes holds the page close to the lantern. Her magnifier hovers over the margin.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
There's something else.

*In the far corner of the page, in the tiniest, neatest, most careful pencil lettering, is a
single line:*

**PAGE (close-up):** *"(Actually... it was only a squirrel.)"*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the tiny pencil words.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(reading)
"Actually... it was only a squirrel."

*Watson rolls over. He looks at the page. He looks at Holmes. He looks, very gently, at
Anjali.*

**WATSON**
(softly)
Someone read your story. And someone corrected it.

**HENRY**
(whispering)
Who says "actually"?

*A beat. Holmes's eyes go round. She is looking at the small lilac smudge on the bench. She is
looking at the neat, tiny letters. She is remembering something: Beryl, with a sketchbook and
paint-stained thumb, saying "Actually..."*

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
Look closer.

**WATSON**
Notice more.

**HENRY**
(trying)
Think it through?

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles the three clues in turn: the lilac ribbon, the
thumbprint, the pencil "actually."*

**[JOURNAL]** *Watson's doodle: the torn page and the tiny pencil line. A star.*

## ACT THREE: THE REVEAL (8:00 to 9:30)

### INT. THE CRAFT BARN. NIGHT.

*A big, cosy barn lined with shelves of paints, brushes, glitter and ribbon. A long
paint-spattered table. Fairy lights and a single warm lamp. A paper moon hangs from a rafter.*

*At the end of the table, hunched over a huge sheet of watercolour paper, sits BERYL STAPLETON
(9, long red plait, lilac cardigan, paint on her thumb). She is painting a tiny, extraordinarily
detailed squirrel. Beside her, open on the table, is Anjali's fat purple notebook.*

*Around the notebook: a dozen finished paintings, drying on a line. A squirrel with a trumpet. A
hound with a snack. A girl in a cape, surrounded by stars. They are beautiful.*

*The door opens a crack. Holmes, Watson, Henry and Anjali peek in.*

**ANIMATOR:** Beryl does not see them. The paintings should be the loveliest thing in the
episode. Watercolour textures on the 3D set.

*Holmes stops. She holds out an arm to stop the others. She turns and whispers.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Let Anjali go first.

**WATSON**
(softly)
Good idea.

*Anjali steps through the door. Beryl looks up. Her face goes white.*

**BERYL**
(shooting to her feet)
Anjali! I—

*She bangs her knee on the table. A jar of brushes wobbles. She catches it.*

**BERYL (CONT'D)**
(babbling)
I was going to put it back! I was! I just wanted to finish. It's for Camp Day. I wanted to
surprise you. I wasn't going to take it for long. And then you told everyone the Hound did it,
and I... I couldn't say... I didn't know how to say...

*She stops. Her eyes fill. She twirls her plait round and round her finger.*

**BERYL (CONT'D)**
(very small)
I should have asked first.

*Silence. Anjali walks slowly to the table. She looks at the paintings. The squirrel with the
trumpet. The hound with the snack. A tiny painted girl in a purple cape, holding up a
lantern, with a great big smile.*

*She puts her hand on her heart.*

**ANJALI**
(quietly, no performance at all)
You painted my stories.

**BERYL**
(a whisper)
I love your stories. I wanted everyone to see them.

*Anjali is silent for a long moment. Then she lets out a long breath.*

**ANJALI**
(in a small, honest voice)
I didn't mean to blame the Hound. I just... got scared. And when I'm scared, I make it bigger.

**BERYL**
(sniffing)
Actually... you do that a lot.

*Anjali laughs, a surprised, wet, wonderful laugh.*

**ANJALI**
(wiping her eyes)
I do. I really do.

*Beryl laughs too. They are both laughing and crying. Holmes shifts from foot to foot.*

**HOLMES**
(awkwardly)
That's... good?

**WATSON**
(smiling, gently)
That's very good.

## ACT FOUR: THE LESSON (9:30)

### INT. THE CRAFT BARN. CONTINUOUS.

*Anjali and Beryl sit side by side at the table, turning the pages of the notebook. Beryl
points to her paintings. Anjali nods, delighted, and adds bits of stories.*

**ANJALI**
(quietly, to Beryl)
Will you paint the one about the pony-sized squirrel?

**BERYL**
(gently)
Actually... it was only a squirrel.

**ANJALI**
(grinning)
It was a *very big* squirrel.

**BERYL**
(a small smile)
It was a *medium* squirrel.

**ANJALI**
(laughing)
Medium.

**ANIMATOR:** Beryl's whisper in the cold open plants her "Actually" for first-time viewers; keep
it quiet and quick. Their running gag, "very big" versus "medium," can return in later episodes as a
quick background beat.

*Holmes steps forward. She holds out the torn page.*

**HOLMES**
You left this under the bench.

**BERYL**
(reaching)
I was copying it and I tore it.

**HOLMES**
You could have asked.

**BERYL**
(sincerely)
I know. I'm sorry.

**HOLMES**
(thinking)
It's hard to ask first.

**BERYL**
It's harder not to.

*Holmes freezes. A tiny, startled "oh" crosses her face. She looks at Watson. He grins.*

**HOLMES**
(to herself, quietly)
...Curious.

**WATSON**
(smiling)
Mm-hm.

*Anjali stands, strides to the middle of the barn, and throws her cape back.*

**ANJALI**
(booming)
GATHER ROUND!

*Campers in pyjamas pour in, yawning. Anjali holds up a painting.*

**ANJALI (CONT'D)**
Tonight's tale: *The Squirrel*. A true story.

**TAVITA**
(wide-eyed)
How big was he?

*Anjali glances at Beryl. Beryl raises an eyebrow, gently.*

**ANJALI**
(clearing her throat)
...Quite big.

*Beryl smiles.*

### EXT. CAMPFIRE CIRCLE. NIGHT. (BUTTON)

*Soft lantern light. The campers are gathered, sleepy and happy. Anjali tells the tale. A huge
shaggy shape is sitting on the edge of the firelight, head tilted, tail thumping gently on the
grass. He holds a sock.*

*Anjali pauses dramatically.*

**ANJALI**
(whispering)
And the squirrel looked up and said...

*She gestures. The whole audience leans in.*

**MOONY**
(softly)
Woo.

*Everyone laughs. Anjali bows.*

**ANIMATOR:** Moony is welcomed at the edge of the circle. The campers who know are comfortable;
anyone who doesn't would simply see a "shaggy shape." Keep it soft.

**THE END.**

## Complexity and logic check

- **Smell as a clue:** shown as a visible lilac ribbon, so it works for all viewers.
- **Clue 3** needs a callback: viewers must connect "actually" to Beryl. She says it in the pilot
  and episode 3, but this episode is meant to stand alone, so I added a line to Holmes's
  realisation: she remembers Beryl saying "Actually..." with a paint-stained thumb. If it
  isn't clear enough for a first-time viewer, add a one-second flashback of Beryl saying
  "Actually..." earlier in the episode (for example, as a quiet aside to Anjali in the cold
  open crowd).

## Flag summary

| Flag | Where | Fix built in |
|---|---|---|
| Anjali's distress | Act One | Short and honest; Watson and Holmes comfort her; she is never shamed |
| "Stolen" book | Throughout | The "thief" is a friend with a loving surprise; no punishment |
| Beryl's panic | Act Three | Warm, private, and met with kindness |
| Night scenes | Acts Two, Three | Moon, lanterns, fairy lights; always in groups |
| Moony in the herring | Act Two | A sock gag; Zaid is there; Moony is never blamed for long |

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Anjali is vulnerable here.** It's the first time we see her small and honest. It fits her
   flaw (exaggeration) and her arc ("a good story doesn't need to scare anyone"). Keep this
   depth, or lighter?
2. **Cross-episode callback removed.** Holmes no longer says "that's what I said last time" (it
   leaned on episode 2). Beryl's "Actually..." is planted in this episode's cold open.
3. **"Very big" vs "medium"** (Anjali and Beryl's squirrel gag) as a new recurring bit. Keep?
4. **Beryl and the Craft Barn:** a new location (the Craft Barn already appears in the bible's
   world list). I've set it up as the place for art. OK?
5. **Moony's "Woo" at the campfire** appears in the button; he's safe and welcome at the edge of
   the circle. Do any non-camp visitors (like the Brambledown kids in the finale) ever see
   him? That could change how he behaves here.
