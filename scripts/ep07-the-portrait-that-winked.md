# EPISODE 7: "THE PORTRAIT THAT WINKED"

*11 minutes · Script draft 1 · Stage 4*

**Spotlight:** Henry · **Lesson:** Kindness (notice your friends, and show it)
**Clues:** (1) the wink happens only at three o'clock, when a patch of sunlight reaches the frame · (2) a loop of blue string with a neat double bow on the curtain hook · (3) a warm, buttery smell of shortbread
**Red herring:** The twins say the portrait has winked at Baskervilles for a hundred years ("Grandpa told us")
**Setting:** the Hall's portrait gallery

*Script key, look reminders and flags work as in `ep01-the-hound-comes-home.md`.*

**Optional thread:** The dining-room chalkboard reads **"CAMP DAY: 30 DAYS TO GO."**

**Look reminder:** SIR BARTHOLOMEW BASKERVILLE is a goofy painted ancestor: round pink face,
enormous feathered hat, a curly moustache that points at both ears, a very serious expression.
Beside him in the painting sits a shaggy painted dog with one ear up and one ear down. The
whole painting is warm, silly and gold-framed, never spooky.

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00 to 1:00)

### INT. THE HALL, PORTRAIT GALLERY. DAY.

*A long, bright gallery with a polished wooden floor and tall windows down one side. On the
walls, a row of gold-framed paintings of Baskervilles: every one in a wildly silly hat. A
feathered one. A pizza-shaped one. A hat with a tiny boat on it.*

*ANJALI MORTIMER (plum cape, lantern, huge curls) leads a group of small campers along the
gallery like a tour guide with a flag.*

**ANJALI**
(booming, quick)
Welcome to the Gallery of Baskervilles! Remember! The Hound is big, he's shaggy, and he wants a
snack!

**SMALL CAMPERS**
(together)
...WANTS A SNACK!

*They stop in front of the grandest painting, at the end of the gallery. It shows SIR
BARTHOLOMEW BASKERVILLE, tall, proud and moustached, with the enormous feathered hat. At his
side sits a big shaggy painted dog, one ear up, one ear down, tongue out.*

*Behind the group, TAVITA FRANKLAND (hat, telescope) appears in the doorway, gasping.*

**TAVITA**
(not a whisper)
It's the Hound!

*Every head turns. He points the telescope at the painted dog. A beat. He lowers it. He reads
the little brass plaque.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
"Portrait of a dog." ...It's a painting.

*He considers this.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
Oh. Never mind.

*The tour group laughs and moves on. The gallery empties. Sunlight slants through the windows.
Dust motes sparkle.*

*HENRY BASKERVILLE (plaid shirt, star-stickered hat) is still standing alone in front of
the portrait, looking up at the painted man in the feathered hat.*

**HENRY**
(quietly)
Hi, Sir Bartholomew. I'm Henry. I think I'm your great-great-great-something.

*He fishes the photo from his pocket and holds it up to the painting.*

**HENRY (CONT'D)**
That's my dad. And that's my sister Hana. They're at home. In Vancouver. On a ferry.

*A tiny glint of sunlight touches the painting. And Sir Bartholomew's painted eye gives a
sparkling, golden... wink.*

*Henry stares. He blinks. The portrait has returned to normal.*

*Henry's hat goes right down over his eyes.*

**CUT TO: TITLE CARD.** *Golden magnifier swirls. A tiny wobbly harmonica "woo."*

**⚠️ FLAG (cold open):** A portrait that moves is a classic spooky trope. Fix: the painting is
a goofy ancestor in a ridiculous hat, the wink is a sparkle of gold light, the scene is in
bright daytime, and the follow-on beat is Henry pulling his hat over his eyes, a comic reflex.

## ACT ONE: THE CASE ARRIVES (1:00 to 4:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. DAY.

*Fairy lights, maps, the wall chart "THINGS THAT GO BUMP." KURSTIN HOLMES (cap, glasses, raincoat)
is perched on her desk with a magnifier, studying a leaf. MATEO WATSON (teal hoodie, orange chair)
is drawing at the other desk. Henry stands in the doorway, hat low, voice small.*

**HENRY**
Holmes? The portrait winked at me.

*Holmes's head snaps up. She drops the leaf.*

**HOLMES**
(thrilled)
A winking portrait! Excellent!

*Watson's bell goes DING (once, nervous). He doesn't look up. He draws a little faster.*

**HENRY**
(a very small voice)
It's Sir Bartholomew. The one with the feathered hat. I think he winked at me because I'm a
Baskerville.

**HOLMES**
(not hearing the small voice)
A mystery! A portrait! A wink! When? Where? How long? What time?

*Henry's shoulders sink. He pulls his hat lower.*

**WATSON**
(gently)
Holmes. He's a bit shaken.

**HOLMES**
(registering, a beat late)
Oh! Right. Yes. (to Henry, trying) ...That must have been surprising.

**HENRY**
(a wobbly smile)
It was a bit.

*Holmes taps her magnifier twice. She looks at him, properly this time, and softens.*

**HOLMES**
We'll find out why. Together.

*DING-DING from Watson's bell, twice, happy. But his eyes slide away to the window.*

**ANIMATOR:** Watson is being quiet and a bit sneaky. Keep it subtle: a glance at the window,
a pencil that scribbles very fast. Holmes should not notice. Viewers who look closely will.

### INT. THE HALL, PORTRAIT GALLERY. AFTERNOON.

*The gallery, bright and warm. A patch of golden sunlight lies on the polished floor by the
tall windows. Holmes crouches next to it, magnifier out. Watson is parked near the window with
the journal. Henry stands a little back, holding Hana's photo.*

*LINH and MINH BARRYMORE (matching aprons) lean in the doorway, grinning, each with a
cheese-toastie in hand.*

**LINH**
(in a spooky voice)
Sir Bartholomew has been winking at Baskervilles for a hundred years.

**MINH**
(deadpan)
It's an honour.

**HENRY**
(wide-eyed)
A hundred years?

**HOLMES**
(not looking up)
Any evidence?

**LINH**
(sheepish)
...Grandpa told us.

**MINH**
Grandpa also says the soup is magic.

**HOLMES**
Is it?

**LINH & MINH**
(together)
It's very good soup.

**HOLMES**
(drily)
That's gossip, not a clue.

*The twins laugh. Linh bumps Henry's elbow, friendly.*

**LINH**
(warmly, to Henry)
We're kidding. Mostly.

**MINH**
(signing, then aloud)
Welcome home.

**SUBTITLE:** *Welcome home.*

*Henry's hat star twinkles. His face does something small and wonderful. The twins wander off,
munching.*

**ANIMATOR:** The twins' gossip is the red herring, and it is affectionate. It never makes
Henry feel excluded; it makes him feel included. No malice.

**HOLMES**
(rising)
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles the gallery.*

*She checks the big clock at the gallery's end. 2:40.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Twenty minutes. Everyone watch the portrait.

## ACT TWO: THE INVESTIGATION (4:00 to 8:00)

### INT. THE HALL, PORTRAIT GALLERY. 2:50 TO 3:00.

*The sunlit patch on the floor has crept closer to the portrait. It slides across the
polished boards, inch by inch, golden and slow. Everyone watches.*

*Holmes narrates in a low voice, like a nature documentary.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
The sun moves. The patch of light moves. And in a moment...

*The sunlight reaches the bottom of the painting's gold frame. It climbs. It touches the
painted dog's nose. It glides up the painted jacket. It touches the painted face.*

*And Sir Bartholomew's painted eye gives a golden, sparkling wink.*

**HENRY**
(a gasp)
There!

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the sunlit patch, the
frame and the winking eye.*

*The sunlight slides on. The eye returns to normal.*

**WATSON**
(softly, writing)
It winked at three o'clock exactly. When the light touched it.

**HOLMES**
(delighted)
It only winks when the sun reaches it!

*She marks the floor with a piece of chalk where the sunlit patch ended.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
So it's not a ghost. It's not a curse. It's... light.

**HENRY**
(a little crestfallen)
Oh.

*Holmes looks at him. She looks at his face. She sees it, a tiny second late.*

**HOLMES**
(quickly)
But light is interesting! Light has a reason!

*Henry gives a faint nod, still a little deflated.*

**[JOURNAL]** *Watson's doodle: the sunbeam, the frame, the wink. A star.*

*Watson looks at Henry with a worried frown, then at the window. His bell goes DING (once,
nervous).*

### INT. THE HALL, PORTRAIT GALLERY. A LITTLE LATER.

*Holmes strides toward the tall window at the end of the gallery. The curtains hang in heavy,
velvet folds. She stops. Her eyes narrow.*

*On the curtain hook, a loop of bright blue string dangles. It is tied in a neat, tidy,
double bow, a bow that has clearly been tied by someone who cares about bows.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
A string.

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the blue string and the
double bow.*

*She pulls it gently. It goes taut. Something behind the curtain tinkles.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Something is hanging here.

*Watson's bell goes DING (once, sharp). Holmes doesn't hear it.*

**HENRY**
(peering)
Why would anyone hang a string?

**HOLMES**
(grinning)
Excellent question. To hold something up.

*She sniffs the air. Her nose twitches. A golden-brown ribbon of "smell" curls up from the
curtain, with tiny shortbread-biscuit icons dancing inside it.*

**ANIMATOR:** The smell is a warm golden ribbon with biscuit icons, matching the lilac smell-line
convention from earlier episodes.

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Butter. Sugar. Something baked.

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the golden ribbon.*

**HENRY**
(sniffing, suddenly very still)
That's... shortbread.

*Holmes turns. Henry's whole face has changed. His eyes are shining.*

**HENRY (CONT'D)**
Gran's shortbread. She sends it in a tin. It smells exactly like that.

**WATSON**
(writing, softly)
Writing that down...

*He writes. His hand shakes slightly. He draws the string, the bow, and the shortbread. Beside
it, a tiny star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

*Holmes looks at the string. At the bow. At the shortbread smell. At Watson, who has gone
suddenly very interested in his pencil.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
Look closer.

**WATSON**
(very quietly)
Notice more.

**HENRY**
(trying)
Think it through?

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles Watson.*

## ACT THREE: THE REVEAL (8:00 to 9:30)

### INT. THE HALL, PORTRAIT GALLERY. CONTINUOUS.

*Holmes parts the heavy velvet curtain with both hands.*

*Hanging from the blue string, in the sunlit window, turning slowly in the golden light, is a
small, shining, painted tin shape: a little ferry boat, cut from the lid of a shortbread tin,
with two tiny sails and a painted funnel. On its side, in careful lettering: HANA.*

*The sun strikes the tin. A bright, quivering spot of reflected light dances along the gallery
wall, across the floor, up the frame, and into Sir Bartholomew's painted eye.*

*Wink.*

*The gallery is very quiet.*

**HOLMES**
(softly)
Oh.

*Henry takes a step forward. His hand goes to his mouth.*

**HENRY**
(a tiny voice)
It's a ferry.

*He turns slowly. Watson has rolled forward. Beside him, from behind a pillar, a small shape
with a long red plait and a lilac cardigan steps out: BERYL STAPLETON, holding a paintbrush
and a cup of paint. Her cheeks are pink.*

**WATSON**
(nervous, quiet)
We made it at the Craft Barn. Beryl painted it. I did the string.

**BERYL**
(small)
Actually, Watson did the bow.

**WATSON**
(smiling)
A double bow.

**HENRY**
(whispering)
But why?

*Watson takes a breath.*

**WATSON**
You come here every day at three to talk to Sir Bartholomew. You look at the photo. And
you're sad. I... we saw you.

**BERYL**
(softly)
We wanted you to have something that sparkled. From home.

*Henry looks at the ferry boat. At the dancing spot of light. At the painted eye winking.*

**WATSON**
(quietly)
Your dad's ferry. We thought if it winked every day at three, it would feel like someone was
waving at you.

*Henry's hat comes off. His eyes are shining, and he is smiling so wide his face might break.*

**HENRY**
(wobbly)
Back home, we'd wave at the ferry.

*He lifts his arm. He waves at the dancing light. Beryl waves. Watson waves, DING-DING-DING.*

**ANIMATOR:** This is the emotional peak. Hold the shot of three kids waving at a spot of
light on the wall. Gold light. Soft music. No jokes.

*Holmes looks at the three of them. She looks at the ferry, the string, the bow. She turns to
Watson, and, for once, she says it plainly.*

**HOLMES**
(quietly)
You noticed before I did.

**WATSON**
(gently)
You noticed after. That counts.

**HOLMES**
(grinning, a little embarrassed)
Curious.

## ACT FOUR: THE LESSON (9:30)

### INT. THE HALL, PORTRAIT GALLERY. CONTINUOUS.

*Holmes pulls a folded card from her raincoat pocket. She holds it out to Henry with both hands.*

**HOLMES**
I made you something. I drew it after the first week and then I forgot to give it to you.

*Henry unfolds it. It is a big, wobbly drawing of a magnifying glass with a smiley face. In
careful block letters:*

**CARD (close-up):** *"HENRY BASKERVILLE: OFFICIAL NOTICE-ER. WE NOTICED YOU. (WATSON FIRST.
ME SECOND.)"*

*Henry reads it twice. Then he throws both arms round Holmes. She goes rigid as a plank, then
slowly melts and pats his back.*

**HOLMES**
(muffled)
...You're welcome.

*Linh and Minh peek in at the doorway, toasties paused halfway to their mouths. Both go "aww"
silently. Minh signs something and Linh swats him.*

**MINH**
(signing, subtitled: "Told you he'd like it.")

**SUBTITLE:** *Told you he'd like it.*

**LINH**
(whispering)
You did NOT.

---

### INT. THE HALL, PORTRAIT GALLERY. NIGHT. (BUTTON)

*Moonlight. The gallery is soft and silver. The portraits seem asleep. The ferry boat
turns slowly in the window, catching the moon.*

*A huge, shaggy shape pads in on silent paws and sits in front of the big portrait. MOONY
looks up at the painted dog beside Sir Bartholomew: one ear up, one ear down, tongue out.*

*Moony tilts his head. The painted dog doesn't move. Moony tilts it the other way.*

*Slowly, one ear goes up. The other goes down. His tongue lolls out.*

*Moony sits, perfectly matching the painted dog's pose. He and the painting look at each other
for a very long moment.*

**MOONY**
(very softly)
...Woo?

*The ferry boat's reflection slides across the painting's eye. Sir Bartholomew winks.*

*Moony's tail thumps once, twice, three times.*

**ANIMATOR:** A quiet, sweet "mystery for another day": Moony and the painted dog look alike.
Do not explain it. This is a seed for the future collar-tag story and must work as a pure
cuddly moment for first-time viewers.

**THE END.**

## "HOW HOLMES FIGURED IT OUT" (9:30 to 11:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Fairy lights. The wall chart. Holmes and Watson on the floor with the journal. A plate of
shortbread between them, a shortbread tin beside it with a lid missing. Henry sits on his
bunk, Hana's photo on his knee, a gold-star sticker on his hat.*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Case journal, day fifty-six. Case closed. The portrait wasn't winking because he's a
Baskerville. He winked because of a very special ferry.

**HOLMES**
Let's look at the clues.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame fills the screen.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**CLUE ONE: THE THREE O'CLOCK WINK**

*The sunlit patch replays, sliding up the frame, and touching the painted eye.*

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
The wink happened at three o'clock, exactly when the patch of sun reached the painting. So
it wasn't magic. It was light.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE TWO: THE BLUE STRING**

*The blue string replays, with its neat double bow.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A string on the curtain hook, tied in a very neat double bow. Something was hanging by it.
And someone who ties very good bows had put it there.

*A gold star, and a doodle of Watson tying a bow.*

**CLUE THREE: THE SHORTBREAD**

*The golden smell-ribbon replays, with biscuits dancing.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
The smell of shortbread. Henry knew it straight away. Gran's shortbread tin. So the ferry
was made from its lid. And only one person at camp gets shortbread in a tin.

*A gold star, a heart, and a doodle of a tin ferry.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(turning to Henry)
I looked for a clue and I found a kindness.

**HENRY**
(smiling)
That's the best kind.

**HOLMES**
(grinning)
Curious... very curious!

*Watson writes, with a flourish: "NOTICE YOUR FRIENDS. THEN TELL THEM." He draws a heart.
DING-DING-DING: three dings for "solved."*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Case closed. Hound cleared.

*Holmes adds a line to her wall chart: "THINGS THAT GO BUMP: A FRIEND WITH A SUN-CATCHER."*

**HENRY**
(softly, to the photo)
Back home, we'd wave.

*He waves at the photo. A tiny reflection dances on the cabin wall from the window. He waves
at that too.*

## Complexity and logic check

- **Clue 1** is shown, not told: a patch of sunlight crawls up the frame and the wink happens.
- **Clue 2** is an object with a clear "someone made this" trace (a very neat bow).
- **Clue 3** is a smell shown as a golden ribbon (the same visual language as episode 6).
- The solution is emotional rather than logical: viewers will guess "someone made it for
  Henry," and Holmes catches up.

## Flag summary

| Flag | Where | Fix built in |
|---|---|---|
| A portrait that moves | Cold open, Act Two | Goofy ancestor; the "wink" is a sparkle of light; daytime; Henry's hat-over-eyes gag |
| Henry's homesickness | Throughout | Real, tender, resolved by friends within the episode; nothing prolonged |
| Watson keeping a secret | Acts One, Two | His nerves are gentle and sweet; Holmes misses them (her flaw); no deception harms anyone |
| Moony and the painted dog | Button | Quiet, cuddly; no explanation; first-time viewers just see a funny likeness |
| Twins' "curse" tease | Act One | Warm, quickly denied ("Grandpa also says the soup is magic") |

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Moony and the painted dog** is a seed for the Baskerville-tag story, which we agreed to
   leave for a later episode. In this draft, there is no explanation. If you'd prefer to
   avoid setting up a future plot in a standalone episode, I can cut the button and use
   Moony cuddling Henry instead.
2. **Henry's "Hana" ferry** gives the sun-catcher its name. It echoes his baby sister; should
   Hana be an off-screen character the show returns to?
3. **Holmes's card** says "WATSON FIRST. ME SECOND." She admits she was slow. Is this too
   much repeated growth after the pilot? I made it a small, funny card to keep it light.
4. **Beryl's "Actually, Watson did the bow"** is a gentle reuse of her catchphrase. OK?
5. **The twins' Welcome home** line and sign: nice touch, or too sweet? It's the second time
   the twins act as warm background in an episode; they might get a bit more mischief in the
   next one.
