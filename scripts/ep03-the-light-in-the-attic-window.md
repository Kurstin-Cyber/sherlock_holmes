# EPISODE 3: "THE LIGHT IN THE ATTIC WINDOW"

*11 minutes · Script draft 1 · Stage 4*

**Spotlight:** Holmes and Jasper · **Lesson:** Curiosity (look for the cause before the monster; and tell your friends what you're doing)
**Clues:** (1) the light shines only on clear nights · (2) silvery dust on the attic sill · (3) a tally sheet in Jasper's neat writing
**Red herring:** Two "glowing eyes" in the attic window (a teddy bear's shiny buttons)
**Setting:** the Hall's attic, the lawn, Gorse Cabin

*Script key, look reminders and flags work as in `ep01-the-hound-comes-home.md`.*

**Optional thread:** The dining-room chalkboard reads **"CAMP DAY: 50 DAYS TO GO."**

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00 to 1:00)

### EXT. THE HALL, LAWN. DUSK.

*Golden dusk. Pink clouds. The Hall's tall stone tower glows. A line of campers sits on the
grass with their cocoa, looking up at the sky, pointing at the first stars.*

*ANJALI MORTIMER (cape, lantern, huge hair) stands on a tree stump like a ship's captain.*

**ANJALI**
(booming, quick)
Remember! The Hound is big, he's shaggy, and he wants a snack!

*A few campers say it with her: "...WANTS A SNACK!"*

**ANIMATOR:** The same quick reminder as every episode. Keep it short, and over by 0:12.

*Beside her, HENRY BASKERVILLE (tall, red plaid, hat with a gold star sticker) sips cocoa
and gazes upward. His eyes follow the tower... up... up... to the small round window at the
very top. It is dark.*

*Then it isn't.*

*A soft, silvery-white glow turns on in the attic window. It shimmers. It flickers. It
looks like a small, strange moon.*

**HENRY**
(quietly, tugging Anjali's cape)
Anjali. There's a light in the attic.

*Anjali looks. Her eyes go huge and delighted.*

**ANJALI**
(a hushed, theatrical whisper)
The Hound's eye...

**HENRY**
(hat down over his eyes)
Why do you sound so happy?

*Cut to TAVITA FRANKLAND, hat flapping, telescope up, behind a hedge.*

**TAVITA**
(not a whisper)
It's the Hound!

*Every head turns to him. A beat. Tavita lowers the telescope. He looks at the end.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
...I'm not sure. It might be a lamp.

*A beat.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
(brightly)
It's a lamp!

**CUT TO: TITLE CARD.** *Golden magnifier swirls. A tiny wobbly harmonica "woo."*

## ACT ONE: THE CASE ARRIVES (1:00 to 4:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Fairy lights. A shelf of jars. A big paper chart on the wall: "THINGS THAT GO BUMP AND
WHAT THEY REALLY ARE" in IMANI HOLMES's tidy handwriting. It has only four entries.*

*Holmes is pacing in her mustard raincoat. MATEO WATSON is at his desk with the journal.
Henry sits cross-legged on the rug, holding a mug.*

**HENRY**
(a small, brave voice)
Back home, we'd turn the light on and see what's there.

**HOLMES**
(stops pacing)
Henry, that is *exactly* what a detective does.

**HENRY**
It is?

**HOLMES**
Curious... very curious!

*She taps her magnifier twice, then hurries to the window. The attic window is lit again,
a silvery smudge in the dark.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
It's been there three nights now. Same time. Same shimmer.

**WATSON**
(writing)
Case journal, day thirty-six. A light in the attic window.

*He doodles the tower with a glowing window. Beside it: three tiny moons, one for each night.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The three moons in the doodle are all clear and bright. A small star.*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
(looking up)
Um. It was cloudy on Tuesday. The light was off.

**HOLMES**
(turning, delighted)
Tuesday! Was it?

*Watson flips back. A doodle of Tuesday: a big grey cloud. No light.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
The light shines when the sky is clear. And not when the sky is cloudy.

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the three clear moons
and the grey cloud.*

**HENRY**
(thinking)
Do hounds care about clouds?

**HOLMES**
(a pause)
Henry. That is an excellent question.

*She writes it on the chart. Under "THINGS THAT GO BUMP" she adds: "THINGS THAT LIKE CLEAR
NIGHTS."*

**ANIMATOR:** Holmes's chart is a cute running gag. Each episode she adds one line. Keep the
handwriting neat and the chart partly visible.

### INT. THE HALL, STAIRCASE. NIGHT.

*Holmes, Watson and Henry stand at the foot of the grand wooden staircase. The portraits of
old Baskervilles look down in silly hats. A rope across the bottom step. A sign in neat,
upright handwriting:*

**SIGN:** *"ATTIC: CLOSED. By order. Rules are rules. J.S."*

**WATSON**
(reading)
"J.S."

**HOLMES**
(taps the sign, thinking)
J.S.

*Beat. Footsteps. JASPER STAPLETON (11, sweater vest, clipboard, badge) appears at the top of
the stairs. He stops short. He stands very straight.*

**JASPER**
(stiffly)
Good evening. The attic is closed.

**HOLMES**
Who closed it?

**JASPER**
(a tiny pause)
It was... closed. By order.

**HOLMES**
Whose order?

**JASPER**
(clipboard clutched to chest)
The... Camp Chief's. Rules are rules.

*He taps his clipboard twice, quickly. Holmes notices. Her eyes narrow.*

**HOLMES**
Curious...

**JASPER**
(too fast)
Good night.

*He walks away, very stiffly, very quickly, nearly bumping the banister.*

**HENRY**
(whispering)
He's hiding something.

**WATSON**
(gently)
Or he's nervous.

**HOLMES**
Or both.

## ACT TWO: THE INVESTIGATION (4:00 to 8:00)

### INT. THE HALL, ATTIC. DAY.

*Next morning. Bright. A dusty, sunlit attic with a slanting roof, old trunks, a rocking
horse, a pile of faded flags, a box of puppets and a tall mirror. Sunbeams cut through the
dust. It is cosy, not creepy.*

*Holmes, Watson and Henry have arrived. Watson has come up in a small, old-fashioned lift
that the campers painted with stars. It is slow and wobbly and delightful. DING!*

**ANIMATOR:** The old lift is a charming feature, a tiny "cage lift" with a painted sky
inside. It's the Hall's accessible route. Never make it a joke at Watson's expense.

**WATSON**
(rolling out)
It's... big.

**HENRY**
(ducking under the beams)
Back home, we'd call this "a cosy fort."

*Holmes heads straight to the round window at the end. Her magnifier goes to work.*

**HOLMES**
Windowsill.

*She holds up a finger. It's dusted with a shimmering silver powder. It sparkles in the
sunbeam.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the silvery dust.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Silver dust! It sparkles.

**HENRY**
(whispering)
Is it hound dust?

**HOLMES**
(patient)
I don't think hounds have dust.

*From behind a trunk, there's a rustle.*

*Everyone freezes. A pair of round, shiny, glowing eyes stares out from the dark corner of
the window seat.*

**ANIMATOR:** Make the eyes round, shiny and clearly button-like. The attic is sunny; the
"eyes" are lit by the sunbeam. The sound is a creak, not a growl.

**TAVITA**
(popping up from behind the trunk with his telescope)
I SEE THE EYES!

**ALL**
(jumping)
AH!

*Tavita points his telescope at the eyes. Slowly, carefully, he walks over. He parts the dust
sheet.*

*Sitting on the window seat, squashed and lopsided, is an old teddy bear. Two shiny black
buttons for eyes.*

**TAVITA**
(slowly)
...It's a bear. With buttons.

*Silence. Then the teddy bear's head falls off.*

*It lands in Tavita's hat. Tavita doesn't blink.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
Oh. Never mind.

*Everyone laughs. Watson's bell goes DING-DING. Henry laughs so hard his hat falls off.*

**ANIMATOR:** The headless-teddy gag should be sweet, not eerie. Soft music; the bear's head is
sewn on with big, happy stitches.

*Holmes sets the bear on a trunk. She dusts her hands.*

**HOLMES**
The Hound's innocent. Again.

**WATSON**
(writing)
Writing that down!

*A voice from the stairs, stiff and urgent:*

**JASPER (O.S.)**
Don't touch anything!

*Jasper appears at the top of the stairs. He is out of breath. He holds a roll of white
sheet.*

*He sees them. He sees the dusty finger. He sees the open window. His face falls.*

**JASPER (CONT'D)**
You went up.

**HOLMES**
(politely)
We did.

**JASPER**
(too fast)
The attic is dangerous! There might be—it's all— (to himself) rules are rules.

**HOLMES**
Is it dangerous?

*Jasper's mouth opens. Closes. His eyes flick to the corner of the room, where a long
white sheet is draped across a frame, next to a small lamp.*

**WATSON**
(gently)
Jasper, is everything okay?

**JASPER**
(quickly)
Fine! I'm fine! Everything's fine!

*He taps his clipboard twice, fast. Then he drops it. Papers flutter out.*

*Watson rolls over. He picks up the clipboard. A paper slips out of it and lands face-up on
the floorboards.*

**HOLMES**
(bending, reading)
It's a list.

*Close on the sheet: dozens of short, tidy lines in groups of five, with the headings
"MOTHS (BROWN)," "MOTHS (WHITE)," "MOTHS (FLUFFY)." Small star-shaped sketches in the margin.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the tally marks and
Jasper's neat handwriting.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(reading slowly)
"Moths. Brown. White. Fluffy."

**HENRY**
Moths?

*Jasper looks as if he would like to melt into the floorboards.*

**JASPER**
(very small)
...They're for a survey.

**WATSON**
(writing)
A survey!

**[JOURNAL]** *The tally sheet, doodled. A star.*

*Holmes looks at the dust on her finger. She looks at the tally sheet. She looks at the
lamp. She looks at the sheet.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly, delighted)
Look closer.

**WATSON**
Notice more.

**HENRY**
(smiling)
Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles the dust, the tally and the lamp.*

## ACT THREE: THE REVEAL (8:00 to 9:30)

### INT. THE HALL, ATTIC. A MOMENT LATER.

*BERYL STAPLETON (lilac cardigan, long plait, sketchbook) has quietly come up the stairs
and stands in the doorway. She looks at her brother. She doesn't say anything.*

*Holmes walks slowly around the lamp and the sheet, magnifier out.*

**HOLMES**
Three clues.

*She holds up a finger.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
One. The light only shines on clear nights.

*A glowing outline of the clear moons.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Two. Silver dust on the windowsill.

*A glowing outline of the dust.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Three. A tally sheet. Moths. Brown. White. Fluffy.

*A glowing outline of the sheet.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(to Beryl, kindly)
Beryl, what is silver dust?

**BERYL**
(small voice)
It's wing dust. From a moth.

**HOLMES**
So a moth was on the sill. A lot of moths. Which means...

*She looks at the lamp and the white sheet.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
It's a moth light! Moths love bright lights on clear nights. You put up a lamp and a white
sheet. The moths fly to it. And you count them.

*She turns to Jasper.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
You're counting moths.

*A pause. Jasper nods.*

**JASPER**
(quietly)
For the Nature Club. The Park Moth Survey. It's only for a week. I had permission. The
Camp Chief left a note.

*He pulls out a small folded note.*

**NOTE (close-up):** *"Attic okay for moths. Good luck! Don't forget to tell your friends. -C.C."*

*Jasper looks at the last line. He winces.*

**JASPER (CONT'D)**
(quietly)
I forgot that last part.

*Silence. Then Henry steps forward.*

**HENRY**
(gently)
We thought it was the Hound.

**JASPER**
(horrified)
The... Hound?

**HENRY**
(a small smile)
Anjali said it was the Hound's eye.

**JASPER**
(sighing)
Anjali.

**BERYL**
(softly)
Jasper. If you'd told them, they'd have helped.

*Jasper looks at her. He looks at his sheet. At the lamp.*

**JASPER**
(very quietly)
...I wanted to do it right. By myself.

**BERYL**
(Gently)
Actually... it's more fun with friends.

**ANIMATOR:** Beryl's "Actually" is a gentle nudge, not a correction. Keep her voice soft.

*Holmes steps forward and, a little awkwardly, offers Jasper her hand.*

**HOLMES**
We have a lot of lists in common.

**JASPER**
(accepting, a little stiffly)
...We do.

*They shake. The same upright pose. The same hand position. Watson and Beryl share a smile.*

**WATSON**
(softly)
You could have just asked us.

**HENRY**
Back home, we'd turn the light on and see what's there.

**JASPER**
(trying a tiny smile)
Well. Tonight it's on.

## ACT FOUR: THE LESSON (9:30)

### INT. THE HALL, ATTIC. NIGHT.

*Dark outside. Inside the attic: the lamp glows silver-white through the sheet. It is
beautiful. The room is lit with golden lantern light too, and a string of fairy lights
Watson has hung along the beams.*

*Holmes, Watson, Henry, Beryl, Anjali and Tavita sit on the floor with cocoa. Jasper stands
beside the sheet with his clipboard and a pencil.*

*A moth lands on the sheet. Then another. A soft, fluffy, golden one. Then a white one with
silver edges. Then a tiny brown one. They make small, wonderful patterns on the cloth.*

**JASPER**
(proudly, quietly)
Brown. Brown. White. Fluffy.

**HENRY**
(whispering)
They're beautiful.

*Jasper looks up. His face does something it hasn't done all episode: it softens.*

**JASPER**
(a small smile)
Thank you for coming up.

**TAVITA**
(whispering)
Can I count the fluffy ones?

**JASPER**
(blinking)
...Yes. You can count the fluffy ones.

**TAVITA**
(delighted)
I'M A SURVEYOR!

*He holds up his telescope. He accidentally points it at his own foot.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
...My foot is not a moth.

**EVERYONE**
(whispering, laughing)
Shh!

**ANIMATOR:** Outside the window, far below in the garden, a huge shaggy silhouette sits,
head tilted at the glowing window. A moth lands on his nose. He sneezes. A tiny golden puff.

**SFX:** *Achoo.*

*Jasper stops. He looks at the window. He tilts his head, then smiles.*

**JASPER**
(softly)
...I think someone's watching.

*Holmes puts a finger to her lips. Smiling.*

## "HOW HOLMES FIGURED IT OUT" (9:30 to 11:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Fairy lights. A cup of cocoa on the rug. The journal open between Holmes and Watson. Henry
leans in the doorway, hat with star sticker, half-asleep.*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Case journal, day thirty-six. Case closed. The light in the attic was not a hound. It was
a moth light.

**HOLMES**
Let's look at the clues.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame fills the screen.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**CLUE ONE: THE CLEAR NIGHTS**

*Watson's doodle of the three clear moons and the grey cloud replays and glows.*

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
The light shone on clear nights. Not on cloudy ones. Hounds don't care about clouds. But
moths do. Moths fly best when the sky is clear.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE TWO: THE SILVER DUST**

*The silvery dust on Holmes's finger glows in close-up.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
Silver dust on the windowsill. Beryl told us what it was: wing dust. Moths leave it
behind when they land. Lots of moths. Lots of dust.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE THREE: THE TALLY SHEET**

*Jasper's tidy tally sheet replays. The lines and headings glow.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A sheet of tally marks. Neat lines in groups of five. Brown. White. Fluffy. Someone was
counting moths. And only one person at camp counts in groups of five.

*A gold star, and next to it, a doodle of Jasper, labelled "J.S."*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(turning to Henry)
And you asked the best question of all.

**HENRY**
(sleepy)
Me?

**HOLMES**
"Do hounds care about clouds?"

**HENRY**
(smiling)
I didn't know the answer.

**HOLMES**
(grinning)
That's what makes it a good question.

*Henry beams. Watson writes, with a flourish: "ASK QUESTIONS. FIND OUT WHY."*

*He draws a heart. DING-DING-DING: three dings for "solved."*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Case closed. Hound cleared.

*Holmes adds one line to her chart on the wall: "THINGS THAT LIKE CLEAR NIGHTS: MOTHS."*

**HOLMES**
Curious... very curious!

**THE END.**

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **The attic lift.** To keep the Hall accessible, I added a small, slow, star-painted
   "cage lift." That's a new world detail. Okay to keep? (It would also help Watson in later
   episodes: the portrait gallery, the bell tower.)
2. **Holmes's "THINGS THAT GO BUMP" chart.** A running gag I added; she adds one line per
   episode. It's optional and easy to cut.
3. **The Camp Chief's note** ("Don't forget to tell your friends. -C.C.") is the lesson's
   gentle hook. It uses the "adults as notes" rule. OK?
4. **Clue 1** needs one extra step (moths prefer clear nights). I let Holmes say it in the
   recap rather than at the discovery, so the viewer can find the connection once they have
   the dust and the tally sheet. If it's too much for ages 5 to 8, I'd swap Clue 1 for
   "the light is a round, flat, white glow, not a pair of eyes."
5. **Anjali** is the one who calls the glow "the Hound's eye." Holmes doesn't trim her
   exaggeration directly in this episode; Henry's question does it indirectly. Do you want a
   direct Holmes line?
