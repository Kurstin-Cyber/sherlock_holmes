# EPISODE 4: "THE BOOTS THAT VANISHED"

*11 minutes · Script draft 1 · Stage 4*

**Spotlight:** Watson · **Lesson:** Teamwork (everyone has a job, and the plan works because of all of them)
**Clues:** (1) a white feather inside the one boot they find · (2) tiny webbed prints across the mud, spotted from above · (3) a faint "quack, quack" from the reeds
**Red herring:** Henry steps in the mud and loses his own boot ("The Squelch ate it!")
**Setting:** the Grimpen Squelch and the Squelch Lookout Deck

*Script key, look reminders and flags work as in `ep01-the-hound-comes-home.md`.*

**Optional thread:** The dining-room chalkboard reads **"CAMP DAY: 45 DAYS TO GO."**

**Safety rule for this episode:** The Squelch is **ankle-deep, warm, grey and silly.** Nobody sinks.
A painted measuring post says so. Show it early.

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00 to 1:00)

### EXT. BOARDWALK TRAIL, NEAR THE SQUELCH. MORNING.

*Bright sun. Purple heather. A line of campers marches along the Boardwalk, singing a nonsense
song. At the back, TAVITA FRANKLAND (huge hat, telescope) marches loudest, swinging his arms.*

*Ahead, the wooden path ends at a wide, flat, shiny grey meadow of mud with reeds around the
edge. A tall painted post stands at the edge of the mud. On it, a smiling cartoon boot and
the words: "THE GRIMPEN SQUELCH. Ankle deep! Squelch responsibly!" A line marks "ANKLE" at
exactly boot height.*

**ANIMATOR:** Establish the depth clearly in the first ten seconds. The post is the episode's
safety promise to the audience.

*ANJALI MORTIMER (cape, lantern, scarf) walks backwards in front of the line, like a
drum-major.*

**ANJALI**
(booming, quick)
Remember! The Hound is big, he's shaggy, and he wants a snack!

*The campers cheer: "SNACK!"*

*Tavita, singing, steps off the Boardwalk onto a stepping stone. The stone wobbles. His foot
goes down into the mud.*

**SFX:** *Schlorp.*

*He lifts his foot. It comes out bare. His boot stays behind. It slides, slowly, beneath the
mud, as though something pulled it. A tiny bubble goes blip.*

*Everyone stops. Silence. Tavita looks at his bare foot. He looks at the mud.*

**TAVITA**
(a whisper that isn't)
The Squelch... ate my boot.

*A small camper in the crowd whispers.*

**CAMPER**
It's the Hound!

*Anjali clutches her cape in delight.*

**ANJALI**
That's the fifth boot this week!

*Tavita sniffs the air, thinks, and points his telescope at the mud. He looks into the wrong
end.*

**TAVITA**
(muffled)
I can see my boot. It's very small.

*He turns the telescope round.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
...It's gone.

**CUT TO: TITLE CARD.** *Golden magnifier swirls. A tiny wobbly harmonica "woo."*

---

## ACT ONE: THE CASE ARRIVES (1:00 to 4:00)

### EXT. THE SQUELCH LOOKOUT DECK. MORNING.

*A round wooden platform at the very end of the Boardwalk, built by campers. A railing painted
with daisies. A little flag on a pole. A pulley with a rope and a bucket. A painted sign:
"SQUELCH CONTROL. Ring bell for service."*

*MATEO WATSON (teal hoodie, orange wheelchair with stickers, journal on his lap) sits at the
rail with a pair of binoculars on a strap. His chair bell is clipped to the railing. He's
quiet and happy, in his element.*

*IMANI HOLMES (cap, glasses, raincoat) arrives, a little out of breath. HENRY BASKERVILLE
(plaid, star-stickered hat) follows, ducking under the flag.*

**HOLMES**
Five boots. A whole week. Watson, we need the case journal.

**WATSON**
(opening it)
Already open.

*He holds up a page. It's a list of five boots, each with a tiny doodle and a name beside it.*

**JOURNAL (close-up):** *1. Minh Barrymore, green boot. 2. Jasper Stapleton, neat tan boot.
3. A small camper, red wellie. 4. Beryl Stapleton, lilac wellie. 5. Tavita Frankland, left
brown boot.*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Five boots. All lost at the edge of the Squelch. All on the left foot.

**HENRY**
(counting on his fingers)
Left, left, left, left, left.

**HOLMES**
(delighted)
Curious... very curious!

*She taps her magnifier twice. Then she looks at the mud. Then at the painted post. Then at
Watson. A small, slightly pained look crosses her face.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(frowning)
It's a lot of mud.

**WATSON**
(brightly)
It is. And I can see all of it from up here.

*He lifts the binoculars. A little wiggle of his shoulders: he's ready.*

**HOLMES**
(realising)
You can see more from up there than I can from down here!

**WATSON**
(grinning)
That's why it's called Squelch Control.

*DING-DING goes his bell, twice, happy.*

**ANIMATOR:** Watson is cheerful and capable. The deck is *his* place. No sad music, no pity
shots. If there is any "nobody can reach" tension, it belongs to the mud, not to him.

*A shout from the Boardwalk. Minh Barrymore (green apron, orange hearing aids) jogs up, one
bare foot and one boot, hair sticking up.*

**MINH**
(breathless)
Holmes! I found something!

*He holds out a muddy green boot, its sole flapping slightly.*

**MINH (CONT'D)**
It was caught on a reed at the very edge.

**HOLMES**
(taking it)
Your boot! One of the five.

**MINH**
(signing: "Look inside.")

**SUBTITLE:** *Look inside.*

*Holmes turns the boot upside down and shakes it. A tiny, soft, white feather drifts out,
slowly spinning in the air, and lands on her sleeve.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
A feather.

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the feather as it
floats and settles.*

**HENRY**
(squinting)
A white one.

**WATSON**
(writing)
Writing that down!

*He sketches the feather in the journal, tiny and perfect. Beside it: "WHO LOSES FEATHERS?"
and a star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows softly.*

**HENRY**
(thinking)
Does a hound lose feathers?

**HOLMES**
(a beat)
Henry. No. A hound loses fur.

*Henry nods, deeply pleased with himself. His hat star twinkles.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles the feather.*

## ACT TWO: THE INVESTIGATION (4:00 to 8:00)

### EXT. THE GRIMPEN SQUELCH, EDGE. LATE MORNING.

*Holmes, Henry and Tavita stand at the edge of the mud, in a row. Tavita now has a spare
boot on his right foot and nothing on his left. Watson watches from the deck above.*

*Holmes dips one toe into the Squelch. It's warm. She wiggles it.*

**HOLMES**
It's only ankle deep.

**TAVITA**
(proudly)
Everyone knows that! It says so on the post!

**HOLMES**
Then somebody is taking boots from an ankle-deep puddle.

**HENRY**
(a bit unsure)
Can I help?

**HOLMES**
You have the longest legs at camp. Take a big step to that stone.

*Henry steps, one foot onto a stone. It's slippery. His arms spin like windmills. He tips
forward, one foot slides, and the other slides, and...*

**SFX:** *SCHLORP!*

*Henry stands in mud up to his ankle, one foot with a boot, one foot without. His left boot is
gone. His hat star twinkles. His face is perfectly calm.*

**HENRY**
(a pause)
The Squelch ate my boot.

**TAVITA**
(delighted)
That's SIX!

**HENRY**
(sinking a little deeper, which is to say, an inch)
Back home, we'd call that "a pickle."

*Holmes grabs his arm and pulls. Henry's boot returns with a mighty *PLOP*, along with a
fountain of grey mud.*

**HENRY (CONT'D)**
(holding up the boot, triumphant)
It's not eaten! It was just stuck!

**ANIMATOR:** Keep it slapstick and silly, with spluttering sounds. The mud is warm and grey.
Nobody is in danger, and Henry is laughing.

**TAVITA**
(disappointed)
Oh. Never mind.

**HOLMES**
(drily)
So the Squelch only keeps boots that get stuck. It doesn't eat them.

**HENRY**
Then where did the five go?

*A pause. Holmes looks across the mud, a wide, silvery, empty meadow, edged with tall reeds.
She cannot see anything. She frowns.*

**HOLMES**
(softly)
I can't see far enough.

*From the deck, a DING. Watson holds up the binoculars.*

**WATSON**
(calling down)
Holmes! I can see.

### EXT. THE SQUELCH LOOKOUT DECK. CONTINUOUS.

*Watson's binoculars sweep slowly across the mud. We see through them: a circular, bobbing view
of silvery grey, and then, in the middle of it, tiny marks.*

*A trail of small prints leads from the edge of the mud, a long wandering line across the
Squelch, to the tall reeds on the far side. Each print is a tiny three-pointed star shape,
joined by faint webbing.*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
(excited, to himself)
Little prints. Webbed. Three toes. Lots of them.

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the trail of tiny
webbed prints across the mud, to the reeds.*

*He swings the binoculars to follow. The prints end at a thick clump of reeds.*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
(calling down, delighted)
HOLMES! They go to the reeds! A long trail of tiny webbed feet!

**HOLMES (O.S.)**
(thrilled)
Webbed feet? That's not a hound!

**HENRY (O.S.)**
(calling)
What has webbed feet and likes boots?

*Watson doodles: a three-toed webbed print, a star, and a question mark.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

*Then, faintly, from the reeds, drifts a soft sound.*

**SFX:** *Quack. Quack-quack. Quack.*

*Watson stops. He lowers the binoculars. He cups a hand to his ear. He grins.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline pulses around the reeds as the
sound ripples out.*

**WATSON**
(whispering, delighted)
Oh. Oh!

**ANIMATOR:** Show the quacks as gentle yellow sound-ripples drifting across the mud, so every
viewer can "see" the sound. A tiny, cheerful sound.

*Watson rings his bell three times. DING-DING-DING!*

---

## ACT THREE: THE PLAN (8:00 to 9:30)

### EXT. THE SQUELCH LOOKOUT DECK. LATER.

*The team has gathered on the deck: Holmes, Henry, Tavita, Anjali (cape, lantern), Minh.
Henry is trying to scrape mud off his boot. Tavita holds a lump of mud like a baby.*

*Watson has unrolled a large sheet of paper on a crate. It's a map of the Squelch,
beautifully drawn, with a grid, the reeds, the stones and little arrows.*

**WATSON**
(calmly, a natural leader)
Okay. I know where the boots are. They're in the reeds. And I think I know who put them
there. But we need everyone.

*He taps the map.*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
Holmes, you're the Spotter. You go out on the stepping stones, and look for boots.

**HOLMES**
(saluting)
Spotter.

**WATSON**
Henry, you have the longest arms. You're the Reach.

**HENRY**
(thrilled)
The Reach!

**WATSON**
Tavita, you're the strongest. You hold the rope.

**TAVITA**
(flexing, puffing his chest)
THE ROPE!

**WATSON**
Anjali, you tell the best stories. If there's a surprise in the reeds, I need someone to
calm it down with a lullaby.

**ANJALI**
(whispering, deeply moved)
A lullaby. I've been training for this all my life.

**MINH**
(signing)
And me?

**SUBTITLE:** *And me?*

**WATSON**
(signing back, carefully)
You find footprints.

**SUBTITLE:** *You find footprints.*

*Minh's face lights up. He gives a thumbs-up.*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
And I'll be Squelch Control. I see everything. I tell you where to go.

*He sets the binoculars on his lap and holds up the bell.*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
One ding for "stop." Two dings for "go." Three dings for "found it."

**ALL**
(together)
One for stop. Two for go. Three for found it!

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles the whole team, each person in turn.*

**HOLMES**
(to the team)
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**ALL**
(together, ragged and delighted)
Look closer! Notice more! Think it through!

**ANIMATOR:** This is the first time the whole team says the chant together. Make it noisy,
uneven and joyful.

### EXT. THE GRIMPEN SQUELCH. CONTINUOUS.

*Holmes steps carefully from stone to stone, arms out. Henry stands on the first stone, arms
stretched, holding a long pole. Tavita pays out the rope behind them. Anjali sings softly. Minh
walks the edge, eyes on the ground. Above them, Watson calls out directions.*

**WATSON (V.O.)**
Two dings, go left. One ding, stop. Holmes, there's a stone behind you.

*DING-DING. Holmes hops. DING. She freezes. DING-DING. She hops again.*

**WATSON (V.O.) (CONT'D)**
(quietly)
Slow. You're doing great.

*Holmes reaches the reeds. She parts them gently with one finger.*

*DING-DING-DING!*

---

## ACT FOUR: THE REVEAL (9:30)

### EXT. THE REEDS, FAR SIDE OF THE SQUELCH. CONTINUOUS.

*Hidden in the middle of the tall green reeds, a perfect little nest made of FIVE BOOTS. A
green boot, a tan boot, a red boot, a lilac boot, a brown boot. All lined with soft down and
twigs. In the middle of the nest, a mother duck sits proudly with eight tiny, fluffy
ducklings.*

*She looks at Holmes. She quacks, once, politely.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Oh.

*Anjali begins, very softly, to sing a lullaby. The ducklings snuggle in. The mother duck
blinks, long and slow.*

**ANIMATOR:** Warm gold light across the reeds. This is the emotional high point: gentle,
pretty, soft.

**HENRY**
(whispering, over her shoulder)
She made a nest out of our boots.

**HOLMES**
(softly, a tiny catch in her voice)
Boots are warm. And dry. And just the right size.

*She counts the boots. Five. She looks up at the deck, far away, where Watson is watching with
his binoculars. She raises her hand. He raises his. A tiny, friendly wave across the mud.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(calling softly)
Watson! You were right!

*DING-DING-DING comes the distant bell.*

---

## ACT FIVE: THE KINDNESS (9:30)

### EXT. THE SQUELCH LOOKOUT DECK. LATER.

*The team has gathered again on the deck. The boots have been left in the nest. Everyone is
thinking.*

**HENRY**
(sadly)
But Jasper and Beryl need their boots.

**TAVITA**
(sniffling)
And I need my boot.

**WATSON**
(thinking)
And the ducklings need a nest.

*He picks up his pencil. Draws, quickly. A small box with a round door. A little ramp. A tiny
pillow.*

**WATSON (CONT'D)**
We build her a nest box. Right here, by the reeds. A warm, dry house for ducks. And we give
her something soft for the inside.

**MINH**
(signing, grinning)
Old socks.

**SUBTITLE:** *Old socks.*

**TAVITA**
(delighted)
I HAVE SO MANY OLD SOCKS!

*A montage: the team builds a small wooden nest box from a crate. Tavita saws it, badly.
Anjali paints "DUCK HOUSE" on the front. Henry holds the lantern. Holmes measures. Minh sands
it smooth. Watson directs, ringing the bell, and rolling to the end of the deck to pass the
last tools. Everyone does their part.*

*A sock goes inside. Then five socks. Then fifty socks.*

*The mother duck waddles to the box and sniffs it. Then she steps in, circles twice and
settles down. Her ducklings tumble in after her, peeping.*

*She quacks, once, politely. She slides out of the boot-nest and, one by one, nudges the five
boots toward the water with her beak.*

**HENRY**
(gasping)
She's giving them back!

*The boots wash gently to the Squelch edge. Each one lands in the shallows with a happy plop.*

**ANIMATOR:** Let the boots drift into view one by one, a green boot, a tan boot, a red boot,
a lilac boot, a brown boot. Cheerful music. Nobody gets muddy except Tavita.

*Tavita whoops and pulls his boot on. It squelches. He grins.*

**TAVITA**
It's PERFECT!

---

## "HOW HOLMES FIGURED IT OUT" (9:30 to 11:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Fairy lights. Holmes and Watson on the floor, the journal between them. Five clean boots sit
in a row by the door, each with a tiny drawing on it. Henry dozes against a beanbag, with a
duckling-shaped sock puppet on his hand.*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Case journal, day forty-one. Case closed. The Squelch didn't eat the boots. A duck borrowed
them.

**HOLMES**
Let's look at the clues.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame fills the screen.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**CLUE ONE: THE FEATHER**

*The white feather replays, floating out of Minh's boot, glowing.*

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A white feather inside a lost boot. Hounds have fur. Feathers belong to birds.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE TWO: THE WEBBED PRINTS**

*Watson's binocular view replays: the tiny three-toed prints leading across the mud.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
Tiny prints with webbing between the toes. From high up, Watson could see the whole trail. I
couldn't see it from down in the mud.

*A gold star, and a tiny doodle of Watson with binoculars.*

**CLUE THREE: THE QUACKING**

*The quack ripples replay across the Squelch.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A soft quack, quack from the reeds. Feathers. Webbed feet. Quacking. There's only one
creature that fits.

**HENRY**
(sleepily, waking)
A duck.

**HOLMES**
A duck!

*A gold star, a heart, and a doodle of a mother duck wearing a boot.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(turning to Watson)
And I wouldn't have solved it without you.

**WATSON**
(shaking his head)
You wouldn't have solved it without *everyone*.

**HOLMES**
(grinning)
...Without everyone.

*Watson writes, with a flourish: "A CASE IS A TEAM." He draws six small stars, one for each
member. DING-DING-DING: three dings for "solved."*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Case closed. Hound cleared.

---

### EXT. THE SQUELCH, DUCK HOUSE. DAWN. (BUTTON)

*Soft golden light. The mother duck leads eight ducklings out of the Duck House, in a line,
down to the water. A very large, very shaggy dog sits at the edge of the Boardwalk, ears
flat, watching them waddle past.*

*The smallest duckling looks up at Moony. Moony looks at the duckling. Moony backs away, tail
between his legs, tiptoeing in reverse.*

**SFX:** *A tiny, trembling "woo."*

*The duckling quacks. Moony bolts. He disappears behind a bush, tail sticking out.*

**ANIMATOR:** Moony's afraid of ducks. A short, silly sight gag, and a reminder he's a big,
gentle goof, not a monster.

**THE END.**

## Complexity and logic check

- Each clue needs **one step**: feather means bird; webbed feet means water bird; quack
  means duck. Together they point to one animal.
- The solution (a duck collects boots) is visual: the nest of boots is the reveal image.

## Teamwork beat map (lesson shown, not told)

| Character | Job | What it takes |
|---|---|---|
| Watson | Squelch Control and planner | Sees the whole picture; directs by bell |
| Holmes | Spotter | Trusts a plan she didn't make |
| Henry | The Reach | Long arms, steady hands |
| Tavita | The Rope | Strength |
| Anjali | The Lullaby | A calm voice for a nervous duck |
| Minh | Footprints | Sharp eyes; signs with Watson |

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Watson's role.** I gave him a lot of the lead in this episode: he spots two of the three
   clues, makes the plan, and says the lesson. That fits his spotlight, but Holmes still gets
   the chant and her flaw (she forgot he could see more from above). Okay?
2. **The deck and the Boardwalk.** I'd like the Squelch Lookout Deck in the world bible as
   a fixed location, built by campers, with a bell, a pulley and a flag. Add it?
3. **Minh's signs** in this episode: "look inside," "and me?", "you find footprints," "old
   socks." A sign-language consultant is needed.
4. **The chant said by the whole team** in Act Three. It's the first time. Should the
   chant stay Holmes's, with others joining only in rare moments?
5. **Acts Four and Five are crowded** in the 9:30 window. If the timing pass shows the
   episode running long, I'd trim the montage first.
6. **Moony's fear of ducks** is a new trait for the bible (Moony: scared of small things). It
   fits his profile (scared of squirrels, wind, hats). OK to add ducks?
