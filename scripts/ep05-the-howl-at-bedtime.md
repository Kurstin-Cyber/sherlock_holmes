# EPISODE 5: "THE HOWL AT BEDTIME"

*11 minutes · Script draft 1 · Stage 4*

**Spotlight:** Minh · **Lesson:** Curiosity (find the cause before you fear the monster)
**Clues:** (1) the howl comes in puffs that match the wind (Minh feels it through the floor) · (2) it always comes from the east, the way the wind blows · (3) a loose, flapping cover on the old chimney pipe
**Red herring:** Tavita blames Moony; Moony is snoring in the boot room (a different sound entirely)
**Setting:** Bracken Cabin, the Hall's boot room, the East Terrace

*Script key, look reminders and flags work as in `ep01-the-hound-comes-home.md`.*

**Optional thread:** The dining-room chalkboard reads **"CAMP DAY: 40 DAYS TO GO."**

**Night-scene rule for this episode:** every night shot is lit by fairy lights, a night-light, a
lantern or the moon. No scene is dark. The "howl" is round, soft and slightly silly, like a
big friendly horn.

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00 to 1:00)

### INT. BRACKEN CABIN. NIGHT.

*A cosy wooden cabin with six bunks. Fairy lights are strung along the beams. A round night-light
shaped like a moon glows by the door. Outside, the wind is rising: the curtains breathe in and
out, and a branch taps the window.*

*HENRY BASKERVILLE (pyjamas with tiny canoes, star-stickered hat) is tucked into the bottom bunk,
the bottle cap and the photo of Dad and Hana on his pillow. ZAID SELDEN (6, his big yellow
raincoat hung on the bedpost, stuffed rabbit TUT under his chin) is in the next bunk. ANJALI
MORTIMER (curly hair in a silk night-scarf) sits cross-legged on the top bunk with a torch under
her chin.*

**ANJALI**
(whispering, theatrical)
Remember! The Hound is big, he's shaggy, and he wants a snack!

**HENRY**
(sleepily)
You said that at breakfast.

**ANJALI**
It bears repeating.

*She clicks off the torch. Zaid gives Tut a squeeze. The wind pushes the curtains. A long
silence. Then, from somewhere outside and above, comes a low, round, wobbly sound.*

**SFX:** *"Wooooo-oo-oooooo."*

*Henry sits up. His hat slides over his eyes. Anjali's mouth makes a perfect O.*

**ANJALI (CONT'D)**
(a thrilled gasp)
The Hound!

**ZAID**
(very small, matter-of-fact, from under Tut)
That's not Moony. He's asleep.

*Anjali and Henry turn to look at him. Zaid pulls Tut up over his nose.*

**ANJALI**
(whispering, impressed)
Then what is it?

*The howl comes again, longer this time: "Wooooo-oo-oo-oo-ooooo." Three more campers sit up
in their bunks and pull their blankets to their chins.*

**ANIMATOR:** The howl is round, low and soft, a bit like blowing over the top of a big
bottle. Show it as pale gold ripples drifting past the window, never as a dark shape. The
moon-light stays on.

**CUT TO: TITLE CARD.** *Golden magnifier swirls. A tiny wobbly harmonica "woo."*

**⚠️ FLAG (cold open):** Night, wind and a howl. Fix: fairy lights, a moon night-light, cosy
bunks, friends together, and Zaid's deadpan "That's not Moony" turns the beat funny at once.

## ACT ONE: THE CASE ARRIVES (1:00 to 4:00)

### INT. THE HALL, DINING ROOM. MORNING.

*Bright sun through tall windows. Porridge. The campers are very sleepy: heads on arms, spoons
in the air, one camper asleep face-down in a bowl.*

*LINH and MINH BARRYMORE (matching aprons, her yellow headband, his orange hearing aids) carry a
pot between them. Linh yawns loudly. Minh blinks slowly.*

*IMANI HOLMES (cap, glasses, raincoat) and MATEO WATSON (teal hoodie, orange chair) arrive with
the journal. Holmes is wide awake, and glowing.*

**HOLMES**
Good morning, everybody! Why is everybody asleep?

**LINH**
(a yawn the size of a cave)
Three nights of howling.

**HOLMES**
(delighted)
Three nights! Watson, write that down.

**WATSON**
(writing, gently)
Um. They look really tired.

**HOLMES**
(not hearing)
A mystery!

*Minh sets down the pot and wipes his hands on his apron. He walks to Holmes. He signs
carefully, then speaks.*

**MINH**
(signing, then aloud)
I think I can help.

**SUBTITLE:** *I think I can help.*

**MINH (CONT'D)**
I don't hear the howl first. I feel it.

*He lays his palm flat on the wooden table. The camera pushes in on his hand. A gold ripple
spreads from his fingers across the wood.*

**MINH (CONT'D)**
It comes through the floor. It goes: (he taps) mmm... mmm... mmmm. A little hum. And then
the howl.

**HOLMES**
(fascinated)
You feel it before you hear it?

**MINH**
(shrugging)
The floor tells me first.

*Holmes looks at Minh's hand on the table. A brief, genuine "oh" crosses her face.*

**HOLMES**
Minh. You are the best witness we have.

*Minh blinks. His ears go pink, and a tiny smile creeps onto his face.*

**MINH**
(deadpan)
I know.

*Linh rolls her eyes, but she is smiling.*

**HOLMES**
Tonight, you come with us.

*DING-DING goes Watson's bell, twice, happy.*

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. EVENING.

*Dusk, gold and rose, through the window. Holmes is at her wall chart, "THINGS THAT GO BUMP."
Watson is at his desk. Minh sits on the floor next to Henry, who is holding his bobble hat in
both hands.*

**HENRY**
(a small voice)
Back home, if we heard a noise at night, we'd check on our dog, Biscuit.

**HOLMES**
(snapping her fingers)
Good thinking! Moony sleeps in the boot room on windy nights. Dogs make noises. We check him first.

*She taps her magnifier twice.*

**HOLMES**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles the room. It lingers on Minh.*

*Watson opens the journal to a clean page and writes a title: "THE HOWL." He turns to Minh.*

**WATSON**
(signing, slowly: "Tell me what it feels like.")

**SUBTITLE:** *Tell me what it feels like.*

*Minh thinks. He holds out his hand, palm up, and moves it like waves.*

**MINH**
It's not loud. It's soft. It rises and falls. Like breathing. Like... (he searches) like when
the wind pushes on a door.

*Watson draws it: a sleepy wave with a tiny face. Next to it, a gust of wind.*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Writing that down!

*Outside the window, the wind picks up. The curtains puff out. The fairy lights sway.*

## ACT TWO: THE INVESTIGATION (4:00 to 8:00)

### INT. THE HALL, UPPER LANDING. NIGHT.

*A long wooden landing with a polished floor. Moonlight pours through tall windows, silvery and
soft. Lanterns hang from hooks. Tiny glowing night-lights run along the skirting boards like a
path of stars.*

*Holmes, Watson, Henry and Minh stand together. Watson holds his chair's flashlight, a warm
pool of gold. Henry carries a lantern. Minh has taken off his shoes, and stands in socks.*

**ANIMATOR:** Lit, golden, friendly. The night-lights make the hall look like a starry
runway. Show the kids close together.

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Wait for it.

*They wait. The wind whistles gently outside. The curtains shiver.*

*Minh crouches and lays both palms on the floorboards. His face goes still, calm and
concentrated.*

*A gold ripple moves along the boards from the east end of the landing, slow and soft. It
rises and falls, rises and falls, like breathing.*

**MINH**
(low)
Here it comes.

*He holds up a finger. Waits. Then:*

**MINH (CONT'D)**
Now.

**SFX:** *"Wooooo-oo-oooo."*

*The howl drifts down the corridor, the same slow shape as the ripple.*

**MINH (CONT'D)**
(opening his eyes)
The floor first. Then the howl.

*Beat. The wind outside dies. The ripple fades.*

*Silence.*

*Then the wind picks up again.*

*The ripple.*

*Minh nods.*

**MINH (CONT'D)**
Floor. Howl.

*The wind falls again. No ripple. No howl.*

**WATSON**
(quietly, eyes wide)
When the wind blows, it howls. When the wind stops, it stops.

**HOLMES**
(whispering, delighted)
It only howls on windy nights.

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the wind, the ripple and
the howl in a row.*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Wind... ripple... howl.

*He doodles three small pictures in a row, with arrows between them: a gust, a wave, a "woo."
Under it, a star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows softly.*

**HENRY**
(whispering)
Do hounds care about the wind?

**HOLMES**
(a pause)
Henry. That's an excellent question.

**HENRY**
(worried)
Is it?

**HOLMES**
It's brilliant. We'll find out.

*Henry's hat star twinkles.*

### INT. THE HALL, BOOT ROOM. NIGHT.

*A cosy room by the back door: pegs of coats, rows of boots, a rack of umbrellas, a big squashy
basket. A round night-light glows on the shelf.*

*Holmes pushes the door open. Everyone peers in.*

*TAVITA FRANKLAND (hat, telescope, pyjama shirt) is tiptoeing between the boot racks in
slippers, whispering loudly.*

**TAVITA**
(whispering, much too loud)
I FOUND THE HOWLING!

*Everyone freezes. From the basket in the corner comes a deep, rhythmic rumble.*

**SFX:** *Hrrrrr... pshhh. Hrrrrr... pshhh.*

*Tavita points his telescope at the basket. He whispers, tragically.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
It's Moony. He's making the howl noise!

*The basket shifts. A huge shaggy head rolls over. One floppy ear. One up. A pink tongue
hangs out. His eyes are shut. A bubble forms at his nose, inflates, and pops.*

**SFX:** *Hrrrrr... pshhh.*

*MOONY is fast asleep, on his back, paws in the air, a sock draped over his nose.*

**HOLMES**
(softly)
That's not howling. That's snoring.

*She turns to Minh.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Is that the same?

*Minh crouches. He puts his palm on the floor next to the basket. A different ripple: short and
snuffly, in and out, in and out, a small bubble-shaped one.*

**MINH**
(shaking his head, smiling)
Different. This is a bubble. The howl is a wave.

**HOLMES**
(to Tavita)
Moony's innocent.

**TAVITA**
(slowly, lowering the telescope)
Oh. Never mind.

*Everyone laughs, quietly, so as not to wake Moony. Moony's tail thumps once in his sleep.*

**ANIMATOR:** Keep the scene sweet and funny. The sock on Moony's nose rises and falls with each
snore. Do not add any menace.

### EXT. THE HALL, EAST TERRACE. NIGHT.

*A flat stone balcony at the east end of the Hall, with a thick, safe stone railing and
lanterns hung on hooks. Beyond it, silver heather hills under a huge, kind moon. A weathervane
shaped like a rooster spins on the tower above, creaking.*

*The four friends step out. The wind is stronger here, full of glittering mist. Henry holds on
to his hat.*

*Holmes licks a finger and holds it up. She turns slowly.*

**HOLMES**
East. The wind is blowing from the east.

*The weathervane rooster points east, trembling. The wind chimes on the lantern hooks swing the
same way.*

*From the end of the terrace, where the stone wall meets the tower, comes the sound.*

**SFX:** *"Wooooo-oo-ooooo."*

*It is close now. Rounder. Warmer. Everyone looks in the same direction.*

**WATSON**
(slowly)
It comes from the east.

**HOLMES**
The same way as the wind.

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the weathervane, the wind
and the direction of the sound in a single golden arrow.*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Wind from the east. Howl from the east.

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle: a rooster weathervane with an arrow pointing east, and a "woo" at the
end. A star.*

**HENRY**
(a small voice)
...It sounds like a ferry horn.

*Everyone turns to him. Henry is looking out over the heather. His face is soft.*

**HENRY (CONT'D)**
At home, when it's foggy, the ferries blow a long, low horn. Wooooo. Just like that. I
thought... I thought it was strange. But I know that sound.

*Holmes opens her mouth. She closes it. She looks at Watson. Watson gives the tiniest
encouraging nod.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly, finding it)
...You miss it.

**HENRY**
(a wobbly smile)
A bit.

*Holmes puts her hand on Henry's shoulder, a little awkwardly.*

**HOLMES**
It sounds like home. That's... not scary. That's nice.

*Henry's face lights up. DING-DING from Watson's bell.*

**ANIMATOR:** Holmes manages a small, clumsy moment of kindness here. She notices *after* Watson
nudges her, as always. Keep the beat brief.

## ACT THREE: THE REVEAL (8:00 to 9:30)

### EXT. THE HALL, EAST TERRACE. A MOMENT LATER.

*Minh walks slowly along the terrace wall, one hand trailing the stone. He stops at the corner.
At knee-height, sticking out of the wall, is an old brass pipe, as thick as a drainpipe, with a
rusty round cap over the end. The cap is loose. It flaps back and forth on a single hinge in the
wind.*

*Each time it flaps, the air curls over the open end of the pipe, and the pipe sings.*

**SFX:** *Flap. Flap. "Wooooo-oo-oo-ooo."*

*Minh crouches. He puts a palm flat on the pipe. His eyes go wide.*

**MINH**
(low)
It's humming. Here.

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the loose cap, the
flapping hinge and the pipe's mouth.*

*Holmes joins him, magnifier out.*

**HOLMES**
(quietly)
A chimney pipe. An old one. With a loose cover.

**WATSON**
(rolling up, writing)
Writing that down!

*He sketches the pipe and its flapping cap, and adds a tiny "WOO" above it, with a star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

*Holmes stands. Her eyes dart from the wind to the pipe, to the weathervane, to the floor.*

**HOLMES**
(slowly)
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles the loose cap.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Clue one: the howl comes on windy nights. Clue two: it comes from the east, the same way
the wind blows. Clue three: a loose cap on an old pipe.

*She holds up three fingers. She grins.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
When the wind blows across the pipe, the cap flaps and the pipe sings. It's not a hound. It's
a hooter.

**HENRY**
(delighted)
Like blowing across the top of a bottle!

**HOLMES**
Exactly like that.

*Minh holds out his hand to the pipe. The ripple pulses through his palm. His face is calm.*

**MINH**
(quietly)
I knew it wasn't a monster. Monsters don't hum.

**HOLMES**
(sincerely)
Thank you, Minh. We'd never have found it without you.

*Minh gives a small, proud nod. DING-DING.*

## ACT FOUR: THE LESSON (9:30)

### EXT. THE HALL, EAST TERRACE. A LITTLE LATER.

*Zaid, in his yellow raincoat over pyjamas, has come up, holding Tut. Anjali follows, torch
under her chin. Linh and Tavita peer from the doorway.*

**ANJALI**
(hushed, thrilled)
So it was NOT the Hound.

**HOLMES**
It was a pipe.

**ANJALI**
(deflating, then perking up)
A haunted pipe!

**HOLMES**
A windy pipe.

**ANJALI**
(sighing, happily)
A beautiful, windy pipe.

*Minh turns to the group. He signs, and speaks.*

**MINH**
(signing, then aloud)
We could fix it.

**SUBTITLE:** *We could fix it.*

**LINH**
(from the doorway, yawning)
Fix it? But it's so... pretty.

**HENRY**
(a quiet voice)
Can we keep the sound? Just softer?

*Everyone looks at Henry. Then at each other.*

**WATSON**
(smiling)
We could tie the cap so it doesn't flap. Then the wind makes a long, low hum. Not a wobble.

**MINH**
(nodding)
Like a lullaby.

*A montage, quick and warm: Watson ties the cap with a loop of ribbon. Minh adjusts it by feel,
palm on the pipe. Holmes holds the lantern. Tavita holds the ribbon (badly) and gets it wrong
twice. Linh adds a yellow bow. Zaid pats the pipe.*

*The wind blows. The pipe hums: a long, low, soft, gentle note, like a ferry horn far across
the water.*

**SFX:** *"Hooooooooooo..."*

*Everyone goes quiet. Henry closes his eyes. His smile is wide and wet-eyed.*

**HENRY**
(softly)
That's it. That's exactly it.

---

### INT. BRACKEN CABIN. NIGHT. (BUTTON)

*Fairy lights. The moon night-light. The curtains puff softly. The campers are all in their
bunks, fast asleep. Henry hugs his hat. Zaid hugs Tut. Anjali snores, upside down, one foot in
a boot.*

*The wind blows. The pipe hums its gentle ferry horn from far away.*

*From the Hall's boot room, a second, tiny sound drifts out across the lawn. A sleepy,
satisfied, wobbly "woo," in harmony.*

**ANIMATOR:** Moony "sings along" in his sleep, a soft golden note that wafts out of the boot
room window and curls around the pipe's tone. It is the one time Moony's howl is part of the
sound, and it is a lullaby.

**THE END.**

## "HOW HOLMES FIGURED IT OUT" (9:30 to 11:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Fairy lights. The wall chart. Holmes and Watson on the floor with the journal. Minh sits
cross-legged on a cushion, a mug of cocoa in his hands, his orange hearing aids glinting.*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Case journal, day forty-six. Case closed. The howl at bedtime was a very windy pipe.

**HOLMES**
Let's look at the clues.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame fills the screen.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**CLUE ONE: WIND, RIPPLE, HOWL**

*Watson's doodle of the gust, the wave and the "woo" replays and glows.*

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
The howl only came when the wind blew. Minh felt it through the floor, first the ripple, then
the howl. When the wind stopped, it stopped. A hound would howl whenever he liked. This only
happened in the wind.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE TWO: THE EAST WIND**

*The weathervane rooster and the golden arrow replay.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
The wind blew from the east, and the howl came from the east. Same way. They were connected.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE THREE: THE LOOSE CAP**

*The brass pipe replays, its cap flapping, the pipe humming.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
A loose cap on an old chimney pipe. When the wind blew across it, the cap flapped and the
pipe sang. Not a monster. Just air.

*A gold star, a heart, and a tiny doodle of the pipe with a bow.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(turning to Minh)
You felt it before any of us heard it.

**MINH**
(shrugging, smiling)
The floor told me.

**HOLMES**
(sincerely)
That's the best kind of clue.

*Minh's smile grows.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
And Henry knew the sound. He just hadn't said so.

**WATSON**
(gently)
He knew it was home.

*A tender beat. Holmes looks at the journal.*

**HOLMES**
(grinning)
Curious... very curious!

*Watson writes, with a flourish: "FIND THE CAUSE. MEET THE SOUND." He draws a heart. DING-DING-DING:
three dings for "solved."*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Case closed. Hound cleared.

*Holmes adds one line to her wall chart: "THINGS THAT GO BUMP: WIND IN A PIPE." Beneath it, in
smaller writing: "...AND SOMETIMES A LULLABY."*

## Complexity and logic check

- **Clue 1** is visible (the ripple and the gust shown together) and needs one step: "only on
  windy nights" means wind is involved.
- **Clue 2** is visible (the weathervane arrow) and needs one step: "same direction as the
  wind" means they're connected.
- **Clue 3** is the answer shown. Holmes then gives a simple image: "like blowing across a
  bottle."

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Zaid's line** is now a deadpan "That's not Moony. He's asleep." It is a joke about the
   secret everyone present already knows, not a slip.
2. **Henry's dog Biscuit** (at home) is new. It gives Holmes a reason to check on Moony in the
   boot room, and it deepens Henry's homesickness. Keep Biscuit as a fixed detail of his
   family?
3. **"It sounds like a ferry horn"** is the heart of the episode and comes from Henry's habit of
   comparing everything to home. Keep?
4. **The East Terrace** is a new location (a flat stone balcony on the Hall's east side, with a
   thick railing). It keeps the high-up sequence safe. Add it to the bible?
5. **Moony sings in his sleep** after the case is solved. He is never a suspect in this
   episode, so it is a wink, not a blame. Is it too close to the line?
