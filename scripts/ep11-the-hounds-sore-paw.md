# EPISODE 11: "THE HOUND'S SORE PAW"

*11 minutes · Script draft 1 · Stage 4*

**Spotlight:** Zaid and Moony · **Lesson:** Kindness (asking for help is brave, and helping gently is kind)
**Clues:** (1) a soft whimper, not a howl · (2) a prickly burr stuck in the heather by the den · (3) Moony keeps holding up one front paw
**Red herring:** Tavita thinks Moony has a bellyache from too many sandwiches
**Setting:** the Hall's boot room, the Stone Circle den

*Script key, look reminders and flags work as in `ep01-the-hound-comes-home.md`.*

**Optional thread:** The dining-room chalkboard reads **"CAMP DAY: 10 DAYS TO GO."**

**The one exception:** In this episode the Hound really is part of the mystery. He is not a culprit.
He is a friend who needs help. Everything stays gentle: a small burr, a soft whimper, a quick fix.
No blood, no real hurt, and Moony is comforted and cheerful within the episode.

---

## COLD OPEN (0:00 to 1:00)

### INT. THE HALL, BOOT ROOM. MORNING.

*A cosy room by the back door: pegs of coats, rows of boots, a rack of umbrellas, a big squashy
basket in the corner. Sunlight slants through a small round window. A moon night-light sits on
the shelf, switched off for the day.*

*ANJALI MORTIMER (cape, lantern, curls) is giving a sleepy morning tour to two small campers.*

**ANJALI**
(booming, but gentle, it's morning)
Remember! The Hound is big, he's shaggy, and he wants a snack.

*The small campers nod, still half asleep. Anjali opens the boot-room door a crack and steps in.
She stops.*

*From the basket in the corner comes a sound. Not a howl. Not a snore. A tiny, wobbly,
whimpering noise.*

**SFX:** *"Wh... wh-woo... woo."*

*Anjali's face falls. She tiptoes to the basket.*

*MOONY is lying curled up, head on his front paws, his big eyes open. His tail gives a small
thump. He tries to stand, and stops. He lifts his front left paw an inch off the floor. He puts
it down. He lifts it again.*

*TAVITA FRANKLAND appears in the doorway, telescope raised, hat flapping.*

**TAVITA**
(a hushed whisper, which for once actually is hushed)
It's the Hound...

*He looks at Moony. His voice drops.*

**TAVITA (CONT'D)**
He's limping.

*A long, quiet, golden beat. Moony looks up at them and lets out another small, sad "woo."*

**CUT TO: TITLE CARD.** *Golden magnifier swirls. A tiny wobbly harmonica "woo," this time a
little softer.*

**⚠️ FLAG (cold open):** An animal in distress. Fix: Moony is warm and safe in his cosy basket,
in bright morning sun; the whimper is small; his tail is still thumping; and the friends arrive
within seconds.

## ACT ONE: THE CASE ARRIVES (1:00 to 4:00)

### INT. THE HALL, BOOT ROOM. MOMENTS LATER.

*A little crowd has gathered: IMANI HOLMES (cap with flaps tied up, golden-blonde loc buns
poking out, glasses, raincoat), MATEO WATSON (teal hoodie, orange chair), HENRY BASKERVILLE
(plaid, star-stickered hat), Anjali and Tavita. Everyone is crouched around the basket,
whispering.*

*ZAID SELDEN (yellow raincoat, stuffed rabbit Tut) is not there.*

**HENRY**
(whispering)
Where's Zaid?

**WATSON**
(gently)
He's usually the first one here.

*Holmes kneels by the basket. She is quieter than usual. She looks closely at Moony, who
looks back with big brown eyes.*

**HOLMES**
(softly, to Moony)
What's the matter, Moony?

*Moony lifts his front left paw an inch. He puts it down. He sighs. Sparkles puff softly from
his nose.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(to the others, whispering)
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles Moony, very gently.*

*Holmes leans in. She listens. The whimper comes again, and she tilts her head.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(slowly)
That's not a howl.

**HENRY**
(whispering)
It sounds different.

**HOLMES**
It sounds smaller. Sadder. A howl is a happy sound for Moony. This is something else.

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline shows two sound ripples side by
side: a big, round, golden one (the happy "woo") and a small, wobbly, pale-blue one (the
whimper).*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Two sounds. A happy one and a sad one.

*He sketches two ripples side by side. A star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

**TAVITA**
(thinking hard, hand on belly)
Maybe it's his tummy.

**ANJALI**
(gasping)
Too many sandwiches!

**TAVITA**
(sadly)
I gave him four.

**HOLMES**
(drily)
Four?

**TAVITA**
(quickly)
...Two. Three. Two and a half.

*Everyone looks at Tavita. He shrinks into his hat.*

**ANIMATOR:** This is the red herring and it is played gently. Tavita is genuinely worried and
a bit guilty; nobody is cross.

*Moony thumps his tail. He gives Tavita a lick on the hand. He holds up his front left paw
again.*

**WATSON**
(softly)
Holmes. He keeps lifting that paw.

**HOLMES**
(quickly)
I saw. I'm still thinking.

*She taps her magnifier twice, but her face is serious.*

### INT. THE HALL, BOOT ROOM. LATER.

*The little crowd has left to get breakfast, leaving Holmes, Watson and Henry. Moony has gone
back to sleep. Watson is writing.*

**WATSON**
Case journal, day seventy-six. Something's wrong with Moony. We don't know what.

**HENRY**
Maybe we should ask Zaid. He knows Moony best.

**HOLMES**
(snapping her fingers)
Good thinking, Henry!

**HENRY**
(blushing, proud)
Back home, we'd ask the person who feeds the dog.

*Holmes grabs her cap. Watson rolls out the door. Henry follows. The door swings shut.*

*In the quiet room, Moony opens one eye. He sighs, and holds up his paw to the empty air.*

## ACT TWO: THE INVESTIGATION (4:00 to 8:00)

### EXT. THE BOARDWALK TRAIL, NEAR THE STONE CIRCLE. MORNING.

*Bright sun. Purple heather. The three friends hurry along the Boardwalk toward the Stone
Circle. In the distance, a small figure in a yellow raincoat crouches among the heather.*

**HENRY**
(pointing)
There he is!

*They slow down and approach quietly. ZAID SELDEN is kneeling by the entrance to Moony's den,
a cosy hideaway under the biggest stone, hung with a blanket and a few fairy lights. He's
muttering to Tut.*

**ZAID**
(very quietly)
I looked everywhere, Tut. I can't find it.

*He parts a patch of heather and peers into it. He pulls out a handful of grass. He sighs.*

**HOLMES**
(gently)
Zaid?

*Zaid jumps. He swings round, wide-eyed, and hides Tut behind his back.*

**ZAID**
(whispering)
I was just... looking at the flowers.

*Holmes opens her mouth. She looks at Watson. She looks at Zaid's anxious face.*

**HOLMES**
(carefully, trying)
Are you looking for something?

*Zaid says nothing. His chin wobbles.*

**WATSON**
(very gently)
Zaid. Moony isn't well. We saw him. We want to help.

*Zaid's eyes fill. He looks at the ground, then at the sky, then at Tut.*

**ZAID**
(a tiny voice)
I didn't know who to ask.

*Holmes kneels down so she's eye to eye with him.*

**HOLMES**
(quietly)
You can ask us.

*A long pause. Zaid takes a breath. Then, in a small voice, but very clearly:*

**ZAID**
Please... can you help Moony?

**ALL THREE**
(together, softly)
Yes.

*Zaid throws his arms round Holmes. She goes rigid, then pats his back.*

**ANIMATOR:** The moment is warm and simple. Hold the hug. Do not undercut it with a joke.

### EXT. MOONY'S DEN. MOMENTS LATER.

*Zaid leads them to the den. Under the biggest stone, a cosy hollow hung with fairy lights,
a rug and a bowl labelled "MOONY" in crayon. Beside it is a patch of heather with prickly
brown balls stuck to the stems.*

*Holmes crouches. She sweeps her magnifier slowly over the heather. Her eyes sharpen.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
Burrs.

*Her magnifier hovers over a round, brown, spiky ball, no bigger than a gooseberry, caught on
a heather stem. Beside it, a few matted grey hairs.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the spiky burr and the
grey hairs on the stem.*

**HENRY**
(peering)
What are they?

**HOLMES**
Prickly seed balls. They stick to anything that brushes past.

**WATSON**
(writing)
Burrs. And grey fur.

*He doodles a spiky ball with a tuft of grey hair attached. A star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

**ZAID**
(very quietly)
Moony walks through the heather. Every day.

**HOLMES**
(softly)
So he brushed against it.

*A thought crosses her face. She turns slowly.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Zaid, was Moony holding up his paw this morning?

**ZAID**
(nodding, with a tiny sniff)
Yesterday, too.

*Henry gasps softly.*

**HENRY**
(worried)
Is it his paw?

### INT. THE HALL, BOOT ROOM. LATER.

*The five of them (Holmes, Watson, Henry, Zaid, Tavita) are crowded round Moony's basket again.
Moony is propped up on a cushion. Tut sits beside him. Tavita has brought a bowl of water, which
Moony ignores.*

*Holmes sits cross-legged in front of Moony. She holds out a hand.*

**HOLMES**
(softly)
Moony. May I see?

*Moony looks at her. He looks at Zaid. Zaid gives a small nod.*

*Slowly, Moony lifts his front left paw and places it in Holmes's hand.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 1st]** *Chime. Sparkle. The glowing outline traces the held-up paw.*

**ANIMATOR:** Moony is trusting, gentle and brave. He does not growl, snap or flinch. He looks
a little embarrassed and a lot hopeful.

*Holmes turns the paw over very gently. She peers between the toes with her magnifier. Her
breath catches.*

*Between two of his soft toe pads, tucked deep in the fur, is a small, spiky, brown ball.*

**HOLMES**
(whispering)
There.

**HENRY**
(whispering)
A burr.

**WATSON**
(writing)
Burr in the paw.

*He draws a paw print with a tiny spiky ball between the toes. A star.*

**[JOURNAL]** *The doodle glows.*

**HOLMES**
(quietly, to herself)
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**[CHANT]** *The golden magnifier frame circles Moony's paw.*

## ACT THREE: THE REVEAL (8:00 to 9:30)

### INT. THE HALL, BOOT ROOM. CONTINUOUS.

*Holmes lifts her head. Her eyes are shining.*

**HOLMES**
Three clues. Clue one: Moony's sound is a small whimper, not a happy howl. Clue two: burrs in
the heather by his den. Clue three: he keeps holding up one paw.

*She turns to the group.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
He's got a burr stuck in his paw. It's small, and it's prickly, and it hurts when he walks.

**TAVITA**
(looking relieved, then guilty)
So it wasn't the sandwiches?

**HOLMES**
It wasn't the sandwiches.

**TAVITA**
(a tiny voice)
Good. And also... I'm sorry about the sandwiches.

*Moony licks his hand. Tavita sniffs hard.*

**ANJALI**
(arriving with a lantern, whispering)
Is he going to be alright?

**HOLMES**
(gently)
He's going to be fine. We just need to take it out. Gently.

*She looks round. Her face is serious and kind.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
I need everyone's help.

**ANIMATOR:** This is a quiet team moment, not a loud one. Everyone has a small job, and
Holmes is the one who speaks softly.

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Zaid, you hold his head. He trusts you most.

*Zaid kneels by Moony's head and strokes his ears. Moony leans into him.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Henry, hold the paw very still. You have the steadiest hands.

*Henry kneels by the paw and holds it carefully.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Anjali, tell him a story. A calm one.

**ANJALI**
(softly, theatrical, but gentle)
Once upon a time, in a land of heather and stones, there lived a great big hound with the
softest paws in the world...

*Moony's ears twitch. His eyes half close.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Watson, you're on timing. Tell me when he's calm.

**WATSON**
(softly, watching)
Calm... calmer... calm. Now.

*Holmes washes her hands in the bowl of warm water, dries them, and very slowly reaches in
between his toes with two fingertips.*

*Everyone holds their breath.*

*A small, soft roll. The burr comes free. She holds it up on her palm: a tiny, spiky brown
ball. It's no bigger than a gooseberry.*

*Moony blinks. He looks at his paw. He puts it down. He puts weight on it. He looks surprised.*

*His tail begins to thump. Faster. Faster. His whole back end wiggles.*

**MOONY**
(a joyful, wobbly, golden)
Woo-oo-oo!

*Everyone cheers softly. Tavita whoops, and puts a hand over his own mouth. Zaid throws his
arms round Moony's neck. Moony licks his face from chin to forehead.*

**ANIMATOR:** Golden music notes spiral from Moony's mouth. This is the emotional high point.
Hold it. Let the relief breathe.

## ACT FOUR: THE LESSON (9:30)

### INT. THE HALL, BOOT ROOM. LATER.

*A bath. A big copper tub in the middle of the boot room, full of bubbles. Moony sits in it,
looking dignified and slightly confused, with a heap of bubbles on his head like a crown. Linh
and Minh (aprons rolled up) wash his back with big sponges. Tavita holds a towel the size of
a blanket. Anjali sings in the background.*

*Zaid sits on the edge of the tub, holding Tut. He's smiling, a real, wide, gap-toothed
smile.*

**ZAID**
(shyly, to Holmes)
Thank you.

**HOLMES**
(gruffly)
You asked.

**ZAID**
(a pause, then bravely)
It was hard.

**HOLMES**
(gently)
I know.

*Watson's bell goes DING-DING.*

**WATSON**
(softly)
Asking for help is brave.

**ZAID**
(slowly, testing it)
...Asking is brave.

*Moony shakes his whole body. Bubbles fly everywhere. Everyone shrieks with delight. A bubble
lands on Holmes's cap. She doesn't move. She lets it sit there.*

### EXT. THE STONE CIRCLE DEN. EVENING. (BUTTON)

*Golden hour. Zaid and Moony sit side by side in front of the den, watching the sunset. Moony
has a soft grey sock wrapped round his front left paw, tied with a bow.*

*Moony looks at the sock. He sniffs it. He looks delighted.*

**ZAID**
(whispering)
It's a bandage. But it's also a sock.

**MOONY**
(a soft, happy)
Woo.

*Zaid leans against him. Tut sits on his lap. The sky turns pink.*

**ANIMATOR:** This is the quiet end of the story. Slow music. No gag.

**THE END.**

## "HOW HOLMES FIGURED IT OUT" (9:30 to 11:00)

### INT. HEATHER CABIN. NIGHT.

*Fairy lights. The wall chart. Holmes and Watson on the floor with the journal. A grey sock is
drawn on the cover of the journal, with a tiny bow. On the wall chart, in neat letters:
"THINGS THAT GO BUMP."*

**WATSON**
(reading)
Case journal, day seventy-six. Case closed. Moony had a burr in his paw.

**HOLMES**
Let's look at the clues.

**[CHANT]** *Golden magnifier frame fills the screen.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
Look closer. Notice more. Think it through.

**CLUE ONE: THE WHIMPER**

*The two sound ripples replay: the happy golden "woo" and the small blue whimper.*

**[CLUE ALERT #1, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
Moony's happy howl is big and round. This was small and wobbly. A different sound means
something is different.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE TWO: THE BURRS**

*The spiky burr and the grey hairs on the heather stem replay.*

**[CLUE ALERT #2, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
Prickly burrs on the heather near his den, with grey fur caught on them. He walked through
them. Anything that brushes past can pick one up.

*A gold star.*

**CLUE THREE: THE LIFTED PAW**

*Moony holding up his front left paw replays.*

**[CLUE ALERT #3, 2nd]** *Chime. Sparkle.*

**HOLMES (V.O.)**
He kept lifting the same paw. Not his tummy, not his tail. The paw. And there, between his
toes, was the burr.

*A gold star, a heart, and a doodle of a dog in a sock.*

**HOLMES (CONT'D)**
(turning to Watson)
Zaid knew all along that something was wrong.

**WATSON**
(gently)
He just didn't know how to ask.

**HOLMES**
(quietly)
I should have noticed he was missing from the breakfast table.

**WATSON**
(smiling)
You did notice. Henry noticed first. You noticed second.

**HOLMES**
(a small, rueful smile)
Second is still noticing.

*Watson writes, with a flourish: "ASK FOR HELP. HELP GENTLY." He draws a heart. DING-DING-DING:
three dings for "solved."*

**WATSON**
(writing)
Case closed. Hound... helped.

*Holmes adds a line to the wall chart: "THINGS THAT GO BUMP: A BURR. (AND A SOCK.)"*

**HOLMES**
Curious... very curious!

## Complexity and logic check

- **Clue 1** is shown as two sound ripples: a big golden one and a small blue one.
- **Clue 2** is an object (a burr), and the answer is found within seconds.
- **Clue 3** is a behaviour children recognise from real pets: favouring a paw.
- This is the most emotional, least puzzle-like mystery of the season. The "mystery" is a
  diagnosis, and the heart of the episode is Zaid asking for help.

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **No tweezers.** Holmes rolls the burr out with two clean fingertips while Zaid steadies the head.
   Tweezers near a pet's paw is something children might copy; this is gentler and safer to imitate.
2. **"The Hound is cleared"** doesn't apply here. In the recap, Watson writes "Hound...
   helped." OK?
3. **Holmes's "Second is still noticing"** is another growth beat. I kept it small and
   self-aware. Cut it if you want her to stay oblivious.
4. **Zaid's line "Asking is brave"** is the lesson. It's said by Zaid, not by an adult,
   after Watson prompts him. OK?
5. **The sock bandage** is a gag and a gesture of care. Is it okay for a dog to wear a sock
   around a paw, or should we avoid showing something viewers might copy with their own pets?
