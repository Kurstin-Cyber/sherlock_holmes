## What each character does in a mystery

| Character | Job in the team | Typical contribution |
|---|---|---|
| **Holmes** | Notices | Spots the clues; leads the chant; solves it |
| **Watson** | Records and asks about feelings | Journal doodles; makes the "second look" at each clue; checks on people |
| **Henry** | Asks "why?" | Fresh eyes; the one who says the obvious thing nobody else did; viewer stand-in |
| **Mortimer** | Brings the case | Tells the legend; arrives with the spooky story of the week |
| **Jasper** | Rival and pilot villain | Pilot: the schemer. After: a fussy, rule-loving rival who sometimes helps |
| **Beryl** | Conscience | Honest correction; the "that's not quite true" that cracks a case |
| **Barrymore twins** | Insiders | Know every sound, smell and shortcut in the Hall; solve "what made that noise?" |
| **Zaid** | Secret keeper | Knows where the dog is; the soft heart of the show |
| **Tavita** | Red herring supplier | Reports what he "sees" through the telescope, usually wrongly and always funnily |
| **Moony** | Mascot | Wags, snacks, gets blamed, gets cleared |

The Clue Alert, chant and journal are shared devices. Their owners are Holmes (chant),
Watson (journal) and the show itself (chime and sparkle).

## Group dynamics

- **Holmes and Watson** are the core. Holmes notices things. Watson notices people. They
  need each other: Holmes solves the puzzle, Watson solves the feelings, and the lesson
  usually lives where those two meet.
- **Henry** is the audience's way in: new to the Hall, asks the questions a newcomer would.
- **Jasper and Beryl** are siblings who disagree about the right thing to do.
- **The twins and Tavita** are the funny energy: the twins know the Hall, Tavita "knows"
  the park (and is wrong).
- **Zaid and Moony** are the heart. Every mystery ends with a small nod to them.

## Consistency rules (apply to every episode)

1. **Look is fixed.** Each character has a signature outfit and colour (see the profiles).
   They can add a coat, a hat or a lantern, but the signature piece is always on screen.
2. **Voice is fixed.** Each profile has a "How they talk" note and sample lines. Keep to it.
3. **Catchphrases are used once or twice an episode,** never forced into every scene.
4. **Strengths and flaws both show up.** Every character gets a moment of being good at
   something and a moment of getting it wrong.
5. **Nobody is only one thing.** The disability, size or heritage of a character is never
   their joke, their problem or their whole personality.
6. **Nobody is rescued for being different.** Mateo is never "helped over" a hill; the
   park has an accessible Boardwalk Trail (see "World additions" below) and his chair is a
   tool in the plot as often as anyone's flashlight.

## World additions this stage needs

To keep the park open to everyone, I'm proposing two small additions to the Stage 1 world.
Both are small and don't change the plot:

- **The Boardwalk Trail:** a wooden path winding through the heather from the Hall to the
  Stone Circle and Tor Hill's base. Kids painted a flower on every post. Mateo's chair
  rolls it easily; the Squelch is the only place it doesn't go, so he sometimes works from
  the edge.
- **The Ramp:** the Hall's front steps have a wide wooden ramp. Painted by the campers.

### On adults

The brief says every character is a child. To keep this working, I propose:

- A camp is always "run" by kids and storybook logic. There is a camp bell, a camp rota and
  a camp "Head Cabin" nobody sees. Adults exist only as **off-screen voices or notes**
  (a note under a door, a bell, a voice from far away, "the Camp Chief says...").
- Family appear in photos, letters and phone calls where it matters (Nana Odessa's letters
  to Holmes, Henry's dad's video call). They are voices and pictures, not characters.

Confirm or change.
