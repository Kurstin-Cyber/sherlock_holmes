## Look

- Slim and small for eleven, pale, neat dark hair parted exactly.
- Tan sweater vest over a collared shirt, sensible shoes, a Nature Club badge on his chest.
- Clipboard, pencil, and a butterfly net.
- **Signature colour:** tan and olive green.
- **Silhouette:** a net, a clipboard, a stiff straight back.

## Personality

**Strengths**
- Hard-working and organised
- Genuinely knowledgeable about nature (bugs, birds, plants)
- Loyal to his club and loves his little sister
- Honest at heart; when caught, he doesn't lie for long

**Flaws**
- Competitive: wants to win the Park Prize so badly he forgets everyone else
- Won't share, or hates the idea of sharing credit
- Rule-obsessed; he makes up rules and expects everyone to follow
- Bad at asking for help; he'd rather scheme than say "I'm worried"

**Quirks**
- Straightens things (pencils, hats, other people's collars)
- Taps his clipboard twice when he's nervous
- Plays a **harmonica**, badly, when no one is listening (a pilot clue: the "howl")

## How he talks

Precise, prim and a little too formal. Full sentences, "Actually," "Technically," "As
President." Gets faster and higher-pitched when his plans wobble.

> "Rules are rules!"
>
> "Technically, that path is closed. Because I closed it."
>
> "I only wanted... to keep the butterfly safe."

## Family and culture

- Welsh grandmother (**Nain**) sends Welsh cakes in every parcel; he guards them but
  cracks and shares at the end of the pilot.
- Parents live apart, so Jasper and Beryl have "two homes" and keep tidy lists of what
  goes where. He's the family's list-maker for a reason.

## The pilot (seed ideas, not locked until Stage 3)

Jasper's motive is **harmless and sympathetic**: he's guarding a rare butterfly he
discovered, hoping it will win the Park Prize and prove he belongs. He doesn't want to
hurt anyone; he only wants campers to stay away from one meadow. The "howl" is his
harmonica, and his scheme gets bigger than he planned.

**His lesson:** sharing a discovery makes it better, not smaller. He's invited to share the
butterfly with the whole camp, and the Park Prize goes to the Nature Club *and* Holmes's
team for solving it.

## After the pilot

Jasper stays a recurring character: the rule-loving rival who sometimes helps (his nature
knowledge solves a clue) and sometimes causes a mini-mystery with a rule or a sign
("DO NOT ENTER"). Never a villain again, only a rival.

## Relationships

- **Beryl:** his younger sister and his conscience. He grumbles at her, and listens.
- **Holmes:** friendly rivals. Both love lists and neatness.
- **Anjali:** each thinks the other is too much. Each is a little right.
- **Zaid:** surprisingly gentle with him; Zaid reminds him of someone small to look after.

## Animation notes

- Staged as stiff and upright, so any loosening (laughing, getting muddy) is a joke.
- His harmonica is a small bright detail kept off-screen until the clue.

## Never

- Never scary or threatening, in voice or in action
- Never shamed in front of the camp; the resolution is private and kind first
- Never plays the "bad guy because of how he looks or where he's from"
