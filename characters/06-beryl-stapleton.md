## Look

- Small, with a long red plait to her waist, freckles, and big hazel eyes.
- Lilac cardigan with butterfly-shaped buttons, a Nature Club badge (vice-president), and
  a sketchbook under her arm.
- **Signature colour:** lilac and soft green.
- **Silhouette:** a long plait and a sketchbook.

## Personality

**Strengths**
- Honest to the bone; she cannot tell a fib without blushing
- Gentle, observant and brave in a quiet way
- A talented nature artist: her sketches of bugs and birds are clues
- Loves her brother, and tells him the truth *because* she loves him

**Flaws**
- Hates conflict, and puts off saying the hard thing
- Feels guilty about everything, even things that aren't her fault
- Struggles when loyalty and honesty pull in different directions
- Too shy to speak up in a crowd

**Quirks**
- Plait-twirling when she's torn
- Whispers "sorry" to bugs she accidentally steps near
- Draws instead of talking when she's upset

## How she talks

Soft, careful, a little shy. Starts with "Um," then lands on a plain true sentence.
Her honesty arrives politely, and then it sticks.

> "Actually... that's not quite true."
>
> "I don't think we should, Jasper. It isn't fair."
>
> "Sorry, little beetle."

## Family and culture

- Same Welsh family as Jasper; she'll tell you the Welsh word for butterfly (*iâr fach yr
  haf*) if you ask, and the story behind it.
- Her **two-home life** makes her very good at packing, labelling and remembering where
  things are. In a case, she's the one who knows "that was in the *other* bag."

## Role in the team

Beryl is the **honest voice.** In the pilot, she's torn between loyalty to her brother and
telling the truth; she chooses honesty, kindly. In later episodes, her careful
corrections ("Actually...") crack open cases, because she says out loud what other kids
only suspect.

## Relationships

- **Jasper:** her big brother. She admires him; she won't lie for him.
- **Holmes:** her hero. Imani sees her details; Beryl sees Imani's feelings.
- **Watson:** two quiet souls who understand each other without words.
- **Zaid:** the other shy one; they sketch together.

## What she learns (series-long theme)

That telling the truth gently isn't betraying anyone; it's looking after them.

## Animation notes

- Her sketchbook pages are painted watercolour, a different art style from Watson's
  crayon journal, so the two never get confused.
- Her blush is her tell: a pink wash on her cheeks when she's tempted to fib.

## Never

- Never a snitch; her honesty is always kind and always private first
- Never scolded for speaking up
- Never gets the "too shy" laugh; her shyness is respected
