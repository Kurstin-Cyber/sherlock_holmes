# Anjali Mortimer

*Camp storyteller · Doyle's Dr. Mortimer · DRAFT for approval*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Anjali Mortimer |
| **Age** | 11 (oldest of the main cast) |
| **Heritage** | Indian (Tamil) |
| **Cabin** | Bracken Cabin |
| **Role** | Tells the legend; brings the first case |
| **Catchphrase** | "And now... the TRUE story!" |

## Look

- Round, solid and strong, with a warm brown face and a huge laugh.
- Enormous curly hair she ties up with a silk scarf; tendrils escape everywhere.
- Plum-purple velvet cape (really a curtain she sewed herself) over a bright yellow top.
- Always carries a lantern with a tiny golden flame (an LED, in storybook flicker).
- **Signature colour:** plum purple and gold.
- **Silhouette:** big hair, cape, lantern held high.

## Personality

**Strengths**
- A storyteller through and through: voices, sound effects, pauses
- Generous and warm; everyone feels welcome at her campfire
- Knows the camp's history and every rumour
- Confident, never shy about being the centre of attention

**Flaws**
- Exaggerates: a squirrel becomes "a squirrel the size of a pony"
- Can't resist adding a spooky detail, which sometimes causes the rumours she then worries
  about
- Gets carried away and forgets her audience is scared or small
- Hates being corrected in the middle of a story

**Quirks**
- Whispers louder than she talks
- Ends every story with a wink
- Keeps notes of "story ingredients" (squeaks, creaks, weather) in her cape pockets

## How she talks

Big, theatrical, sing-song. Long, rolling sentences that end in a drumroll. Switches to a
small normal voice when she's embarrassed.

> "Gather round, little campers... for the tale of the Hound!"
>
> "And now... the TRUE story!"
>
> "Okay, okay, I might have added the part about the pony."

## Family and culture

- Lives with her aunt and uncle and four cousins in a house full of noise and food. She
  tells stories because dinner is the only place to be heard.
- Her paati (grandmother) taught her to tell stories; her paati's sayings open some of
  her tales ("Once upon a time is the best time").
- Brings idli and a spicy-sweet snack mix to campfires, and shares it round.

## Role in the team

Anjali tells the legend in the opening campfire scenes and **brings the first case**: the
spooky thing someone saw or heard that she heard about. She's a reporter and a rumour-mill
in one, which makes her a good partner and a good red herring.

## Relationships

- **Holmes:** Anjali tells the story; Holmes checks the facts. They love and annoy each
  other equally.
- **Tavita:** her best "audience." They exaggerate together.
- **Jasper:** he thinks she makes campers scared; she thinks he's no fun. Both are a bit
  right.
- **Henry:** the legend is *about* his family, and she tells it with a little extra
  kindness once she realises.

## What she learns (series-long theme)

That a good story doesn't need to scare anyone to be good, and that checking the facts
makes a story better, not smaller.

## Animation notes

- Her cape swoops on cue; her lantern lights her face from below in a cosy, never creepy,
  way (a warm gold, with sparkles in the air).
- Her stories are shown as short **paper-cut puppet** sequences, a visual change that
  separates legend from reality.

## Never

- Never mocked for her size, her hair or her volume
- Never the "fat funny friend": she's the show's best performer and a leader
- Never scares a small camper on purpose

## Consistency checklist

- [ ] Cape, scarf-tied hair, lantern in frame
- [ ] The legend told once in the pilot; short echoes in later episodes
- [ ] "And now... the TRUE story!" when the facts get revealed
- [ ] An exaggeration that Holmes gently trims down
