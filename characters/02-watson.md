# Mateo Watson

*Best friend and case-journal keeper · Doyle's Dr. Watson · DRAFT for approval*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Mateo Watson (everyone calls him "Watson") |
| **Age** | 10 |
| **Heritage** | Mexican-American |
| **Cabin** | Heather Cabin, with Holmes |
| **Role** | The heart of the team; keeps the case journal and narrates |
| **Catchphrase** | "Writing that down!" |
| **Uses** | A wheelchair |

## Look

- Sturdy, broad smile, warm brown skin, dark hair that sticks up at the back.
- Teal hoodie with a pocket full of pencils.
- **Wheelchair:** bright orange, covered in stickers (a dog, a star, a taco). It has a
  little bicycle bell, a clip-on flashlight, and a satchel on the back that holds the case
  journal.
- The case journal is a fat green notebook tied shut with a ribbon.
- **Signature colour:** teal and orange.
- **Silhouette:** orange wheels, the hoodie, a pencil behind one ear.

## Personality

**Strengths**
- Kind and warm. He notices when someone is sad, left out or nervous.
- A good listener, and a very good question-asker
- A gifted doodler; his drawings make clues easy to see
- Brave in the ways that count: he says the hard true thing, quietly
- Loyal: he never leaves a friend behind

**Flaws**
- Worries a lot, and overthinks ("What if it *is* a real hound?")
- Avoids arguments and says "it's fine" when it's not
- Over-writes: his journal pages run off the edge
- Gets distracted drawing and misses what's happening next to him

**Quirks**
- Rings his chair bell twice when he's happy, once when he's nervous
- Draws a small star next to the best clue
- Hums when he writes

## Ability: how it's handled

Mateo uses a wheelchair, and it is just part of who he is.

- He's not the "sidekick who needs help." He's an equal, and often the one with the key
  idea.
- The chair is **a tool in the plot,** like Holmes's magnifier: the bell is a signal, the
  flashlight lights a dark corner, wheel tracks are tracks in mud (and a good red-herring
  gag when someone mistakes them).
- The world is built to include him: the Hall has a ramp and the park has the
  **Boardwalk Trail**.
- Where he *can't* go (the Squelch), the story doesn't dwell. He works from the edge, with
  binoculars or a rope-and-bucket trick.
- Nobody "inspires" anyone by being around him. Nobody pushes him without asking.

## How he talks

Warm, thoughtful, a little hesitant. Says "um" and "I think" often, then says the
important thing clearly. Reads his journal aloud in a calm, storytelling voice.

> "Case journal, day six. Something strange happened at camp."
>
> "Um, Holmes? I think Henry's sad."
>
> "Writing that down!"

## Family and culture

- Has **two dads**, Papá Rafa and Dad Ben, who send him voice messages and cookies.
  (Heard on recordings, seen in photos on his chair.)
- His abuela's tamales are legendary. Sometimes he shares tamales from a care package, and
  the whole cabin lines up.
- Says "ay, no" when startled. A few Spanish words appear naturally and are always clear
  from the scene.

## Role in the team

Watson holds the journal, which is the **second look** at each clue. When a clue appears,
he doodles it, and the doodle appears again before the reveal. He also keeps Holmes kind:
his questions ("How do you think they feel?") turn a solution into a good ending.

## Relationships

- **Holmes:** best friend. Admires her brain; she depends on his heart.
- **Henry:** the first to welcome him. They bond over missing home.
- **Zaid:** a soft protector; the two are quiet together.
- **Tavita:** laughs at his reports, then helps him check them.

## What he learns (series-long theme)

To speak up when something isn't fine, and that his worries are sometimes right. He's
braver in every episode, but each episode starts a little nervous again.

## Animation notes

- The journal opens with a soft page-turn sound. Doodles are 2D crayon-and-pencil on
  the 3D page and pop out in a small sparkle.
- His chair has real movement: little spins, bell dings, a wheelie on a happy ending.
- Never film him from above in a way that makes him small.

## Never

- Never the one who needs rescuing for being in a wheelchair
- Never carried up or down steps without his say-so
- Never described as "brave for just being there"
- No jokes about the chair itself

## Consistency checklist

- [ ] Orange chair with stickers, bell, flashlight, satchel on screen
- [ ] "Writing that down!" once or twice per episode
- [ ] The journal opens and closes the episode
- [ ] A moment he is right, a moment he worries too much
