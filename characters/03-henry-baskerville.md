# Henry Baskerville

*New camper from the founding family · Doyle's Sir Henry Baskerville · APPROVED*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Henry Baskerville |
| **Age** | 9 |
| **Heritage** | Japanese-Canadian and Scottish |
| **Cabin** | Bracken Cabin |
| **Role** | New arrival, the viewer's way in; the "Baskerville who came home" |
| **Catchphrase** | "Back home, we'd..." |

## Look

- Very tall and gangly for nine. Long arms and legs, a little clumsy.
- Curly auburn hair, freckles across his nose, dark almond-shaped eyes.
- Red-and-black plaid flannel shirt (a Vancouver nod) and a bobble hat ("toque") he pulls
  down when nervous.
- A small, worn photo of his dad and baby sister in his shirt pocket.
- **Signature colour:** red plaid.
- **Silhouette:** tallest kid at camp; hat with a bobble.

## Personality

**Strengths**
- Curious and enthusiastic about everything new
- Great at asking "why?" and "what's that?" without worry
- Generous: shares his maple candy with everyone
- Funny in a clumsy way, and good at laughing at himself

**Flaws**
- Homesick; goes quiet and checks his photo
- Trips over things, spills things, knocks things down
- Says yes to everything to fit in, then regrets it
- Believes the legend a little too much in the first minute

**Quirks**
- Pulls his hat over his eyes when something scary pops up, then peeks out
- Counts his steps when nervous
- Calls the Hall "the Big House" until he gets used to it

## How he talks

Eager, rushing, a bit breathless. Starts sentences with "Oh!" or "Wait!" Compares
everything to home. Gets quiet and short when sad.

> "Back home, we'd have pancakes for this!"
>
> "Wait, I'm a Baskerville? Is that the *good* kind?"
>
> "Oh! Sorry! Was that your foot?"

## Family and culture

- Lives in Vancouver with his **single dad** (a ferry-boat cook) and his **baby sister
  Hana**. His late-night video calls with Dad and Hana are the warmest moments in his
  episodes.
- Mum's side is Scottish (his Gran sends shortbread); Dad's side is Japanese-Canadian
  (his Ojiichan taught him how to make onigiri). He shares both.
- Is the only camper whose family name is on the manor's walls, and he doesn't know
  what to do with that.

## Role in the team

Henry is the **newcomer's eyes.** He asks what the audience is wondering and says out
loud what everyone else already assumed. He is the first to be spooked by the Hound, and
the first to say it might be friendly.

## Relationships

- **Watson:** first friend at camp. Watson notices Henry is homesick before Holmes does.
- **Holmes:** adores her cap; sometimes feels she looks past him.
- **Zaid:** meets him in the pilot; the two quietly bond.
- **Moony:** the Hound howls "when a Baskerville comes home," so Henry is part of the
  legend. In the pilot, he becomes the Hound's favourite person.

## What he learns (series-long theme)

That home can be wherever kind friends are. In each episode his homesickness gets a gentle,
funny resolution, but it never goes away completely. That's what makes him real.

## Animation notes

- His height needs good staging: low camera angles make him look lanky, not scary.
- Hat-over-eyes and peek-out is his comic beat.
- His video-call scenes are warm and golden, with a small phone screen glow.

## Never

- Never mocked for being clumsy (the laugh is *with* him)
- Never "just the Baskerville"; he's a person first, a legend second
- Homesickness never turns into crying that lasts; the camp always rallies

## Consistency checklist

- [ ] Red plaid and bobble hat in frame
- [ ] "Back home, we'd..." once per episode, and the answer is funny or sweet
- [ ] A moment of clumsiness; a moment of kindness
- [ ] Photo of Dad and Hana appears at least once
