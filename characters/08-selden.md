# Zaid Selden

*Shy younger camper, the dog's secret friend · Doyle's Selden · APPROVED*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Zaid Selden |
| **Age** | 6 (youngest in the cast) |
| **Heritage** | Lebanese |
| **Cabin** | Bracken Cabin (junior bunk) |
| **Role** | Secretly looks after the Hound; the heart of the show |
| **Catchphrase** | "He's not scary. He's shy." |

## Look

- The smallest kid at camp, with big dark eyes, soft black curls and a gap-toothed smile
  (when he smiles, which is rare at first).
- An **oversized yellow raincoat** that comes down to his knees (a hand-me-down from his
  older cousin), rain or shine.
- Carries a floppy stuffed **rabbit named Tut**.
- **Signature colour:** sunshine yellow.
- **Silhouette:** a tiny yellow figure with a droopy rabbit.

## Personality

**Strengths**
- Gentle and patient, especially with animals and anyone small or scared
- Notices when something is lonely, and cares for it
- Loyal and secretive. Once he promises, he keeps it.
- Brave when it counts: he speaks up for Moony in front of the whole camp

**Flaws**
- Very shy; whispers or says nothing at all in a crowd
- Keeps secrets even when he shouldn't. He can't ask for help.
- Frets when he's out of his routine
- Hides when he's embarrassed (usually under the raincoat hood)

**Quirks**
- Talks to Tut instead of to people when nervous
- Collects crusts, cheese and bits of sandwich in his raincoat pockets (for the dog)
- Hums the same four notes every night

## How he talks

Very few words. Soft, slow, a little hesitant; the smallest voice in the cast. Speaks
properly full sentences when he's talking about animals.

> "He's not scary. He's shy."
>
> "Moony likes cheese. And being brushed."
>
> "I couldn't tell. I promised."

## Family and culture

- Lives with his **mum** and his **teta (grandmother)** in a flat above a bakery. Teta
  sends him manoushe (flatbread with za'atar) and sweet *ma'amoul* biscuits in the post.
- He shares the manoushe with Moony, who loves the za'atar.
- His mum sends him voice notes in Arabic and English, and his raincoat has her name
  stitched in the collar.

## Role in the team

Zaid is the **secret keeper** and the **soft heart** of the show. In the pilot, he's the
only one who knows the "Hound" is a lost, friendly dog he's been feeding crusts and cheese.
Afterwards, he's the main reason Moony is safe at camp, and a quiet voice of reason when
others get scared.

## Relationships

- **Moony:** best friend. Zaid is the only person who can make the Hound sit.
- **Henry:** the first big kid who doesn't tower over him.
- **Beryl:** fellow quiet kid; they draw together.
- **Holmes:** idolises her. She, gently, works out how to ask him a question softly.

## What he learns (series-long theme)

That asking for help is brave, and that secrets that protect someone are fine, but secrets
that scare people need a friend to share them with.

## Animation notes

- He's often framed small in the shot, with a big world around him. Use warm light and
  wide, friendly spaces, not looming shadows.
- Tut the rabbit has simple expressions: ears up, ears down.
- His four-note hum is part of the score and signals "Moony's near."

## Never

- Never the one in danger; his secret is gentle, not risky
- Never ridiculed for being small or shy
- Never made to explain himself in front of the whole camp; his reveal is private first
