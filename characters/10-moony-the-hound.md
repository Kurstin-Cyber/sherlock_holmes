# Moony, the Hound

*The friendly dog, secret camp mascot · Doyle's Hound · APPROVED*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Moony (given by Zaid, because his eyes shine "like two little moons") |
| **Age** | About 3 (in dog years: a goofy teenager) |
| **Breed** | A shaggy mix: part wolfhound, part sheepdog, all goof |
| **Home** | Secret den in the Stone Circle; naps in the Hall's old boot room |
| **Role** | Misunderstood dog turned secret camp mascot |
| **Catchphrase** | "Woo-oo-oo!" (a harmonica-y howl) |

## Look

- **Huge:** as tall as a pony from the kids' point of view, but with a soft, floppy,
  cartoonish build. A grey shaggy coat, bushy eyebrows and a long beard.
- Big brown, kind eyes. The "glow" is a **reflective collar** (and later a dab of glow paint
  from the pilot), not his eyes.
- One ear stands up and the other flops down.
- A long tail that wags the whole back half of him.
- **Signature colour:** soft silver-grey and a bright reflective collar.
- **Silhouette:** a huge shaggy shape with one floppy ear and a wagging tail. In the pilot,
  seen only in silhouette until the reveal, and **always with a goofy detail** so he reads
  as funny, not scary.

## Personality

**Strengths**
- Loving, loyal, endlessly forgiving
- A superb sniffer; he can follow a scent when the kids can't
- Gentle with small people (especially Zaid)
- Dramatic: he does everything with his whole body

**Flaws**
- Loves snacks a little too much (steals sandwiches)
- Clumsy; sits on things, knocks things over, wags objects off tables
- Gets scared of small things (squirrels, wind, hats)
- Howls when he's happy, hungry or lonely, which gets him blamed

**Quirks**
- Tilts his head at every question
- Sneezes when the heather blooms
- "Sings" along when Jasper plays harmonica

## Voice

No talking. Sounds only: a soft **"woo-oo-oo,"** huffs, sneezes, a happy "boof." The howl
is **sweet and silly, more harmonica than horror,** and it always has a wobble at the end.
Think a very big, very happy kazoo.

## Backstory (seeds, not locked until Stage 3)

Moony is a lost, friendly dog who wandered into the park long ago. He was not a pet, and
lived on scraps until Zaid found him and started sharing sandwiches. His "glowing eyes" come
from an old reflective collar that belonged to a Baskerville dog, with a tag nobody has
read. (The tag can be a gentle pilot payoff: it links Moony to the Baskerville legend.)

## Series role

- **Pilot:** the "Hound." He appears in silhouette, then is revealed to the audience as
  a big, shaggy, friendly dog.
- **After the pilot:** the camp's **secret mascot.** Not every kid knows. The team keeps
  him hidden because camp rules ("no pets") might separate him from them (see
  `open-questions.md`).
- **Each episode:** something spooky happens, everyone whispers "It's the Hound!", and
  Moony is innocent. He's usually sleeping, eating or hiding in the background of the real
  culprit's mystery. Every ending includes a small Moony wink: a wag, a woo, a stolen
  sandwich.

## Relationships

- **Zaid:** his best friend and the only one he truly obeys.
- **Henry:** the legend says the Hound howls "when a Baskerville comes home." He's very
  excited about Henry.
- **Holmes:** treats him as a suspect, then as a witness, then as a friend.
- **The twins:** supply cheese. Moony adores them.
- **Tavita:** Moony is the thing Tavita sees in every bush.

## Scare flags and rules

- Never growls, snarls or shows teeth
- Never chases anyone in a way that looks threatening (he bounds, he doesn't stalk)
- Shown in silhouette only before the reveal, with a goofy detail every time
- The "glowing" is a reflective collar or glow paint, explained in the episode
- Never harmed, lost in danger, or sad for long

## Animation notes

- Animate him like a huge puppy: big paws, wobbly legs, bursts of speed, sudden stops.
- His howl is shown as soft **golden musical notes** drifting over the heather.
- His collar catches the light with a gentle shimmer, never an eerie glow.
