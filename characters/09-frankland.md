# Tavita Frankland

*The telescope kid · Doyle's Mr. Frankland · DRAFT for approval*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Tavita Frankland |
| **Age** | 9 |
| **Heritage** | Samoan-New Zealander |
| **Cabin** | Gorse Cabin |
| **Role** | Reports everything he "sees" through his telescope |
| **Catchphrase** | "I SEE IT! ...Oh. Never mind." |

## Look

- Tall, strong and broad, with a huge laugh and a bigger smile; thick black curly hair.
- A wide-brimmed straw sun hat and a bright **blue camp shirt** with sleeves rolled.
- A **brass telescope** strapped over his back, and a pair of binoculars around his neck.
  He uses them on everything, including things too close to see.
- **Signature colour:** sky blue and straw yellow.
- **Silhouette:** a big round hat and a telescope sticking up behind his head.

## Personality

**Strengths**
- Warm, loud, and the first to cheer anyone up
- Energetic, strong and brave; the first to volunteer for a hill
- Observant in his way: he *does* see things, he just doesn't always know what they are
- Loyal; if a friend is in trouble, he's the first there

**Flaws**
- Jumps to conclusions: "I SEE a hound!" (it's a bush)
- Exaggerates, loudly, and hates to be proven wrong in public
- Can't whisper, which makes secrets hard
- Gets distracted by shiny things (including shiny things in the telescope)

**Quirks**
- Holds the telescope backwards at least once per episode
- Sings a happy tune as he marches
- Names clouds

## How he talks

Loud, cheerful, fast and a bit boasting. Lots of capital letters, lots of exclamation
marks. Collapses into giggles when he's wrong.

> "I SEE IT! ...Oh. Never mind."
>
> "It's a hound! A giant hound! ...A cat? It's a cat."
>
> "Okay, but it was a VERY big cat."

## Family and culture

- Comes from a big, noisy family with **four siblings**, all of whom write him letters
  (mostly drawings).
- His **nana** plays the ukulele on video calls and sings him to sleep. Tavita sings her
  songs on marches.
- Brings *panikeke* (Samoan banana pancakes) to the cabin on rainy days and *koko alaisa*
  (a sweet rice porridge) when someone's sad.
- Says "Aua le popole!" ("Don't worry!") when someone's upset. The meaning is always clear
  from his hug.

## Role in the team

Tavita is the show's **red herring supplier.** Each episode he "sees" something through his
telescope, reports it wrongly, and sends the team the wrong way. The twist: his wrong
reports usually contain one true detail that turns into **Clue #2 or #3.** He reports,
Holmes untangles.

## Relationships

- **Anjali:** his best performer and audience; they feed each other's stories.
- **Linh and Minh:** rivals-turned-friends; each is sure their account of events is right.
- **Watson:** the sensible friend who gently checks his reports.
- **Henry:** the first to say the Hound "is real" (he sees it everywhere).

## What he learns (series-long theme)

To say "I think I saw..." instead of "I saw...", and that being wrong in a friendly way
is just fine.

## Animation notes

- The telescope's "view" is shown as a round iris shot, so we see exactly what he sees,
  and what it *actually* is.
- His laughs are big: bouncing shoulders, hat falls off.
- Always frames his big wrong guess as a gentle gag, never a failure.

## Never

- Never "the dumb one": his wrongness is optimism, and he often has half the answer
- Never reduced to a Pacific Islander stereotype; his culture shows in food, family and
  language, not in costumes
- Never mocked for being big; his size is just his size

## Consistency checklist

- [ ] Sun hat, telescope, binoculars
- [ ] "I SEE IT! ...Oh. Never mind." in each episode he appears
- [ ] One wrong report that contains one true detail
