# Linh and Minh Barrymore

*Kitchen-helper twins · Doyle's Mr. and Mrs. Barrymore · DRAFT for approval*

| | Linh | Minh |
|---|---|---|
| **Age** | 10 (fraternal twins) | 10 |
| **Heritage** | Vietnamese-Irish | Vietnamese-Irish |
| **Cabin** | Kitchen Loft (a tiny room above the kitchen) | Kitchen Loft |
| **Role** | Know every creak in the Hall | Know every creak in the Hall |
| **Look** | Short, quick, yellow headband, yellow apron | Short, quick, **orange hearing aids**, green apron |
| **Voice** | Fast, bossy, laughing | Calm, dry, a deadpan joker |

**Shared catchphrase** (they finish each other's sentences):
- Linh: "Third stair!"
- Minh: "...always squeaks!"

## Look

- Matching kitchen aprons (yellow for Linh, green for Minh) with a tiny pot embroidered on
  the pocket. Both wear soft kitchen clogs.
- Linh: black hair in a high ponytail, a yellow headband.
- Minh: black hair cropped short, **bright orange hearing aids** worn proudly and
  decorated with tiny stickers.
- **Signature colour:** yellow and green, with a splash of orange.
- **Silhouette:** two matching short shapes, a ponytail and a pair of bright ears.

## Personality

**Linh**
- *Strengths:* quick, confident, knows the schedule of the whole camp, and who owes whom
  a favour
- *Flaws:* bossy, gossipy, can't keep a secret for longer than a day

**Minh**
- *Strengths:* sharp, calm, funny; notices tiny sounds and vibrations (he feels the
  floorboards); a deadpan joker
- *Flaws:* stubborn, sulks when teased, hates to admit he's wrong

**Together**
- Bicker constantly and kindly, and defend each other instantly
- Know every creak, squeak, draught and shortcut in the Hall
- Happiest in the kitchen; sneaky about snacks

## Ability: how it's handled

Minh wears hearing aids and uses a mix of speech and **sign language** with Linh at
home. The twins sign to each other across a noisy dining hall, and Holmes and Watson
learn a few signs.

- Sign language is shown **with subtitles and clear visuals**, and is used as a normal part
  of the twins' lives, and sometimes as a plot tool (a silent message across the hall).
- Minh isn't "fixed" or "pitied." His hearing aids are cool: he swaps the covers, and he
  can take them out to "go quiet" at will.
- Clue Alerts carry **a visual sparkle** as well as a chime, which means every viewer
  gets every clue.
- In the pilot, his sensitivity to **vibrations** can help with the howl: he feels it
  through the floor before anyone hears it.

## How they talk

Fast back-and-forth, the second twin finishing the first's thought. Linh's lines are
long and breathless; Minh's are short and dry.

> **Linh:** "That door only creaks when someone's trying to be quiet!"
> **Minh:** "Which is why it creaks all the time."
>
> **Linh:** "Third stair!" **Minh:** "...always squeaks!"

## Family and culture

- Raised by their **Grandpa Barrymore** (Irish) and **Bà Ngoại** (Vietnamese
  grandmother) who run the camp kitchen from "somewhere offstage." Their voices float
  from the kitchen; their soup is real and wonderful.
- Their kitchen cooks **phở, bánh mì, Irish soda bread and cheese toasties,** often all in
  one meal. The twins hand them out on the dining-hall hatch.
- Linh counts in Vietnamese when she's nervous (một, hai, ba...). Minh counts in Irish
  (aon, dó, trí...). They have never agreed on which is better.

## Role in the team

The twins know **every sound and smell in the Hall.** If something creaks, rattles or
goes missing, they know where, when and usually why. They are the "inside men" of the
mystery, who supply the sound-based or kitchen-based clue. Their snack-sneaking habits
also make them suspects when something goes missing.

## Relationships

- **Holmes:** treats them as expert witnesses, which makes them very proud.
- **Zaid:** they sneak him cheese; they know he's hiding something and, to their credit,
  keep it quiet.
- **Tavita:** natural rivals, each sure their version of events is true.
- **Jasper:** they find him stuffy and tease him; he responds with rules.

## Animation notes

- Always **animate the twins as a pair**, ideally in matching poses, with small
  differences (Linh a half-beat ahead).
- Their signing needs a sign-language consultant (see `open-questions.md`).
- Floorboard creaks are shown as a **ripple of light** along the boards, so all viewers
  can "see" the sound.

## Never

- Never use Minh's hearing as a joke or a plot gap
- Never treat sign language as magic; it's just language
- Never make the twins gossipy villains; Linh's chatter is a flaw that hurts nobody for
  long
