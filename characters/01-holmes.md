# Imani Holmes

*Camp detective · Doyle's Sherlock Holmes · APPROVED*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Imani Holmes (everyone calls her "Holmes") |
| **Age** | 10 |
| **Heritage** | Nigerian-British |
| **Cabin** | Heather Cabin, with Watson |
| **Role** | Self-appointed camp detective; solves the mystery |
| **Catchphrase** | "Curious... very curious!" (taps her magnifier twice) |
| **Method chant** | "Look closer. Notice more. Think it through." |

## Look

- Small and wiry, always on her toes, leaning in to look at things.
- Round gold-rimmed glasses, and a green tweed deerstalker-style cap (a nod to the
  original). Two natural hair puffs poke out on either side of the cap.
- Mustard-yellow raincoat over a plain shirt and shorts, sturdy brown boots.
- A magnifying glass on a string around her neck. It frames the golden magnifier effect.
- **Signature colour:** mustard yellow and tweed green.
- **Silhouette:** a cap with two puffs, and a magnifier. Instantly readable in shadow.

## Personality

**Strengths**
- Super observant: notices the smallest thing out of place
- Curious and fearless about asking "why?"
- Honest and fair. She says what she sees, and she stands up for the Hound (and anyone
  wrongly blamed).
- Persistent: never gives up on a puzzle

**Flaws**
- Misses feelings. She sees the muddy boot but not the sad face above it.
- Interrupts people when she has an idea
- A little bossy and proud: she hates being wrong, and "I knew that" is her tell
- Forgets to eat, rest and say thank you when she's deep in a case

**Quirks**
- Hums three notes when thinking
- Draws a tiny circle in the air around a clue with one finger
- Keeps a pocket full of "evidence": a bottle cap, a leaf, a button

## How she talks

Quick, clear, short sentences. Likes lists of three ("Mud. Boots. Squelch."). Asks
questions to herself out loud. When she's wrong, her voice gets small and fast.

> "Look at the boot. The mud is grey. Grey mud. That's the Squelch!"
>
> "Curious... very curious!"
>
> "I didn't know that. But I do now."

## Family and culture

- Raised by her grandmother, **Nana Ngozi**, who writes her letters full of proverbs and
  small riddles. Letters arrive by post; Nana is heard, never seen on screen.
- Nana's jollof rice and puff-puff are Imani's favourite foods. She trades puff-puff for
  clues in the kitchen.
- She says "Nana says..." the way other kids say "My teacher says...". Nana's sayings are
  short and kind ("A quiet question can open a loud door").

## Role in the team

Holmes notices things. The chant frames every solving moment. She leads the investigation
and says the reveal. But she needs Watson to remind her about people.

## Relationships

- **Watson:** best friend and cabin-mate. He tells her what people feel; she tells him what
  things mean. She doesn't always listen, but she always comes back.
- **Henry:** wants to be his friend, doesn't know how. Learns to notice when he's quiet.
- **Jasper:** friendly rivals. They respect each other's rule-following and lists.
- **Zaid:** gentle with him, without realising why she's so gentle.

## What she learns (series-long theme)

That noticing *people* is a clue too. Each episode gives Holmes one small chance to
notice a feeling, and she gets a little better at it. No episode relies on her having
learned it; she always starts the next one a bit forgetful, in a loving way.

## Animation notes

- Eyes dart and focus before she notices something; the camera follows her glance.
- When she thinks, the golden magnifier frame circles her face.
- Her cap tilts when she's puzzled and snaps straight when she's solved it.

## Never

- Never mocks someone for not noticing
- Never solves a mystery without a clue the viewer has also seen
- Never needs saving from anything physical; her danger is being wrong, not hurt

## Consistency checklist

- [ ] Cap, glasses, puffs and magnifier always in frame
- [ ] "Curious... very curious!" once or twice per episode
- [ ] The chant at least once when solving, once in the recap
- [ ] A clue she notices first, and a feeling she misses
