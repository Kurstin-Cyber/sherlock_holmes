# Season 1: Arc and Structure

*Stage 3. APPROVED by the producer.*

## Shape of the season

**13 episodes: a 22-minute pilot special plus 12 standalone 11-minute mysteries**
(about 2 hours 34 minutes in all). Season length and the pilot's double length are proposals;
see "Decisions to confirm."

The series is **looser episodic**: any episode can be watched in any order, and none depends
on another. The only fixed points are the **pilot first** (it introduces the cast and
reveals Moony) and the **finale last** (it is Camp Day). Episodes 2 to 12 can be aired,
streamed or watched in any order.

## The "optional threads" rule

A season arc normally rewards watching in order. Ours rewards it *lightly*. These threads
add warmth for kids who watch everything, and **never** leave a first-time viewer
confused.

| Thread | How it shows up | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Moony's secret** | Every episode ends with a small Moony wink (a wag, a woo, a stolen sandwich) | Never needed to follow the plot |
| **The Camp Day banner** | A banner in the dining hall reads "CAMP DAY: ___ days to go." The number changes with the episode's position in the *suggested* order | Background detail only. Nobody mentions it unless it's the finale |
| **Henry's little home** | A small thing from home (Dad's postcard, Gran's shortbread, Hana's drawing) turns up in the background | Pure Easter egg |
| **Holmes's feelings log** | Each episode Holmes notices one feeling, and Watson doodles a heart in the journal margin | Silent gag |
| **The Park Prize** | Introduced in the pilot (a shared prize for the camp's best discovery, awarded at Camp Day). Later episodes may mention it in passing | A reminder, never a plot need |

### What *can't* carry over

- No unsolved cliffhangers. Every mystery ends in the episode it starts.
- No character change that later episodes must remember. Everyone resets (see the
  consistency rules): Holmes forgets feelings again, Jasper is a rule-lover again, Henry is
  homesick again, in a loving way.
- After the pilot, Jasper is never the villain again. He is the rival.

### Pilot recap line

Every episode after the pilot opens its first minute with Anjali's one-line campfire
reminder ("A giant hound... a snack-loving hound!"), so no one needs to have seen the pilot.

## The season in four small movements

Used for pacing and variety only, not as a continuing story.

| Movement | Episodes | Mood |
|---|---|---|
| **Arrival** | 1 (pilot) | Henry arrives; the team forms; Moony is revealed to the team |
| **Settling in** | 2 to 5 | Camp life: kitchen, attic, the Squelch, bedtime. Cosy mysteries close to the Hall |
| **Out in the park** | 6 to 10 | Wider adventures: trails, Tor Hill, the Stone Circle, a missing story |
| **Heart and home** | 11 to 13 | Feelings-led mysteries; ends with Camp Day |

## Recurring gags

- **"It's the Hound!"** whispered in every cold open, by a different camper each time
- **Tavita's backwards telescope** (once an episode)
- **The third stair** squeaks (the twins; a different thing creaks in each episode)
- **Holmes's puff-puff trades** with the twins for kitchen intelligence
- **Watson's bell:** once for nervous, twice for happy, three times for "I solved it!"
- **Moony's sneeze** at heather, hats or the wrong sandwich
- **"Back home, we'd..."** with Henry's funny home comparison
- **Anjali's exaggerations** trimmed down by Holmes ("The squirrel was *not* a pony.")

### Clue types (so no two mysteries feel the same)

Footprints and marks · sounds · lights · smells and crumbs · objects and tools ·
handwriting · shadows and reflections · animal signs. The episode list marks each clue's
type so we vary them.

## Complexity and scare check for the whole season

- Every mystery: **3 clues, 1 red herring.** No exceptions (the pilot included).
- Every clue has a **visible, concrete object** and needs **one simple step of logic**.
- No night scene longer than a minute without lantern, flashlight, fireflies or stars.
- Dusk is always golden, fog always sparkly.
- Every culprit is harmless, and gets a kind ending.
- The Hound is always innocent of the *blame* (episode 11 is the one time Moony *is* part
  of the mystery, and it's a tender one).

## Decisions to confirm

1. **Season length.** 13 (pilot plus 12) is my recommendation: it fits one summer, gives
   every lesson three outings, and every character a spotlight. Shorter or longer is easy.
2. **22-minute pilot.** A full Doyle retelling won't fit in 11 minutes without rushing the
   clues. I propose a double-length special, shaped as two 11-minute halves, each with its
   own beats. If you'd rather keep 11 minutes, I'd cut the red herring's scene and the
   recap.
3. **Episode 11 breaks the "Moony is innocent" rule once**, on purpose, for heart.
4. **The finale** (episode 13): visiting kids from Brambledown Camp arrive for Camp Day,
   the noise scares Moony into hiding, and the whole camp works together to find him and
   keep his secret. It changes nothing about the secret-mascot engine; it deepens it.
5. **The Camp Day banner countdown** as an optional thread.
6. **The "Park Prize"** as a shared prize for the best discovery, awarded at Camp Day.
