## 2. The Case of the Missing Sandwiches

**Spotlight:** Linh and Minh · **Lesson:** Honesty · **Setting:** kitchen, dining hall

- **Cold open:** Every lunch tray in the dining hall is one sandwich short. Tavita whispers,
  "It's the Hound!" and points his telescope at the window. A paw print is... a smudged
  thumbprint.
- **The case:** The twins ask Holmes to find the thief before their grandparents find out.
- **Clue 1:** The crumbs on the counter are from **two kinds of bread**, bánh mì and
  Irish soda bread.
- **Clue 2:** A scrap of paper with half a **handwritten "sorry"** and a drawing of a star.
- **Clue 3:** The sandwiches vanish at exactly **3:15**, when each twin is "doing chores"
  in a different place.
- **Red herring:** Moony's paw prints in the flour... they're Tavita's hat, rolled across the
  counter.
- **The harmless truth:** The twins quarrelled that morning. Each secretly took sandwiches
  to make the other a peace picnic. They end up at the same hidden picnic spot with two
  different sandwiches.
- **Lesson:** Say sorry out loud, not in secret. Holmes realises she should do the same
  (she interrupted Watson earlier).
- **Moony:** Asleep in the boot room, with a crumb on his nose.
- **Flags:** None. The funniest episode on paper. Sign language shown with subtitles.

## 3. The Light in the Attic Window

**Spotlight:** Holmes, Jasper · **Lesson:** Curiosity · **Setting:** the Hall's attic

- **Cold open:** A soft, glowing light flickers in the attic window each dusk. "Maybe the
  Hound's eyes!" Anjali stage-whispers, then is trimmed down by Holmes.
- **The case:** The attic is "off limits"; nobody has been up there in years. Henry asks
  Holmes to find out why it glows.
- **Clue 1:** The light appears only on **clear nights**, never on cloudy ones.
- **Clue 2:** **Silvery dust** on the attic windowsill that sparkles on Holmes's finger.
- **Clue 3:** A **tally sheet** in Jasper's neat writing: lots of small lines.
- **Red herring:** Tavita sees "two glowing eyes". They are two buttons on an old
  teddy bear in the window.
- **The harmless truth:** Jasper set up a **moth light** (a lamp and a white sheet) in the
  attic to count night moths for the Nature Club survey, and forgot to tell anyone.
- **Lesson:** Ask questions before you fear things (curiosity), and tell your friends what
  you're doing. Jasper invites the camp to a moth-watching night.
- **Moony:** Watching the moths from the garden with his head tilted.
- **Flags:** Attic is dusty but bright, with sunbeams and old toys. No spiders.

## 4. The Boots That Vanished

**Spotlight:** Watson · **Lesson:** Teamwork · **Setting:** the Grimpen Squelch

- **Cold open:** A boot vanishes at the edge of the Squelch with a *schlorp!* "The Hound
  ate it!" whispers a camper.
- **The case:** Five boots have gone missing. Watson, who cannot get close to the mud,
  decides to solve this one from the edge of the Squelch, and asks the others for help.
- **Clue 1:** A **tiny white feather** inside the one boot they find.
- **Clue 2:** **Tiny webbed prints** across the mud.
- **Clue 3:** A faint **quack-quack-quack** from the reeds.
- **Red herring:** Henry steps in the mud and loses his own boot. "The Squelch ate it!"
  He pulls it out with a giant *plop*.
- **The harmless truth:** A mother duck has been collecting stray boots to line a nest. All
  five boots are tucked in the reeds with a row of ducklings.
- **Lesson:** Everyone does their part. Watson plans and draws; Holmes spots; Henry and
  Tavita fetch with a rope and bucket; Watson stays on the edge and directs.
- **Moony:** Cleared; he's too afraid of ducks.
- **Flags:** The Squelch is ankle-deep and harmless. Nobody sinks.

## 5. The Howl at Bedtime

**Spotlight:** Minh · **Lesson:** Curiosity · **Setting:** Bracken Cabin, the Hall

- **Cold open:** At lights-out, a long, low "wooooo..." drifts through the cabins. Every
  head pops out of every bunk. "It's the Hound!"
- **The case:** The campers can't sleep. Minh, who feels it through the floorboards before
  he hears it, offers to help.
- **Clue 1:** The howl only happens on **windy nights**, and Minh feels it through the
  boards.
- **Clue 2:** It **always comes from the east**, the same way the wind blows.
- **Clue 3:** A **loose, flapping vent cover** on the old chimney pipe.
- **Red herring:** Tavita says it's Moony; Moony is snoring loudly beside Zaid.
- **The harmless truth:** The wind blows over the old chimney pipe and makes a "woo,"
  like blowing across a bottle.
- **Lesson:** Look for the cause before the monster. The kids fix the vent, then use it as a
  gentle bedtime "wind song."
- **Flags:** ⚠️ Bedtime setting. Keep every shot lit by nightlights, lanterns and moonlight;
  the cabins are warm and cosy; the "howl" is a soft, round, silly sound.

## 6. The Story That Wasn't There

**Spotlight:** Anjali, Beryl · **Lesson:** Honesty · **Setting:** campfire circle

- **Cold open:** Anjali's story notebook, the one with every campfire tale, is gone on
  campfire night. She tells everyone the Hound took it.
- **The case:** No notebook, no stories. Anjali is nearly in tears.
- **Clue 1:** A faint smell of **paint and pencil shavings** on her cape pocket.
- **Clue 2:** A **lilac smudge** on the bench.
- **Clue 3:** A page of **neat handwriting** that isn't Anjali's.
- **Red herring:** Tavita saw the Hound carrying "a book" (it was Moony with a sock).
- **The harmless truth:** Beryl borrowed the notebook to illustrate the stories as a
  surprise for Anjali, then panicked when everyone blamed the Hound.
- **Lesson:** Ask first, and tell the truth even when it's uncomfortable. Beryl's honesty
  lands softly; Anjali admits she exaggerated.
- **Flags:** None.

## 7. The Portrait That Winked

**Spotlight:** Henry · **Lesson:** Kindness · **Setting:** the portrait gallery

- **Cold open:** The old Baskerville portrait seems to wink at Henry. He hides under his
  hat.
- **The case:** Henry thinks his ancestor is watching him. Holmes wants to find out why.
- **Clue 1:** The wink only happens at **three in the afternoon**, when sunlight comes
  through a window.
- **Clue 2:** A **piece of string** hanging from a hook by the window.
- **Clue 3:** A faint smell of **shortbread**.
- **Red herring:** The twins "know" the portrait is cursed (they're teasing).
- **The harmless truth:** Watson and Beryl hung a **sun-catcher** in the window (made from
  Gran's shortbread tin lid) to make Henry feel at home. The reflection hits the painted
  eye.
- **Lesson:** Notice your friends. Holmes realises Watson and Beryl noticed Henry's
  homesickness before she did. She makes Henry a thank-you card.
- **Flags:** ⚠️ A portrait that "moves" is a classic spooky trope. The wink is a flash of
  light and a smile; the portrait is a friendly, goofy ancestor in a silly hat.

## 8. The Flying Lantern

**Spotlight:** Watson · **Lesson:** Teamwork · **Setting:** Boardwalk Trail, heather hills

- **Cold open:** A glowing light floats over the hills at dusk and bobs. "The Hound's eye!"
- **The case:** Watson thinks it looks familiar.
- **Clue 1:** It **moves with the wind**, not with a creature.
- **Clue 2:** A **long string** snagged on a post.
- **Clue 3:** A faint **flapping** sound, like paper.
- **Red herring:** Tavita swears it's a "flying hound". It's a bird, hanging out near the
  light.
- **The harmless truth:** Watson's own lantern kite for Camp Day got loose. He's too shy to
  say it's his.
- **Lesson:** Ask for help. The whole cabin works together to bring it home.
- **Moony:** Chases the string, then falls asleep tangled in it.
- **Flags:** None.

## 9. The Shadow on the Stones

**Spotlight:** Holmes, Anjali, Tavita · **Lesson:** Curiosity · **Setting:** the Stone Circle

- **Cold open:** At sunset, a giant shadow of a hound stretches across the Stone Circle.
- **The case:** Tavita sees it. Anjali tells it. Holmes investigates.
- **Clue 1:** The shadow **moves when Tavita moves**.
- **Clue 2:** It's **bigger near sunset** and smaller at noon.
- **Clue 3:** The shape matches **Anjali's hair, Tavita's hat and his telescope**, side by side.
- **Red herring:** A sheep near the stones.
- **The harmless truth:** The low sun turns three kids into one giant shadow-hound.
- **Lesson:** Look at things from another angle. The kids spend the rest of the golden hour
  making shadow puppets.
- **Flags:** ⚠️ Giant shadow at dusk. Golden light only; the shadow is silly (wagging ears,
  waving hat). The reveal comes within seconds.

## 10. The Trail That Went in Circles

**Spotlight:** Jasper, Beryl · **Lesson:** Honesty · **Setting:** Boardwalk Trail

- **Cold open:** Campers keep walking the Boardwalk and ending up where they started.
  "The Hound's leading us in circles!"
- **The case:** Jasper's Nature Club signs must be right. He's sure of it.
- **Clue 1:** The signs **point in different directions** each morning.
- **Clue 2:** **Hoofprints** around the signposts.
- **Clue 3:** A tuft of **white wool** on a post.
- **Red herring:** Moony's paw prints on a different path.
- **The harmless truth:** Duchess the sheep leans on the signposts and turns them. Jasper
  fixed them quickly and never told anyone he'd made a mistake.
- **Lesson:** Jasper admits he re-painted a sign wrongly. Honesty makes it easy to fix.
- **Flags:** None.

## 11. The Hound's Sore Paw

**Spotlight:** Zaid, Moony · **Lesson:** Kindness · **Setting:** Stone Circle, boot room

- **Cold open:** A sad little "woo-oo..." from the boot room. Moony is limping.
- **The case:** For the one time, the Hound really is involved. Zaid is worried; he's too
  shy to ask for help.
- **Clue 1:** A **soft whimper**, not a howl.
- **Clue 2:** A **burr** in the heather by his den.
- **Clue 3:** Moony **holds up one paw**.
- **Red herring:** Tavita thinks Moony has a bellyache. It's too many sandwiches.
- **The harmless truth:** A burr is stuck in his paw.
- **Lesson:** Asking for help is brave. The team removes the burr gently and gives Moony a
  bath (and a bow).
- **Flags:** ⚠️ An injured animal. The burr is small, the whimper is soft, and nothing hurts
  for long. Moony is safe and comforted within a minute.

## 12. The Boy Who Saw the Hound

**Spotlight:** Tavita · **Lesson:** Kindness · **Setting:** Tor Hill, dining hall

- **Cold open:** Tavita reports a Hound on Tor Hill again. No one listens ("Oh, Tavita...").
- **The case:** His feelings are hurt. He sits alone with his telescope.
- **Clue 1:** The telescope is **dented**, and the lens is smudged.
- **Clue 2:** A **reflection** in the dining hall window.
- **Clue 3:** A faint **"woo"** from the hill.
- **Red herring:** Another Tavita sighting: a rock.
- **The harmless truth:** This time Tavita was right: there *was* something on the hill. It's
  Moony. The team realises they never listen to him because he's usually wrong.
- **Lesson:** Listen kindly, even to someone who's often wrong. Everyone apologises, and
  Tavita is named "Official Lookout".
- **Moony:** The thing he saw, not a culprit.
- **Flags:** None.

## 13. Camp Day *(season finale)*

**Spotlight:** Zaid, Anjali, whole camp · **Lesson:** Teamwork · **Setting:** The Hall, campfire circle

- **Cold open:** It's Camp Day. Bunting, music and visiting kids from **Brambledown Camp**
  fill the lawn. A small, sad "woo..." drifts from somewhere. Tavita whispers, **"It's the
  Hound!"** Then, louder, "Oh no. Visitors."
- **The case:** A crayon note appears under Holmes's door: **"MOONY IS GONE. PLEASE HELP.
  DON'T TELL."** Holmes must find the writer *and* Moony, quietly, without the visitors
  noticing a giant dog.
- **Clue 1:** The note smells of **za'atar** and has a snagged **yellow thread** on it.
- **Clue 2:** A trail of **cheese crumbs** leads toward the Hall, then stops at the stage.
- **Clue 3:** A faint, wobbly **"woo"** and a **thumping wag** from under the stage.
- **Red herring:** Tavita's telescope "finds" Moony in the bunting. It's a very shaggy mop
  in a bucket.
- **The harmless truth:** Zaid wrote the note. The loud Camp Day music scared Moony, who
  hid under the stage, and Zaid was too shy to ask for help out loud.
- **Resolution:** Everyone works as a team. The twins turn the music down, Watson guides the
  visitors on a "Boardwalk tour", Anjali tells a long story to keep the crowd busy, and
  Henry and Zaid coax Moony out with cheese. The visitors leave happy; the camp keeps the
  secret.
- **Lesson:** Teamwork means protecting what matters, together.
- **Moony:** Gets a Camp Day bandana and a quiet standing ovation.
- **Flags:** Keep the mood joyful and warm. No stress about "being found": the tension is
  gentle, and the camp is clever, not frightened.

   but it is the most character-led episode.

   mitigation.

   spotlight if you'd prefer.
