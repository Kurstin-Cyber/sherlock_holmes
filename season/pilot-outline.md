# Episode 1 (Pilot): "The Hound Comes Home"

*Stage 3 beat outline. APPROVED. 22 minutes, in two 11-minute halves.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Full adaptation of Doyle's *The Hound of the Baskervilles* |
| **Lesson** | Kindness: sharing makes a discovery bigger, not smaller |
| **Spotlights** | Holmes, Henry, Jasper, Beryl, Zaid, Moony |
| **Settings** | The Hall, the campfire circle, the Boardwalk Trail, Tor Hill, the Stone Circle |

## One-line story

A new camper arrives at Camp Baskerville just as the Hound's legend seems to come true.
Holmes and Watson follow glowing paw prints, a silly howl and a missing boot, and find a
big, friendly, shy dog, and a Nature Club president who scared everyone off a butterfly
meadow.

## The mystery

**The harmless truth:** The "Hound" has been scaring campers away from the meadow behind the
Stone Circle. Jasper painted glowing paw prints on the trail and played a "howl" on his
harmonica from Tor Hill, to keep everyone away from a **Silverwing Skipper**, a rare
butterfly he found, because he wants to win the **Park Prize** (a shared prize for the best
discovery, awarded at Camp Day) all by himself. Meanwhile the real dog, Moony, has nothing
to do with any of it. He's a shy, lost stray who has been sharing Zaid's sandwiches, and
who took Henry's boot because it smelled like Dad's cooking.

### The three clues (each shown twice)

| # | Clue | Type | What it means | Simple logic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The **glowing paw prints** are all exactly the same, perfectly evenly spaced, with a faint smell of paint | Footprints and marks | They were stamped, not walked | "Real paws wobble. These don't." |
| 2 | The **howl** has a wobble at the end like a harmonica, and a **scrap of green netting** hangs on a bush at the top of Tor Hill | Sounds, object | A person made the howl, and carries a net | "Dogs don't carry nets. Who does?" (the Nature Club) |
| 3 | **Cheese and crust crumbs** lead to a den at the Stone Circle, beside **wobbly, muddy grey paw prints** (the grey of the Squelch) | Smells and crumbs, footprints | The real dog is a friend who is being fed, and he lives at the Stone Circle | "Crumbs are from a sandwich. Someone's feeding him." |

### The red herring

**Tavita "sees" the Hound on Tor Hill through his telescope.** A huge shaggy shape with
glowing eyes... it's Anjali, rehearsing the legend in the boot-room rug as a costume, with
two bike reflectors for eyes. Funny, cleared up on the spot, and forgotten by the reveal.

### The lesson

Jasper's discovery was a gift, not a prize to be hoarded. Once he shares the butterfly with
the whole camp, it becomes everybody's wonder. The Park Prize goes to the whole camp for
**"Best Discovery: a butterfly and a dog."**

## Part 1 (0:00 to 11:00): "The Legend"

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| **0:00-1:00** | **Cold open.** Golden dusk. Henry arrives at the Hall gates with a too-big backpack and a photo of Dad and Hana. A faraway "Woo-oo-oooo!" drifts over the heather. Tavita, on the East Terrace with his telescope, whispers, **"It's the Hound!"** Henry pulls his hat over his eyes. Tavita lowers the telescope: "Oh. Never mind. It's a bush." Title card. |
| **1:00-3:00** | **Campfire.** Anjali tells the legend as paper-cut puppets (the one-minute tale from the bible). Henry realises it's about *his* family. "Is the Baskerville the good kind?" Anjali: "...The snack-loving kind!" Laughter. |
| **3:00-5:00** | **Meet the team.** Watson narrates, opens the journal and welcomes Henry; he spots the homesickness before Holmes does. Holmes studies Henry's boots, hat and photo: "You're from far away. You miss someone. You have a baby sister." (Observation, kindly.) Henry's mouth falls open. Holmes: "Elementary... um, *Curious.*" She's delighted, then she misses that Henry's sad. Watson gently nudges her. The chant, first time: **"Look closer. Notice more. Think it through."** |
| **5:00-8:00** | **The case.** Next morning, Henry's left boot is gone from outside Bracken Cabin. On the Boardwalk Trail, glowing paw prints lead toward the Stone Circle meadow. Campers gather, whispering, nervous. Jasper arrives with his clipboard: "The meadow is closed. For your safety. Rules are rules!" Holmes crouches. **Clue #1** (Clue Alert, sparkle): the prints are identical and evenly spaced. Beryl twirls her plait and says nothing. |
| **8:00-10:00** | **Tor Hill.** Holmes, Watson and Henry climb the Boardwalk toward Tor Hill at golden sunset (Watson rolling alongside). **Red herring:** Tavita's shaggy "Hound", which turns out to be Anjali in the rug. Big laugh. |
| **10:00-11:00** | **Part 1 close.** At sunset the sparkly mist rolls in. From the Stone Circle comes a soft **"Woo-oo-oo!"** with a wobble. A huge shaggy silhouette stands among the stones. One floppy ear. A wagging tail. It lifts its nose and sneezes. Holmes, Watson and Henry look at each other. **"Curious... very curious!"** To be continued, gently. |

## Part 2 (11:00 to 22:00): "The Hound"

| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| **11:00-12:00** | **Recap.** Watson's journal replays Part 1 in four doodles (so each half works alone). Clue #1 replayed with a glowing outline. |
| **12:00-14:30** | **Tor Hill, golden hour.** The team follows the howl up the hill. **Clue #2:** the howl wobbles; a scrap of green netting hangs on a bush. Watson hums the wobble back; Henry says "That sounds like a harmonica!" Holmes: "A dog doesn't carry a net." The team decides to find out who carries a net. |
| **14:30-16:30** | **Kitchen detour.** The twins serve cheese toasties and share intelligence: "Someone's been taking cheese every night!" "Third stair!" "...always squeaks!" The squeak means a small person sneaks out at night. Minh feels a rhythm in the boards: a tiny pair of feet. Holmes trades sweet potato biscuits for the story. |
| **16:30-18:30** | **The Stone Circle.** At dusk, golden lanterns, fireflies, the team (and Beryl, who follows quietly) find a cosy den in the stones. **Clue #3:** crumbs and wobbly grey paw prints. There is Zaid in his big yellow raincoat, sitting beside the biggest, shaggiest dog anyone has ever seen. "He's not scary. He's shy." Moony noses out and drops **Henry's missing boot**, tied with a bow made of heather. He licks Henry's face. Henry laughs: "He smells like home." |
| **18:30-20:00** | **The reveal.** Holmes gathers the camp at the campfire and reads the three clues: the stamped prints, the harmonica howl and the net, the crumbs and the muddy paws. All point to Jasper. Beryl steps forward: **"Actually... that's not quite true,"** she says gently, correcting Jasper's story. Jasper confesses: the **Silverwing Skipper**, the Park Prize, "I only wanted to keep the butterfly safe." The camp is quiet; then Henry says, "Can I see it?" Jasper leads them all, gently, to the meadow. The butterfly shimmers. Everyone gasps in the golden light. |
| **20:00-20:30** | **The lesson.** Jasper: "It's better when other people see it." Anjali: "Now *that's* a story." The Park Prize is for the whole camp. Moony howls his silly **"Woo-oo-oo!"** and everyone joins in. |
| **20:30-22:00** | **"How Holmes Figured It Out."** Recap with glowing outlines: Clue 1, stamped prints. Clue 2, harmonica wobble and net. Clue 3, crumbs and muddy paws. Holmes with the chant. Final gag: Moony sits on Tavita's telescope. The camp agrees: **Moony is the secret mascot.** Watson's last journal line: "Case closed. Hound cleared." |

## Scare and complexity flags

| | Flag | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| ⚠️ | The first sight of Moony in silhouette (end of Part 1) | Always shown with one floppy ear, a wagging tail and a sneeze. Golden mist, never dark. Cut away to Holmes's grin. |
| ⚠️ | The howl at the cold open | Harmonica-like, with a wobbly end, over a sunny, golden dusk. Tavita's "Never mind" follows within ten seconds. |
| ⚠️ | Glowing paw prints | They look painted: bright, cartoony, neon-green, neatly spaced. They're funny, not eerie. |
| ⚠️ | Zaid alone at the Stone Circle den at dusk | He is with Moony and in a lantern-lit den; the camp already knows he's missing and Beryl is following. He is never lost or in danger. |
| ⚠️ | 22 minutes is long for ages 5 to 8 | Two-part structure with a built-in recap at the halfway point, so it can be watched in two sittings. |
| ⚠️ | Too many characters | The twins and Tavita have only one scene each. Anjali appears three times. |
| ⚠️ | Henry's homesickness | One tender beat with the photo, resolved with a hug and the boot. |
| ⚠️ | Jasper's confession | Private first, then the camp. No one laughs at him. |

## Production notes

- The legend is told as **paper-cut puppets** (a distinct art style).
- Watson's journal is 2D crayon doodles. Beryl's sketches are watercolour.
- The Clue Alert plays twice per clue.
- **Clue 3's wobbly prints** should look nothing like Jasper's stamped ones. That contrast
  is the whole clue.
- The "Never mind" gag with Tavita happens in the cold open and again in the red herring.
