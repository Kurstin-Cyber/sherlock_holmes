## Checks against the shot-language guide

- [x] Watson at eye level on the staircase, in the lift and in the attic; the lift ride is the
  accessible route, shown with delight, not as a problem to solve
- [x] The cold-open glow is silvery and pretty, and Tavita's "It's a lamp!" follows within 10 seconds
- [x] The attic is sunny and cosy in daylight; the night scene has lamp, lanterns and fairy lights
- [x] The teddy bear's head falls off into Tavita's hat; the gag is soft and silly
- [x] Every clue shows twice with matching framing (table above)
- [x] The chime is reserved for clues; the lift uses a plain bell "ding"
- [x] Moony appears only at the end, as a silhouette watching the moths, after the case is solved

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Padding used:** the timing report said ep. 3 had about 0:58 of room. I spent it on the lift
   ride (a star-painted cage lift, about 15 seconds), the sunlit attic establishing shots, and the
   moth-night scene.
2. **Clue 1 is shown in the journal first** (the doodle of the three clear moons), because the
   cabin scene is where the clue is discovered. The second doodle (the grey cloud) is the chime
   moment. This is a small exception to the usual "show the object in the world first"; it works
   because the clue is a pattern across nights, not an object.
3. **The lift** is shown as a charming feature, with a hand-painted starry sky inside. It appears
   again in later episodes; confirm its look.
4. **Jasper's note** needs a clearly readable close-up (the line "Don't forget to tell your
   friends"). In the animatic, hold it for 3 seconds.
5. **Panel count** is similar to episode 2; merging paired close-ups in the animatic will tighten it.
