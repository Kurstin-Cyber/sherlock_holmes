## Checks against the shot-language guide

- [x] The \"getting lost\" cold open is bright, short and silly (a gap-toothed daisy), with no one ever
  far from the Hall
- [x] Jasper's stiffness is awkward pride, not menace; Holmes's bluntness is also called out (Watson:
  \"That was very loud.\")
- [x] Beryl's honesty is gentle and private-first; Jasper apologises to her on camera
- [x] Every clue shows twice with matching framing (table above)

- [x] Watson is at eye level on the Boardwalk throughout; he passes bolts from his lap in the repair

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Padding used:** the timing report said ep. 10 had about 1:14 of room, mostly for the stakeout
   and the Duchess scratch. The sunrise stakeout is the episode's centrepiece (about 110 seconds);
   the mist, the dawn chorus and the tink of the bell set it up before the payoff.
2. **Duchess's scratch** is the comic centre. The signpost should wobble and spin in a way that
   reads clearly: three arrows rotating through a full turn, ending with TOR HILL pointing at the Hall.
3. **The yellow ribbon test** is the one thing children can copy at home; its \"before\" image is
   held for 3 seconds so it sticks.
