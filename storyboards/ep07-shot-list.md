## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Padding used:** the timing report said ep. 7 was about 1:45 short. I spent it on the three
   o'clock sun creep (about 25 seconds, now a full-length wait with a rising harp), the ferry-wave
   hold (9 seconds, no jokes), the pull-outs after each emotional beat, and the closing recap.
2. **The sun-creep shot** (scene 4) is the episode's main set-piece. The patch of light should move
   at a speed a child can follow across the floor, up the frame and onto the painted face.
3. **Moony and the painted dog** (scene 8): the button is the one "seed" in the season. Keep the
   dog's pose identical to the painting's: one ear up, one ear down, tongue out.
4. **The ferry sun-catcher** needs to read clearly as a boat from the gallery's far end: a flat
   silhouette with two sails and a funnel, with HANA in large lettering.
5. **Holmes's card** is the episode's only prop that carries text; hold it for 3 seconds.
