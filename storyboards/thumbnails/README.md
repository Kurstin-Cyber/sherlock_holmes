(SVG)** with PNG previews, in a simple storybook style. They show **staging, framing, colour and
light**, not finished animation art. Characters are simplified so each one's signature
outfit and silhouette is clear.

## Notes for the producer

- **Style:** flat colour, soft glows and rounded shapes, matching the "storybook" brief. Faces are
  deliberately simple (dot eyes, small smiles) so the poses and props carry the shot.
- **Characters** follow the approved profiles: Kurstin's cap with its flaps tied up and
  golden-blonde loc buns; Mateo's orange sticker-covered wheelchair; Henry's red plaid and
  star-stickered bobble hat; Anjali's plum cape and enormous curls; Jasper's net and clipboard;
  Beryl's red plait; the twins' aprons and Minh's orange hearing aids; Zaid's yellow raincoat and
  Tut; Tavita's sun hat and telescope; Moony's mismatched ears and shimmering collar.
- **Moony's silhouette** follows the guide: a floppy ear, an upright ear and a wagging tail,
  backlit gold, with a sneeze.
- **The panel images are not a final art direction.** They show what a frame should contain, not
  how the final 3D look will render.

## Rebuilding

Each image is produced from a small drawing script. If the cast's looks change, the images need
redrawing; ask and I will regenerate them.
