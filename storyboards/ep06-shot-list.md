## Checks against the shot-language guide

- [x] Smells are shown as coloured ribbons with icons (lilac with paint pots and pencils), so every
  viewer "sees" the clue
- [x] Night scenes (the campfire bench and the lawn) have lanterns and a big moon; the Craft Barn has
  fairy lights and a warm lamp
- [x] Anjali's vulnerable scene is held in close-ups at eye level; Watson offers the cushion from his lap
- [x] Beryl's "Actually..." is planted in the cold open for first-time viewers (E06-05) and flashes
  back for one second at Clue 3
- [x] Every clue shows twice with matching framing (table above)
- [x] The chime is reserved for clues; soft harp notes mark the lilac ribbon and the painted girl in
  the cape
- [x] Moony appears only with a sock (the red herring) and in the closing button; he is never a
  suspect for long

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Timing:** the report said ep. 6 had no slack and needed 10 s trimmed from the cold open. I
   trimmed the cold open to 55 s and used the freed time, plus a few seconds per scene, for the
   emotional beats in the Craft Barn (the paintings pan, Anjali's confession).
