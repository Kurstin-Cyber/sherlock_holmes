## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Padding:** the timing report said ep. 4 was already dense after 8:00 and needed no padding, so
   no new material was added; the plan and the nest-box montage are tightened to fit.
2. **The whole-team chant** (scene 5): the golden frame splits into six small rims. Approved
   in the guide; this is its first use.
3. **Nest of boots** (scene 6): the key image of the episode. It needs a clear silhouette so that
   five different-coloured boots read from a wide shot.
