# Timing Report

*Stage 5 · Draft 1 · Estimates only*

## Method

A quick read-aloud estimate of every approved script. For each script I counted:

| Item | Allowance |
|---|---|
| Dialogue | 2.2 words per second (a gentle children's pace), plus 0.7 seconds between lines |
| Action paragraph | 3 seconds each |
| Clue Alert, chant and journal cues | 2.5 seconds each |

This is **not a real timing**. It ignores music, held shots and the title card, and it
under-counts montages (several jobs written as one block). Expect the true running time to be
within about **15 percent** of these figures. The real timings will come from the animatic
with voices.

## What this tells us

- **Most episodes fit.** Eleven of the twelve 11-minute episodes estimate between 8:16 and
  11:03. Once the animatic adds the title card (about 10 seconds), the clue holds (each clue
  is shown twice with a one-second breathing hold, about 30 seconds a season episode) and
  music-only beats, the 9:30 to 10:30 scripts will land close to 11:00 without cuts.
- **The three short ones (eps. 7, 8, 9)** need *more* material, not cuts. The best uses of the
  extra time are the emotional peaks: the three o'clock sun creeping up the frame (ep. 7), the
  untangling of the kite and the quiet high point (ep. 8), and the shadow play (ep. 9).
- **Ep. 6 has no slack.** Trim about 10 seconds from the cold open's setup.
- **Ep. 13 will run long** because the team montage is under-counted. Cut the Park Prize scene
  first if it does.
