## Checks against the shot-language guide

- [x] The animal-in-distress cold open is bright and warm: morning sun, a cosy basket, a small whimper
  and a thumping tail; the friends arrive within seconds
- [x] No growl, snap, flinch or blood: Moony is trusting, gentle and brave, with a small burr that
  rolls out in one soft motion
- [x] Zaid's distress is short and tender; he is comforted at once; the hug is held with no joke
- [x] Holmes washes her hands and uses clean fingertips (no tools near a pet's paw)
- [x] Every clue shows twice with matching framing (table above)
- [x] The chime is reserved for clues; the whimper and the burr are shown as ripples and an object
- [x] Watson is at eye level in every scene; he times the removal from the basket's edge
- [x] The sock bandage is a cuddly gag; the closing button is slow and gag-free

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Timing:** the report said ep. 11's bath scene could flex. I kept it at about 70 seconds to give
   the relief after the burr its full length, and held the hug in scene 4 for 12 seconds.
2. **The burr removal** (scene 7) is the only hands-on moment. Keep it close, calm and quick: no tools,
   a washed hand, Zaid holding Moony's head, Anjali's story as a lullaby.
3. **Moony's whimper** should be visibly different from his happy \"woo\": small, wobbly and pale
   blue against the big golden notes.
4. **Panel count** is on par with earlier episodes.
