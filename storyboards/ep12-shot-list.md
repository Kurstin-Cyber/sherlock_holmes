## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Timing:** the report said the sunset stakeout and Moony's arrival want long holds. The stakeout
   is about 115 seconds, with an 8-second wait, an 8-second arrival, an 8-second sit and an 8-second
   pull-out, plus a three-second silence before the harmonica note.
2. **The \"I see it\"** whisper (scene 7) is the emotional peak; the shot is a low-angle medium of
   Tavita with the sunset behind him. Keep the camera still.
3. **\"Aua le popole\"** appears twice (Tavita's scene and Watson's line near the end). It still needs
