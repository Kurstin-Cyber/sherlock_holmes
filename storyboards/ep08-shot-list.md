## Checks against the shot-language guide

- [x] Watson is at eye level on the Boardwalk, at the tangle and during the quiet high point; the
  Boardwalk is his route, and the camera sits at his eye line when Holmes sits down beside him
- [x] The glowing light is warm and round (a bobbing \"moon with a hiccup\"), never an eerie shape
- [x] The owl is plump, fluffy and silent, with a single drifting feather; played as comedy
- [x] Every clue shows twice with matching framing (table above)
- [x] The chime is reserved for clues; Watson's bell is his own voice (once nervous, twice happy, three
  dings solved)
- [x] Watson's \"Never mind\" is nervous, not a joke; the episode keeps Tavita's \"Never mind\" as the joke
- [x] The quiet high point (scene 8) is held for 12 seconds with five seconds of no dialogue

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Padding used:** the timing report said ep. 8 was about 2:10 short. I put most of the extra time
   into the tangle and the quiet high point (about 20 seconds), the establishing and pull-out shots
   between scenes, and the closing recap.
2. **The kite** needs a clear, memorable shape: a diamond of warm paper with a long tail and a ring
   of fairy lights inside. It must read from far away and as a close-up.
3. **Watson's confession** (scene 7) is one long close-up speech (about 20 seconds in total). In the
   animatic, keep the camera tight and still on him; no cutaways to the others until he finishes.
4. **Jasper's faint harmonica** in the final hold is a wink for pilot viewers; it is quiet and can
   be cut.
5. **Clue 1's shot** pairs the flag and the glow in a single frame, so the link is visible without
   explanation.
