## Checks against the shot-language guide

  cabin; lanterns, moonlight and night-lights on the landing; lanterns and moonlight on the terrace)

## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Padding used:** the timing report said ep. 5 needed longer, quieter holds on its night scenes.
   I added the holds to the landing (8 s), the terrace (8 s), the boot room (4 s) and the closing
   button (4 s), which also gives Henry's ferry-horn moment room to breathe.
