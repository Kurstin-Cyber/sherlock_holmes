## Notes and questions for the producer

1. **Timing:** the report said this episode's montage is under-counted and that the Park Prize scene
   should go first. I cut the Park Prize to about 20 seconds (one panel of Jasper holding the
   golden acorn, one of Moony in the window) and gave the montage about 100 seconds.
2. **The montage** (scene 7) should be shot as a heist-film parody for five-year-olds: cross-cuts,
   thumbs-up, secret signals. Each job is 3 to 5 seconds and uses a character's trait.
3. **Jasper's harmonica cover** (scene 8) pays off the pilot's fake howl; keep it visual (Moony
   woos, the harmonica plays, the crowd cheers).
4. **The last shot** (scene 11) is held for about 7 seconds: the series' final image.
5. **Panel count** is the highest of the season because the montage is cut quickly. In the animatic
   expect it to loosen where the music carries it.
